# Textures

Photos of materials from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/textures), license **CC0** (public domain), downloaded in
1K JPG (color, OpenGL normal, and roughness) on 2026-11-08. Each folder is named after the original asset
(`https://polyhaven.com/a/<folder>`). The mapping to ChaléCAD materials lives in
`apps/web/lib/three/photoTextures.ts`.
