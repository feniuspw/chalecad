# F7-E1: Engine — estimated electrical and plumbing installations

**Goal:** with `project.installations.enabled`, the engine places the electrical and plumbing points based on the rooms and furniture, builds the circuits, sizes the water tank, and costs cables, conduits, pipes, and fittings in the `installations` stage. The reference is the usual minimums of NBR 5410 and NBR 5626. It is a planning estimate, not an electrical design.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `Installations` (`project.installations`: `enabled`, `waterHeater`, `occupants?`, `panel?`) and `Stage` with `installations`, in `schema/project.ts`.
- `InstallationPoint`, `Circuit`, `InstallationsReport`, and `Results.installations` in `schema/results.ts`. Today `compute.ts` returns `installations: null`.
- Catalog:
  - `constants.installations` (NBR 5410/5626, cable table, TUE (dedicated-use outlet) power ratings, factors);
  - materials `pvc_sewer_40`, `ppr_hot_25`, `wire_*`, `conduit_25`, `box_4x2`, `outlet_2pt`, `outlet_20a`, `switch_simple`, `light_fixture`, `breaker`, `breaker_dr`, `panel_box`, `water_valve`, `pipe_fittings`, `siphon_box`, `water_tank_<L>`, `gas_heater`, and `solar_heater`;
  - `installations` labor = 90–120% of the material.

## 1. Points (`installations/points.ts`)
For each resolved room (`model.rooms` with `polygon`):
- **Lighting:** 1 `light` point at the centroid, on the ceiling (`height` = average clear height). In a room > 16 m², 2 or more points (one per ~12 m²), spread out.
  - Power = 100 VA for the first 6 m² + 60 VA for every whole 4 m² (constants).
- **Switch:** 1 `switch` at 1.10 m, on the wall beside the room's first door, 15 cm from the door frame, on the inside. With no door, on the longest edge that has a wall.
- **General-use outlets (`outlet`, 0.30 m):**
  - 1 per `outletPerimeter` m of perimeter (round up), distributed along the edges with a wall, away from the doors.
  - Wet rooms (kitchen, laundry): one per `wetOutletPerimeter`. The first `wetOutletHighCount` are at 1.10 m (`outlet_high`, above the counter) and are rated `wetOutletVA`; the rest are rated `outletVA`.
  - Bathroom: 1 `outlet_high` next to the basin (near the `furn_bath_sink` furniture, if any).
  - Room ≤ 6 m²: at least 1.
  - Circulation and storage: 1 if the area > 2.25 m².
- **TUE:** one `tue` point per piece of furniture whose item is in `tuePower`, next to the furniture.
  - Electric shower: with `waterHeater === 'electric_shower'`, one `tue` point at each `furn_shower` (2.10 m), with power `tuePower.electric_shower`.
- **Water and sewer (per furniture with `water`):**
  - `water_cold` on all.
  - `water_hot` on the shower, basin, and sink, when `waterHeater` is not electric.
  - `sewer` on all: 40 mm for sink, basin, tub, washing machine, and shower; 100 mm for the toilet.
  - `drain` (siphon box) in each bathroom and laundry.
  - Position: on the wall behind the furniture (the furniture's back, rotation), usual heights (water 0.60 m; shower 2.10 m; sewer 0).
- **Panel (`panel`):** at `installations.panel` or, without it, on the interior wall closest to the entry door (first door in `ext-front`), at 1.50 m.
- **Water tank (`tank`):**
  - Volume = occupants × `waterPerPerson` × `reserveDays`, rounded up to the next `tankSizes`.
  - Occupants = `occupants` or `personsPerBedroom` × number of bedrooms, minimum 2.
  - Position: above the bathroom closest to the center (in the ceiling/mezzanine). Visual only; it does not change the structure.
  - Emit `install.tank_load` (info) with the full weight, for the engineer to check the support.
- No rooms marked: `installations` = empty report (no points) and an `install.no_rooms` warning (info): "Marque os cômodos para estimar as instalações".

## 2. Circuits (`installations/circuits.ts`)
- **Lighting:** one circuit per `circuitMaxVA` (group neighboring rooms).
- **General outlets:** same.
- **Kitchen and laundry:** their own circuits (`kitchen`).
- **TUE:** one circuit per appliance above 1,200 W; the smaller ones are joined with the room's outlets.
- **Sizing:**
  - Current = power / voltage.
  - Take the first row of `wireTable` with max current ≥ current. Minimums of 1.5 mm² for lighting and 2.5 mm² for outlets.
- **Cable length:** by plan-view distance from the panel to each point (Manhattan) × `routingFactor`, + `dropPerPoint` per point + level height (points on the mezzanine), × 3 conductors (phase, neutral, ground). Lighting uses 3 conductors and a return.
- **Conduit:** sum of the distances (1 conductor of path) × `routingFactor` + drops.
- **Demand** = Σ power × `demandFactor` / 1000.
- **Warnings:**
  - `install.demand` (info) if demand exceeds 12 kW: "Pode exigir entrada trifásica; confirme com a concessionária" (utility (concessionária)).
  - `install.shower_wire` when the shower circuit requires 6 mm² or more (only reports the gauge).

## 3. Pipes (`installations/pipes.ts`)
- **Cold water:** from the entry point (`project.utilities` kind `water`, or the closest point on the outline) to the water tank, and from the tank to each `water_cold` point. Length = (Manhattan × `routingFactor`) + elevation changes.
- **Hot water:** from the heater (next to the bathroom closest to the tank) to the `water_hot` points.
- **Sewer:** 40 mm from the points to the siphon box/room branch and 100 mm from the toilet and the branches to the outlet (`utilities` kind `sewer`, or the closest radier (raft slab) edge to the bathroom).
- The current `utilities` item in `quantities/items.ts`, which is in the foundation, stays the same. Do not count twice: the new pipes start at the radier outline.

## 4. Quantities (`quantities/items.ts` or `installations/quantities.ts`)
With `enabled`, in the `installations` stage, `hostId` = room id or `installations`:
- **Cables:** per gauge (`wire_<section>`, with 1.5 → `wire_1_5` and 2.5 → `wire_2_5`).
- **Infrastructure:** `conduit_25`; `box_4x2` (one per outlet and switch, and one per wall light point).
- **Electrical parts:** `outlet_2pt` (TUG, general-use outlet), `outlet_20a` (high kitchen outlet and TUE), `switch_simple`, `light_fixture`; `breaker` (one per circuit + main), `breaker_dr` (1, or 2 with more than 8 circuits), `panel_box`.
- **Plumbing:** `pvc_water_25`, `ppr_hot_25`, `pvc_sewer_40`, and `pvc_sewer_100`; `water_valve` (one per bathroom, kitchen, and laundry, + 1 main); `pipe_fittings` (one kit per water/sewer point); `siphon_box`.
- **Equipment:** water tank (`water_tank_<L>`) and `gas_heater` or `solar_heater`, depending on `waterHeater`.
- **Weight:** outside the structural weight (`inWeight: false`), except the full tank, which only goes into the warning.
- Without `enabled`: nothing changes, and the snapshot stays the same.

## Acceptance tests
- 3 × 3 bedroom (perimeter 12 m): 3 outlets, 1 light point of 100 VA (9 m² → 100 VA, no step), 1 switch beside the door.
- 4 × 5 living room (20 m²): light of 100 + 60 × 3 = 280 VA; 2 points (> 16 m²); outlets = ⌈18/5⌉ = 4.
- 2.5 × 3 kitchen (perimeter 11 m): ⌈11/3.5⌉ = 4 outlets, 3 high ones of 600 VA and 1 of 100 VA. Own circuit.
- Bathroom with a 5,500 W electric shower at 220 V (25 A): circuit with 4 mm² and a 25 A breaker per the table.
  - With `gas`: no TUE, with `water_hot` at the shower and basin.
- Water tank: 2 bedrooms → 4 people × 150 × 2 = 1,200 L → 1,500 L. With `occupants: 2` → 600 → 1,000 L.
- Toilet → 100 mm sewer; sink → 40 mm.
- No rooms: empty report and `install.no_rooms` warning.
- Budget:
  - with `enabled`, the `installations` stage appears with labor at 90% of the material (minimum value);
  - without `enabled`, the snapshot stays unchanged.
- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/engine typecheck test lint` green.
- In the return: the simplifications made and what was left out.
