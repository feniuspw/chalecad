# F14-E1: Engine — interior walls cut by the roof

**Problem:** on sloped roofs (A-frame, gable, gambrel), interior walls get the full story height (or the next level) and a rectangular outline `rect(len, height)` (`packages/engine/src/model/index.ts`, the "Paredes do usuário" (User walls) loop). A partition near the A-frame's roof plane, or on the mezzanine, goes through the roof in 3D. The Presentation shows this badly.

**May change:** `packages/engine/src/model/index.ts` (and a new helper in `packages/engine/src/model/`), plus tests in `packages/engine/test/**`. Snapshot `test/snapshot/constants-move.test.ts` only if it changes on purpose (explain).

**Do not change:** schemas (`packages/engine/src/schema/**`, `packages/catalog/src/schema.ts`), `apps/**`.

## What to do
1. For each user wall, the top of the `outline` (u coordinates along the wall, v upward from `baseZ`) becomes `min(currentHeight, roofLowerZ(u) − baseZ)`.
   - `roofLowerZ` comes from the roof planes already computed (there is `roofBelowAt(planes, [x,y])` in `model/rooms.ts` and a local `roofZ`).
   - Use the underside of the roof (the axis plane minus the thickness of the roofing assembly, if easy to obtain). Otherwise, use the axis plane minus 0.05 m.
   - Outside any plane (no roof above): the current height stays.
2. The outline becomes a polygon:
   - sample along u (at the ends, every ≤ 0.25 m, and where the wall crosses the ridge line or the projection of a plane edge);
   - remove collinear points. The typical shape is a trapezoid or a "gable-end pentagon".
   - Minimum height of 0.3 m (walls under a very low roof plane remain as a knee wall).
3. Walls with an explicit `height` follow the same rule (the min already handles it).
4. Openings: if a door or window extends past the cut top, it is kept, but add an existing or new warning (`code: 'wall.roof_clip'`, severity `warn`) saying that door X does not fit under the roof. Look for how warnings are built (`warnings/`). If you need a new code, use a free string, since `EngineWarning.code` is a string.
5. Quantities: the wall area and studs use the outline; check that `generatePieces`/quantities accept a polygonal outline (exterior gable-end walls are already polygons). If they do not, adapt.

## Tests
- A-frame 6 × 9 (ridge 6, knee 0.9) with a partition parallel to the ridge at 0.8 m from the side: the top stays below the roof along the full length.
- Partition perpendicular to the ridge crossing the width: the outline is a pentagon, with the peak at the ridge and low ends.
- A box with a flat slab: nothing changes (rectangular outline equal to before).
- Wall on the A-frame mezzanine: cut.
- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/engine test` and `pnpm typecheck` green.
