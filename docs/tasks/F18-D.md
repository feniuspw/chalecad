# F18-D: Deck aligned to the chalet, structure, piers on sloped terrain, and stairs

User complaint: the deck "is buggy, not aligned and nice with the chalet." They also want the program to already suggest the pilotis (piers)/supports on sloped terrain, following the standards.

## Diagnosis

1. The deck is built over the `typology.width/depth` rectangle on the wall **axis** (`facade()` in `packages/engine/src/extras/geometry.ts`). That is why it goes 6–8 cm into the wall and does not touch the corners.
2. It ignores `model.footprint`. On an L-shaped plan (`combined`) and on the A-frame with annexes, the deck falls over the wing or over the annex.
3. The elevation was a loose number. The +0.30 m default left the deck 30 cm **above** the house floor and above the door thresholds. The slab also clashed with the edge of the radier (raft slab).
4. It floats: there is no joist, beam, post, or foundation. The posts only exist on the covered deck and rise from the floor to the roof.
5. The guardrail closes the three free edges, including in front of the door, and there are no stairs. The deck roof comes out black, flat, and crosses the A-frame and the gable end.
6. The boards follow the world X axis, so side decks end up with perpendicular boards.
7. The deck does not appear in plan or section (`apps/web/lib/plan2d`).

See it in the app: `/editor?new=1&template=gable-loft`, Front view. Also `aframe-wings` and `l-house`.

## Contracts (already on main, do not change schemas)

**`DeckExtra`** (`packages/engine/src/schema/project.ts`): new fields `facadeIndex`, `align` (`start|center|end`), `offset` (now accepts a negative value), `level` (top of the floor relative to the house's finished floor), `guardrail` (`true|false|'auto'`), `stairs` (`'auto'|DeckStair[]`), `support`, `footing`, and `boards`. The `elevation` field became legacy and is ignored.

**Elevation:** `deckTopZ(e, x)` already exists in `geometry.ts`: `level` or `-catalog.extras.deck.stepBelowFloor`.

**`ExtraModel.deck?: DeckFrameModel`** (`packages/engine/src/schema/results.ts`) carries:
- `facade`, `topZ`, `boardThickness`, and `boardDir`;
- `joists`, `beams`, and `ledger`;
- `supports`, each with `groundZ`, `material`, `size`, `footing`, and `braced`;
- `stairs` (`DeckStairModel`), `maxDrop`, `guardrail`, `railGaps`, and `checks`.

**Catalog:** `catalog.constants.extras.deck` (board, joist, beam, ledger, posts, bracing, foundation, `guardrailDrop`, stairs/Blondel, loads). `guardrailHeight` is now 1.10 m (NBR 14718).

**Standards in the catalog:** `nbr7190` (wood), `nbr6120`, `nbr6122`, `nbr14718`, `nbr9050`.

**Terrain:** `packages/engine/src/terrain/surface.ts` exports `SiteSurface`, with `groundZ(x,y)` and `naturalZ(x,y)` in house coordinates, and `flatSurface()`. Card F18-T will swap the surface for one with pads and walls, keeping the same interface.

**Hook:** `packages/engine/src/extras/deckFrame.ts` exports `attachDeckFrames(project, model, surface, catalog)`, empty today. It is already called in `compute.ts` right after the terrain.

## Deliver

### Engine
1. **Facade by the outer face** (`facade`/`deckGeometry`). Use the edges of `model.footprint` whose outward normal points to `side`; order from the outermost to the innermost (tie-break by length); `facadeIndex` chooses the edge and a nonexistent index falls back to 0. The origin is the corner of the outer face, so the "whole facade" goes from corner to corner. Apply `align` with `offset` and attach the deck to the edge. Typologies without a rectangular footprint (L, annexes, A-frame) must work.
2. **Elevation:** use `deckTopZ` everywhere. No slab overlaps the radier: the deck starts at the radier face (`foundation.border`) or sits below its top.
3. **Structure** in `attachDeckFrames`. May also create `extras/deckFrame*.ts`.
   - Boards in the `boardDir`, by the `boards` field.
   - Joists perpendicular to the boards, every `joist.spacing`.
   - Beams under the joists: at the outer edge, plus the intermediate ones needed to respect `joist.maxSpan`.
   - `ledger` at the house when the deck abuts the facade. Without a ledger, a line of posts next to the house.
   - Posts under the beams at each span of at most `beam.maxSpan`, aligned to the corners.
   - **Height of each post:** `topZ - board - joist - beam - surface.groundZ(x,y)`, computed per post (varies on sloped terrain).
   - **`auto` material:** wood up to `support.woodMaxHeight`, steel up to `steelMaxHeight`, and concrete above.
   - **`auto` foundation:** bored pile if the slope exceeds `footing.boredSlopePct`, otherwise spread footing.
   - `braced` when the height exceeds `braceHeight`.
   - A post of height ≤ 0 becomes a direct support (block/shallow footing, height 0) and does not disappear.
4. **Guardrail:**
   - `maxDrop` = largest `topZ - groundZ` sampled on the free edges.
   - With `guardrail: 'auto'`, the guardrail exists only if `maxDrop > guardrailDrop`. With `true`, always; with `false`, never.
   - The `rail` segments in `segments` follow this rule and are interrupted at the stairs (`railGaps`).
5. **Stairs:**
   - **`auto`:** if `maxDrop > stair.singleStepMax`, create a staircase on the outer edge. Center it in front of the door/sliding door of the facade that falls inside the deck (opening with a door `type` on the facade host); without a door, at the center.
   - Risers: `n = ceil(drop / riserMax)`, with `riserHeight = drop / n` and a tread that satisfies Blondel (`blondelMin ≤ 2e + p ≤ blondelMax`, `p ≥ treadMin`).
   - Use `groundZ` at the foot of the stairs; refine once, because the foot changes with the length.
   - Manual stairs follow `DeckStair`.
6. **Deck roof:**
   - Do not cross the main roof in any typology. Test the A-frame (aframe-wings), the combined with a wing in y, the shed, and the gable end (margin for purlins).
   - The roof posts start at the top of the floor, on the structure's posts when present; align them with each other.
7. **Checks in `checks`**, with `norm` = catalog id:
   - joist span ≤ `joist.maxSpan` (nbr7190);
   - beam span ≤ `beam.maxSpan` (nbr7190);
   - post height and bracing (nbr7190);
   - guardrail required and present, at 1.10 m (nbr14718);
   - stairs: riser, tread, and Blondel (nbr9050);
   - foundation: approximate soil stress, with influence area × (`liveLoadKPa` + `deadLoadKPa`) / footing area against `project.design.soilBearingKPa` (nbr6122).

   Catalog standards with `confirmed:false` follow the approval golden rule: `ok` becomes a pending item. Here the status only needs to be coherent; the approval screen already handles it.
8. **Warnings:** `deck.*` in `packages/engine/src/warnings/`. Examples: tall post without bracing, guardrail required and turned off, stairs outside Blondel, deck over embankment/wall.
9. **Quantities** (`extras/index.ts`, materials and prices in `packages/catalog/src/data/materials.ts` and `pricebook-*.ts`).
   - Replace the `deck_frame` by m² with the real structure: treated wood in m³ (joists, beams, and ledger by section × length), posts by material, foundation concrete, and screws/anchor bolts.
   - Guardrail by real length (without the openings) and stairs (treads and stringers).
   - Keep `deck_wood` in m².
10. **Approval:** one verification item per deck, or aggregate in `packages/engine/src/approval/compliance.ts` from `ExtraModel.deck.checks`.

### Web
1. **`apps/web/components/scene/ExtrasView.tsx`:**
   - real boards, or a texture with UV in the `boardDir`;
   - edge/fascia board;
   - joists and beams visible from below;
   - posts from the ground to the beam, with the footing/bored pile visible above the ground;
   - knee brace;
   - stairs with steps and guardrail with posts and handrail.
   - The deck roof uses the main roof's material and color (`project.roof.cladding`, same scene material), with an edge beam.
   - Respect the Structure/Exploded mode like the other extras.
2. **`ExtraInspector.tsx` (deck):**
   - side and facade (`facadeIndex`, when there is more than one);
   - alignment with buttons Start/Center/End/"Centralizar na porta" (Center on the door) and offset;
   - length ("fachada inteira" (whole facade)), depth, and floor level (hint: "em relação ao piso da casa" (relative to the house floor); button "Nivelar com a casa" (Level with the house));
   - covered;
   - guardrail Auto/Yes/No, showing the drop;
   - stairs: Auto or editable list;
   - posts and foundation (Auto shows the choice);
   - boards.
   - Show a summary with the number of posts, the maximum height, and the `checks` with status.
3. **`apps/web/lib/project/extras.ts`:** defaults of the new deck. `sideLength` now comes from the real face (use the engine helpers exported by `@chalecad/engine`).
4. **2D** (`apps/web/lib/plan2d/plan.ts`, `section.ts`): deck outline, boards in light hatching, posts and stairs in plan; deck and posts in section. Touch `section.ts` only in the extras/deck part: F18-T touches the terrain in the same file.
5. **Presets:** check whether `gable-loft`, `aframe-wings`, `aframe-compact`, `l-house`, `gambrel-cabin`, and the wizard look good. Adjust the deck values in the presets if needed (`apps/web/lib/project/presets/*.ts`, `wizard/generate.ts` only the deck part).

## Minimum tests
- Front deck on the whole facade: starts at the outer face and goes from corner to corner (x from −h to W+h); top at −0.03.
- L-shaped plan and A-frame with annexes: the deck does not intersect the footprint (intersection area ≈ 0).
- Sloped terrain 30% descending toward the front, front deck 3 m deep:
  - the outer-edge posts are taller than the inner ones;
  - `groundZ` matches the `surface`;
  - `auto` stairs exist, with the riser between `riserMin` and `riserMax` and Blondel ok;
  - `auto` guardrail present.
- Flat deck at level −0.03: no guardrail (`auto`), no stairs, short supports.
- Deck roof without intersection with the main roof planes on gable, A-frame (aframe-wings), shed, and combined.
- Quantities: wood m³ = Σ section × length, and one foundation per post.
- Web: `ExtrasView`/inspector render without NaN; test of the alignment helper; 2D plan with the deck.

When done: `pnpm typecheck`, engine and web tests green, one commit `F18-D: …` on branch `card/F18-D`.
