# F12-W2: App — usability for non-experts (glossary, explained alerts, property search, contextual help)

**Goal:** someone outside the field understands what they see without leaving the app. Technical terms have a hover explanation, each alert has a "what this means and what to do" in plain language, and the properties have search.

**May change:**
- `apps/web/lib/glossary/**` (new, pure and tested).
- `apps/web/components/glossary/**` (new).
- `apps/web/components/results/AlertsTab.tsx`, `StructureTab.tsx`, `SummaryTab.tsx`, `Kpis.tsx`, and `shared.tsx`.
- `apps/web/components/editor/properties/PropertiesPanel.tsx`, `fields.tsx`, and `SystemSections.tsx`, only to apply the terms and the search.
- Tests alongside.

**Do not change:** `packages/**`, `lib/store/**`, `package.json`, `components/scene/**`, and `lib/three/**`, nor the F11-W1 files in parallel: `components/wizard/**`, `lib/project/wizard/**`, `NewProjectDialog.tsx`, `components/projects/**`, `ExtrasSection.tsx`, `lib/project/extras.ts`, and `lib/plan2d/**`.

## 1. Glossary (`lib/glossary/terms.ts`)
- 40 to 60 terms in layperson pt-BR, with a short sentence and, when it makes sense, a "why it matters". Examples: LSF (steel frame), wood frame, radier (raft slab), stud (montante), track (guia), header (verga), rafter (caibro), purlin (terça), ridge (cumeeira), mezzanine, gable end (empena), eave (beiral), OSB, cement board (placa cimentícia), drywall, PET wool (lã de PET), sandwich roof panel (telha sanduíche), bracing (contraventamento), deflection (flecha), utilization ratio (taxa de uso), kN, ceiling height (pé-direito), guardrail (guarda-corpo), ART (Anotação de Responsabilidade Técnica, the engineer's liability record), NBR, bioclimatic zone, transmittance (U), thermal capacity (CT), absorptance, kWp, TUE, circuit, breaker (disjuntor), RCD (DR), siphoned drain box (caixa sifonada), water column (coluna d'água), A-frame, gambrel, deck, building pad (platô), slope (talude), cut and fill, contingency. (Keep the Portuguese term as the glossary entry key/label, since the UI is in pt-BR.)
- `findTerms(text)` finds the terms in a text (without repeating and without matching inside words), to highlight automatically.

## 2. `<Term>` and `<GlossaryText>` components
- `<Term id="radier">radier</Term>`: dotted underline and a tooltip with the definition (shadcn Tooltip, focus-accessible).
- `<GlossaryText>{text}</GlossaryText>`: applies `findTerms` and wraps only the first occurrence of each term.
- Apply it to:
  - section names and main labels of the Properties (Sistema construtivo (Construction system), Fundação (radier) (Foundation), Mezanino (Mezzanine), Beiral (Eave)…);
  - KPIs (Aço kg/m² (Steel kg/m²), Madeira m³/m² (Wood m³/m²));
  - titles of the structural checks;
  - alert messages.

## 3. Explained alerts (`lib/glossary/alerts.ts`)
- For the most common alert codes, a "O que quer dizer" (What it means) text and an "O que fazer" (What to do) text, in plain language. The codes are scattered across the engine; read `packages/engine/src/warnings/**`, `installations/**`, `performance/**`, and `structure/**`, read-only.
  - Cover at least: `norm.*` (ceiling height, stairs, guardrail, glass, A-frame thrust), `room.*`, `furniture.*`, `install.*`, `thermal.*`, `wood.*`, `steel.index`, `system.profile_mismatch`, and the failed checks.
- In the "Alertas" (Alerts) tab, each alert gets an "Entender" (Understand) that expands this text, and a "Mostrar no 3D" (Show in 3D) button when there are `refs` (use the highlight/selection that already exists).
- In the "Estrutura" (Structure) tab, a layperson text for each failed `kind` ("A viga está curta para esse vão: o piso de cima pode balançar ou ceder" (The beam is too short for this span: the floor above may sway or sag)).

## 4. Property search (`PropertiesPanel.tsx`)
- Field "Buscar ajuste (ex.: beiral, espaçamento, cor da telha)" (Search setting (e.g., eave, spacing, roof panel color)) at the top of the project panel.
- When typing, only the sections whose title or inner labels match remain (accent-insensitive, case-insensitive). The section that matched opens by itself.
- The simplest way: a static index of section → keywords (`lib/glossary/settingsIndex.ts`), tested.
- `Esc` clears.
- Shortcut `/` focuses the search (outside text fields).

## 5. Layperson summary (`SummaryTab.tsx`)
- At the top, a card "Seu chalé em 4 frases" (Your chalet in 4 sentences), generated purely from `results`:
  - area and rooms;
  - cost and what weighs most;
  - whether the structure passed or what to review;
  - a suggested next step ("Marque os cômodos para estimar instalações" (Mark the rooms to estimate installations), "Revise 2 pontos com o engenheiro" (Review 2 points with the engineer)…).
- Tested `plainSummary(project, results)` function.

## Acceptance tests
- `findTerms`: finds, does not repeat, and does not match inside a word ("radiere" does not match).
- Alerts: every covered code has both sentences; a test lists the codes emitted by the engine fixtures and checks coverage of at least 80%.
- `settingsIndex`: "beiral" finds Cobertura (Roofing); "espaçamento" finds Sistema (System).
- `plainSummary` with and without rooms.
- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/web typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, and `build` green (run separately).
- In the return: how to verify and what was left out.
