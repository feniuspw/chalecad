# F17-W1: App — ABNT drawing sheets with title block, architecture drawings, and vector PDF

The professional exports the **legal project** (projeto legal) for city hall: pranchas (drawing sheets) on A-series paper, with a full carimbo (title block), scale, north arrow, and legend. This card does three things:
- assembles the sheet;
- draws the architecture content;
- exports the vector PDF with all the sheets.

Read first:
- `docs/research/aprovacao.md`, sections 3.2, 3.3, 4.1, 4.3, 4.4, and 8.3.
- The contracts on main:
  - `apps/web/lib/sheets/types.ts` and `index.ts`, a skeleton that you implement;
  - `SheetSpec`, `SheetContent`, `ApprovalReport` in `packages/engine/src/schema/results.ts`;
  - `project.legal` in `project.ts`;
  - `catalog.constants.approval` (formats, margins, title block, scales, stages).
- What already exists for reuse:
  - `apps/web/lib/plan2d/*`: `planSvg`, `elevationSvg`, primitives in `svg.ts` (dimensions, graphic scale, north), rooms, furniture;
  - `apps/web/lib/export/dxf.ts`.

The engine (F17-E1) runs in parallel and will fill `results.approval`. While it is not on main:
- test with a `SheetSpec[]` built in the test itself;
- `buildSheets` uses `results.approval?.sheets` and, if absent, the local function `fallbackSheets(project, results)`, with the same sheets of the `legal` stage described in F17-E1.

## Deliver

### 1. Sheet (`lib/sheets/frame`)
- Catalog format, in landscape (A4 also in landscape), with NBR 10068 margins and a border.
- **Title block** (carimbo) in the lower right corner, with `titleBlock.width` as its width. Fields:
  1. work title (`meta.name`);
  2. sheet content (`spec.title`);
  3. stage (label and acronym from the catalog);
  4. owner and CPF/CNPJ (individual/company tax ID);
  5. work address, with lot, block, matrícula, and registration number;
  6. author of the discipline's project for the sheet: name, title, council, registration, state;
  7. execution responsible, when there is an `execution` professional;
  8. ART/RRT number;
  9. scale ("indicada" (indicated) when there are several);
  10. date (today, in pt-BR);
  11. sheet x/y and code (ARQ-01…);
  12. revision: the last of `legal.revisions`, with a table of revisions above the title block;
  13. drawn by and checked by (`drawnBy`, `checkedBy`);
  14. signature spaces for the owner and the technical responsible;
  15. lot area and built area;
  16. "ChaléCAD" and the file name.

  An empty field comes out as a blank line. Never write "undefined".
- **Free space for the city hall approval stamp** (`approvalBox`), above the title block, with the caption "Reservado à aprovação" (Reserved for approval).
- **Notes column** above the title block: notes from the contents, symbol legend, and the `APPROVAL_NOTICE`, or the `DISCLAIMER` if the constant does not exist.
- **Layout:** the contents of a sheet are arranged in rows within the usable area, each with an underlined title and the scale below ("PLANTA BAIXA — TÉRREO  ESC. 1:50" (FLOOR PLAN — GROUND FLOOR  SCALE 1:50)).
  - The final format is the suggested one (`spec.format`) or `legal.sheetFormat` when fixed. If it does not fit, go up one format.
  - If it does not fit even in A0, reduce the scale to the next one in the catalog list and record this in a note.

### 2. Architecture contents (`lib/sheets/arch`, exported in `archRenderers`)
Everything in paper mm at the requested scale, line weight per standard practice:
- cut 0.5;
- view 0.25;
- dimension and text 0.18.

Text of 2.5 mm, titles of 3.5 mm.

- **`situation`:** the lot (rectangle or polygon from `legal.lot`) with the street (`streetName`) and north, the hatched house projection, and the lot dimensions. Without `lot`, returns null.
- **`implantation`:**
  - lot, house projection, and dashed eave;
  - **dimensioned setbacks** to each boundary;
  - lot dimensions;
  - north;
  - hatched permeable area;
  - levels (pad and natural ground at the corners, on sloped terrain);
  - septic tank and well with the distance between them, when located;
  - access.
- **`areas`:** table with the area schedule (`approval.areas`) and the urban schedule (`approval.urban.checks`): index, project, allowed, status.
- **`plan`** (one per level, `target` = level id): starts from `planSvg`, converted to mm at the scale, with:
  - walls with real thickness and cut hatching;
  - frames with door swing;
  - room name and area;
  - exterior dimensions in two lines (partial and total) and main interior ones;
  - **finished floor level** (+0.00, +2.80…);
  - **indication of sections A and B** (dash-dot line with arrows and letter);
  - north;
  - stairs with up arrows.
  - Furniture in a thin line is optional; on legal sheets, only fixtures.
- **`section`** (`target` = 'A' transverse, 'B' longitudinal; new pure function `sectionSvg(model, axis, at)` in `lib/plan2d/section.ts`):
  - Section plane A passes through the middle of the depth; B through the middle of the width, shifted to cross the stairs and the bathroom when present.
  - Draws:
    - the terrain profile (natural and pad);
    - the radier;
    - cut walls (hatching) and views beyond (thin line);
    - the mezzanine floor;
    - the roof (planes with the panel thickness);
    - the ridge;
    - cut openings.
  - **Vertical dimensions:** ceiling height of each level, floor to floor, ridge and eave.
  - **Levels:** floor, mezzanine, ridge.
  - Use the `BuildingModel` (walls, roof planes, levels, stairs, `site`).
- **`elevation`** (`target` = side): starts from `elevationSvg` in mm, with levels, height dimensions, materials in text (facade and roof panel, from the finishes), and ground line.
- **`roof`:**
  - outline of the planes and eaves;
  - ridge;
  - **slope arrows with the inclination in %**;
  - gutters and downspouts, when `roof.gutters`;
  - roof panel specified in the note;
  - north.

### 3. `buildSheets(ctx)` and `exportSheetsPdf(ctx)`
- `buildSheets` draws all the `SheetSpec` with the `registry` (architecture from W1 and engineering from W2). Content without a renderer or empty goes to `skipped` and the sheet continues. A fully empty sheet leaves the list. Numbering x/y at the end.
- `exportSheetsPdf`: **vector** PDF, one page per sheet, at real size (mm), selectable text.
  - Use `jspdf` with `svg2pdf.js`, with pinned versions in `package.json`. Or convert the SVG to `@react-pdf/renderer` primitives, which is already a dependency. Choose the one that gives correct vector text and strokes and justify it in the report.
  - Helvetica font with correct pt-BR accents.
- Optional, if time remains: `exportSheetsDxf(ctx)`, one DXF per sheet (reusing `dxf.ts`), in a .zip.
