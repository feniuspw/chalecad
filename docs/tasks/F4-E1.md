# F4-E1: Engine — sloped terrain (building pad, cut and fill, slopes, retaining walls, machinery)

**Goal:** for `site.type = 'sloped'`, compute the building pad (platô), cut and fill volumes (balanced when `platformElevation = 'auto'`), slopes (taludes), the modified terrain mesh for 3D, retaining walls (arrimo) from the extras, and machinery/transport costs. Fills `results.terrain` (`TerrainReport`) and the quantity lines of the `terrain` stage.

**May change:** `packages/engine/src/terrain/**` (new), `packages/engine/src/extras/**` (retaining wall), `packages/engine/src/compute.ts` (call terrain, timings, and pass the pad elevation onward), `packages/engine/src/quantities/**` (only to integrate the terrain stage lines and the retaining wall), `packages/engine/src/budget/**` (only if labor per machine is needed — prefer service lines with a price), `packages/engine/src/warnings/**`, `packages/engine/src/index.ts`, `packages/engine/test/terrain/**`.
**Do not change:** `src/schema/**`, `packages/catalog/**`.

## Model
- Natural terrain: inclined plane. Natural elevation z(x, y) = −tan(slope) · ((x − cx)·sin(az) + (y − cy)·cos(az)), with slope = atan(slopePct/100), az = `downhillAzimuthDeg` (0 = descends toward +y, 90 = +x), (cx, cy) center of the building; z = 0 at the center.
- Pad: rectangle = bbox of the footprint expanded by `platformMargin` (front = −y, back = +y, left = −x, right = +x). Pad elevation zp: `auto` → zp that equalizes cut volume × (1/compactionFactor… use: required fill volume = cut × swell/… document it) — use the rule: compacted cut (cut / compactionFactor? no) — define explicitly: compacted fill requires `fill / compactionFactor` m³ of loose soil ≈ `fill / compactionFactor / swellFactor` m³ in the cut. Balanced: cut = fill / (compactionFactor·swellFactor)… choose a consistent convention, document it in the code and in the test, and solve zp by bisection.
- Volumes: integrate numerically on a grid (0.25 m step) covering pad + slopes: inside the pad, the difference between natural terrain and zp; outside the pad, slopes with inclination `cutSlopeHV` (cut) and `fillSlopeHV` (fill) until reaching the natural terrain (compute the final surface as max/min between the slope plane and the terrain). `slopeArea` = inclined area of the slopes.
- Export/import: cut surplus (bulked with swellFactor) goes to disposal (bota-fora); shortfall → import soil. `truckLoads` = ceil(bulked transported volume / truckM3). `machineDays` = ceil((cut + fill) / excavatorM3PerDay), minimum 1 if there is any earth movement.
- Lines (stage `terrain`): `excavator_day` (day rates), `truck_trip` (trips; the distance `disposalDistanceKm` enters as a note and multiplies the number of equivalent trips by a factor 1 + km/20 — document it), compaction (also use `excavator_day` or create a note), `geotextile` + `drain_pipe` for cut slopes > 1.5 m (toe of the slope).
- `mesh`: regular grid (0.5 m step) covering pad + slopes + 3 m margin, positions [x, y, z] (z up), triangle indices, `cut` per vertex (natural − final: + cut, − fill) for coloring.
- Retaining wall (`retaining` extras): volume/quantities by type — gabion (m³ = length × height × (0.5 + 0.1·height) base), reinforced concrete (concrete m³ = length × height × 0.2 + footing 0.6 × 0.3, steel 80 kg/m³), block (blocks as a wall + grout in all cells + steel); drain and geotextile if `drainage`. Warning `retaining.engineer` (warn) always: a retaining wall requires an engineered design.
- Integration: the building remains at z = 0 relative to the pad (the 3D view shifts the terrain, not the house): `platformZ` reports the pad elevation relative to the natural terrain at the center.
- Warnings: slope > 30% (warn, "considere fundação em palafita/estacas"); cut slopes > 3 m (warn); fill > 1 m under the building (warn, "aterro sob radier exige compactação controlada").

## Acceptance tests (manual calculation)
- Flat terrain (slope 0) → volumes 0, machineDays 0.
- 10% slope descending toward the front, pad 10 x 12 (margins 0), zp = 0, no slopes (very small cutSlopeHV/fillSlopeHV are not allowed: use a case with slopes and compare against the analytical sum of the pad prism + the slope volume calculated by hand, tolerance 3%). For the isolated pad: cut = fill = (1/8)·L·W·Δh… document the triangular prism formula and check it.
- `auto` balances: with swell/compaction = 1, cut ≈ fill (±2%).
- Positive export when zp is fixed below the natural terrain.
- Mesh with number of vertices = (nx+1)(ny+1) and valid indices.
- `computeAll` with `sloped` fills `terrain` and `terrain` lines; performance < 60 ms.
