# F6-E1: Engine — furniture (model, circulation checks, and budget) and ventilation by openable area

**Goal:** resolve the project's furniture (`project.furniture`) into outlines in the floor plan, check whether it fits and leaves circulation space, and cost it when the user asks. Also fix room ventilation: today it uses the full opening, but it must use the fraction that opens.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `Furniture` (`project.furniture`) and `Budget.includeFurniture` in `packages/engine/src/schema/project.ts`.
  - Rotation 0: width along x, depth along y, front (use side) toward −y.
  - `rotation`: degrees, counterclockwise, around `position`, which is the center.
- `FurnitureModel` and `BuildingModel.furniture` in `schema/results.ts`. Today `model.furniture` is `[]` in `model/index.ts`.

  - `furn_*` materials (category `furniture`, unit `un`, mass, price).
  - `constants.norms.circulationMin` (0.80 m) and `constants.norms.openableFraction` (per opening type).

## 1. Model (`model/furniture.ts`)
- `resolveFurniture(project, catalog, model)` → `FurnitureModel[]`.
  - Each piece of furniture becomes the `size.w × size.d` rectangle rotated by `rotation` around `position`: 4 corners, counterclockwise.
  - `clearance`: the furniture rectangle expanded by the clearances (front toward local −y, back toward +y, sides along ±x). If all four clearances are 0, `null`.
  - `z` is the level elevation.
  - `roomId`: the room on the same level whose `polygon` contains the center.
  - Item missing from the catalog: ignore the furniture and emit a `furniture.unknown` warning (info).
- Call it in `buildModel` after `completeRooms`.

## 2. Warnings (`warnings/furniture.ts`, wired into `warnings/index.ts`)
All with `refs: [furniture.id]`. Use short pt-BR messages that say what to do.
- `furniture.outside` (warn): the outline leaves the level's axis outline, with a 2 cm tolerance.
  - Use the same outline as the rooms: ground floor = axis polygon; mezzanine = `level.polygon`.
  - Inset the outline by half the thickness of the exterior walls: the furniture touches the inner face, not the axis. The thickness comes from `model.walls` (`thickness`).
- `furniture.wall` (warn): the furniture outline crosses a wall (`model.walls` on the same level, axis segment with thickness).
  - 1 cm tolerance, to allow touching.
  - Glass walls (`gable_glazed`) count.
- `furniture.collision` (warn): two pieces with `collides` overlap by more than 1 cm.
- `furniture.clearance` (info): the use zone of a piece is intruded on by another piece or by a wall.
  - Intrusion = intersection area with the zone (outside the piece itself) > 0.02 m².
  - Message: "Pouco espaço na frente de <name>: deixe 0,60 m livres".
- `furniture.door` (warn): the furniture intrudes on a door opening (`door` or `sliding_door`) of the level.
  - Door zone = rectangle of the opening's width × depth equal to the width, on both sides of the wall (door leaf swing).
  - For a sliding door, only a 0.60 m strip on both sides.
- `furniture.circulation` (info): for each room with furniture, check that there is a free path of `circulationMin` from the room's door to each piece.
  - If this gets expensive, do the simple version: the room's free area (area − Σ outlines of the colliding furniture) < 40% of the area → "Cômodo apertado".
  - State in the return which version you did.
- `furniture.headroom` (warn): the furniture height exceeds the clear height within its outline (sloped ceiling of the A-frame, mezzanine).
  - Sample the 4 corners and the center with the same ceiling function the rooms use (slab above / roof).
  - Message with how much is missing.
- `furniture.water` (info): furniture with `water` in a room that is not wet (`wet` false) or outside a room: "Precisa de ponto de água e esgoto".
- `furniture.stair` (warn): the furniture overlaps the projection of a stair (`model.stairs` of the level, outline in plan) or its landing (0.80 m in front of the first step).

## 3. Budget (`quantities/items.ts`)
- With `project.budget.includeFurniture`: one item per piece of furniture from the catalog, grouped by `item`, in quantity of units.
  - Stage `finishing`.
  - `hostId` = id of the first piece of furniture or of the room, whichever is simpler; explain.
  - Mass = material.
- Without the flag, nothing changes: the snapshot stays the same.

## 4. Ventilation by openable area (`model/rooms.ts` or `warnings/norms.ts`)
- Today `room.ventilation` compares the total glazing with area/12. The correct basis is the openable area: Σ (opening area × `openableFraction[type]`).
- Fixed glazed facade (`gable_glazed`) does not ventilate.
- If you need to store the number, compute it in the warning from `openingIds` (without changing `RoomModel`).
- Also adjust the volume-based warning (`norm.ventilation`, if it exists) the same way.

## 5. Engineer package
Furniture warnings with warn level become a question only if there is `furniture.headroom` or `furniture.stair`. The rest is layout, not structure.
