# F8-W3: App — "Obra" tab (bar-chart schedule and purchase checklist)

**Goal:** the self-builder tracks the construction within the project itself. They see a bar-chart schedule (stages over the weeks, with dates from the start) and a purchase list per stage with a "bought" checkbox, amount paid, and how much remains. They mark stages as completed. Everything is saved in the project.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `project.progress` (`startDate?`, `purchased: Record<itemId, { at, paid?, note? }>`, `stagesDone: Stage[]`) in `schema/project.ts`. It changes no calculation.
- `project.budget.durationsWeeks` (duration per stage) and `results.budget.schedule` (weekly disbursement, only when durations exist).
- `results.budget.lines` (items per stage with `itemId`, quantity, unit, and prices) and `byStage`.
- `catalog.constants.leadTimes` (delivery lead times) and the construction guide in `@chalecad/engine` (`reports/guide.ts`). Read how the guide estimates stage durations (catalog man-hours), if there is an exported function.

**May change:**
- `apps/web/components/results/WorkTab.tsx` (new) and the tab registration in `ResultsPanel.tsx`.
- `apps/web/lib/project/work.ts` (new, pure and tested).
- `apps/web/lib/export/**`, for the purchase list with status in CSV. Do not change what the DXF already does.
- `apps/web/lib/pdf/**` and `apps/web/components/pdf/**`, for the optional "Cronograma e compras" (Schedule and purchases) page.
- Tests alongside.

**Do not change:** `packages/**`, `lib/store/**`, and `package.json`, nor `components/editor/**`, `components/onboarding/**`, `components/projects/**`, `lib/project/presets/**`, and `lib/project/templates.ts` (belonging to F8-W2, in parallel).

## 1. Logic (`lib/project/work.ts`)
- `stageDurations(project, results, catalog)`: weeks per stage.
  - Uses `budget.durationsWeeks` when the user defined it.
  - Otherwise, estimates from the guide's or the catalog's man-hours (crew of 2 people, 44 h/week; round to 0.5 week, minimum 0.5).
  - State the origin of each value: "definido" (defined) or "estimado" (estimated).
- `ganttRows(...)`: sequential bars in `STAGE_ORDER` order, only stages with a cost.
  - Allows simple overlap: Installations starts in the middle of Enclosure (Fechamento); Finishing after Installations.
  - Each bar has a start and end in weeks and in dates (`startDate` + weeks). Without `startDate`, the dates stay relative: "Semana 1–3" (Week 1–3).
- `purchaseList(results, progress)`: budget lines grouped by stage, with `bought`, `paid`, and `estimated` (the line's material).
  - Totals per stage and overall: budgeted, paid, remaining to buy.
  - Difference paid − budgeted on purchased lines with `paid`.
- `buyBy(row, stageStart, leadTimes)`: deadline to buy = stage start − category lead time (steel profiles 21 days, concrete 3…). Highlight when the date has already passed and the item was not bought.
- Tests:
  - defined vs. estimated durations;
  - Installations overlap;
  - purchase totals;
  - deadline with and without `startDate`.

## 2. Obra tab (`WorkTab.tsx`)
- Always appears (useful even without data). `HardHat` icon.
- **Top:** start date (date input, saved in `progress.startDate`), total duration, expected finish, and progress (stages done / total; R$ paid / budgeted).
- **Schedule:**
  - horizontal bars in SVG/HTML, one per stage, with the `STAGE_COLORS` color and a weeks/dates label;
  - vertical "hoje" (today) line, when there is a `startDate`;
  - "concluída" (completed) checkbox per stage (`progress.stagesDone`), which makes the bar hatched/faded;
  - field to edit the stage duration (writes `budget.durationsWeeks`, which feeds the weekly disbursement that already exists).
- **Purchases:**
  - accordion per stage with the lines (name, qty, unit, estimated), "comprado" (bought) checkbox (writes `progress.purchased[itemId] = { at: today }`), optional "pago (R$)" (paid) field, and the deadline;
  - filters: Tudo (All) / Falta comprar (Still to buy) / Comprado (Bought).
- All edits use `update('…', draft => …)` (undoable), with `updateCoalesced` on the numeric fields.
- "Exportar lista de compras (CSV)" (Export purchase list (CSV)) button with the status columns.

## 3. PDF
- Optional "Cronograma e compras" page, when there is a `startDate` or any purchase marked:
  - the simplified Gantt (table of stages with start and end);
  - the list of what remains to buy, with deadline.
