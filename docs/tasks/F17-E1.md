# F17-E1: Engine — approval report (code compliance check, urban indices, area schedule, ART/RRT, drawing sheets, descriptive reports)

The professional (engineer or architect) uses ChaléCAD to **validate the project against Brazilian standards** and **export all the documentation** to issue the ART (Anotação de Responsabilidade Técnica, the engineer's liability record) / RRT (the architect's equivalent) and get the project approved at city hall. This card is the pure part: `results.approval` (`ApprovalReport`).

Read first:
- `docs/research/aprovacao.md`, especially sections 1.3–1.4, 3.2–3.3, 4.4, 5, 8.1 to 8.4.
- The contracts already on main:
  - `packages/engine/src/schema/project.ts`: "Fase 17" (Phase 17) block (`Legal`, `Lot`, `Zoning`, `Professional`, `ComplianceReview`, `ProjectStage`, `Discipline`), in `project.legal`.
  - `packages/engine/src/schema/results.ts`: `ApprovalReport` and neighboring types, plus `APPROVAL_NOTICE`.
  - `packages/catalog/src/data/constants.ts` → `approval` (standards with `confirmed`, formats, title block, scales, `artActivities`, `stages`).

## Deliver: `computeApproval(project, results, catalog): ApprovalReport`

The function is pure and runs at the end of `computeAllWith`, after the warnings. It receives the already computed `Results`, without `approval`. The result goes to `results.approval`. Goal: under 5 ms on the example.

### 1. Code compliance check (`compliance`)
Each item has a stable `id`, discipline, standard with edition (taken from `catalog.constants.approval.norms`), criterion, value and limit formatted in pt-BR, `refs`, and `source`. Group them:

- **Architecture and building code:**
  - ceiling height per room, against `zoning.minCeiling` (`missing` if not provided; the existing internal criterion counts as a warning);
  - lighting and ventilation: the `room.lighting` / `room.ventilation` warnings already do this; state the fraction used and mark `manual` with "conferir a fração do código de obras" (check the fraction in the building code);
  - minimum area (`room.area`);
  - guardrail and stairs (`norm.*`, `stair.*`);
  - safety glass;
  - accessibility NBR 9050 (`manual`/`na` for a private single-family home).
- **Urban** (only with `legal.lot`): TO (occupancy rate), CA (floor-area ratio), permeability, setbacks (front, rear, sides), height and stories, against the `zoning`. Missing limit → `missing`.
- **Structure:** one item per *type* of structural check (`stud`, `joist`, `header`, `bracing`, `anchorage`, `rafter`, `purlin`, `gable_stud`), with the worst case. Status by the worst ratio. Use the system's standard: LSF → NBR 16970-2 / NBR 14762; wood → NBR 7190-1. Also add the items for loads (NBR 6120), wind (NBR 6123: V0 and project category, `manual` "confirmar V0 pela isopleta do município" (confirm V0 from the municipality's isopleth)), and combinations (NBR 8681). Steel coating (NBR 15253) and interfaces (NBR 16970-3) enter as `manual`.
- **Foundation:** soil stress (check `soil`), plus the origin of the stress: `legal.soil.source === 'assumed'` becomes `warn`, "tensão admitida sem sondagem: exigir SPT (NBR 6484/8036)" (allowable stress assumed without a soil survey: require SPT). Survey with 0 borings and source `spt` → `missing`. Radier (raft slab) (NBR 6118) → `manual`.
- **Electrical:** from `results.installations.electrical` (see `ElectricalReport`):
  - minimum points;
  - Ib ≤ In ≤ Iz;
  - voltage drop;
  - RCD (DR) in wet areas;
  - SPD (DPS);
  - conduit fill;
  - service entrance standard (`manual`: "confirmar com a concessionária `legal.powerUtility`" (confirm with the utility (concessionária) `legal.powerUtility`));
  - earthing scheme (`legal.earthing`).

  The `elec.*` warnings become items. Without installations → `na` with the detail "instalações desligadas" (installations turned off).
- **Plumbing and sanitary:** from `results.installations.plumbing`: minimum pressure per point, velocity, minimum DN, reservoir, slope, UHC, venting, boxes, septic tank. The septic tank comes out `manual`, "dimensionada pela NBR 7229 (legado); conferir pela NBR 17076:2024" (sized by NBR 7229 (legacy); check against NBR 17076:2024). Distances between septic tank, well, and property line (with `septicPosition`/`wellPosition` in the lot) enter as `manual`, with the measured distance in the value. Stormwater NBR 10844 → `manual`.
- **Documentation:**
  - ART/RRT reported per discipline (the professional with the discipline and non-empty `docNumber`);
  - owner, address, and matrícula (property registry number) filled in;
  - `DISCLAIMER` present: always `ok`.

**Golden rule:** when the item's standard has `confirmed: false`, an automatic `ok` result becomes `manual`, with the found value kept and the detail "pendente de conferência profissional (edição/critério a conferir)" (pending professional check (edition/criterion to verify)). `warn` and `fail` stay as they are.

Copy `project.legal.reviews[id]` to `review`.

### 2. Missing data (`missingInputs`)
Keys with the field path (`legal.owner.name`, `legal.lot`, `legal.zoning.maxOccupancyPct`, `legal.soil`, `legal.professionals.structure` …). `required` depends on `legal.stage`:
- **`legal`:** required are owner, address, lot, an architecture professional, and basic zoning (TO/CA/setbacks). Their absence marks the project as "não pronto" (not ready).
- **`executive`:** adds soil survey and structure, electrical, and plumbing professionals.

### 3. Urban indices (`urban`)
- **Projection:** outer outline of the building plus eaves, joining the rectangles/polygons of the model. The note says "beirais incluídos na projeção; alguns municípios descontam até X m: confira" (eaves included in the projection; some municipalities deduct up to X m: check).
- **Built area:** sum per story by the outer outline; the mezzanine counts if `zoning.mezzanineCounts`.
- **Permeable:** lot − projection − `pavedArea`.
- **Setbacks:** smallest distance from the building projection (without eave, with a note) to each lot boundary. Use `buildingOffset` and `rotationDeg`; for a polygonal lot, distance to the segments, classifying each segment as front/rear/sides by orientation (front = segment closest to y = 0).
- **Height:** from the ground to the highest point. On sloped terrain, use the pad elevation and the lowest natural ground under the projection.
- **`checks`:** one per index.

### 4. Area schedule (`areas`)
- Rooms with name, use, area, and perimeter.
- Per story: built (outer outline of the level) and usable (sum of the rooms).
- Totals and covered open area (porch/covered deck, when present in the extras).

### 5. ART/RRT form (`art`)
- Owner, contractor (falls back to the owner when absent), and work address on one line. Coordinates from `meta.location`.
- The lines come from `catalog.constants.approval.artActivities`, filtered by the project:
  - foundation only with radier;
  - electrical and plumbing only with installations;
  - septic tank only with `sewerDisposal === 'septic'`;
  - architecture: the CAU line when the architecture professional is CAU, otherwise the CREA one;
  - wood structure changes the text to wood frame / NBR 7190.
- Quantities:
  - `built_area`: built area;
  - `radier_area`: radier area, from the model/quantities;
  - `installed_kva`: installed load from the electrical report, in kVA;
  - `septic_volume`: septic tank volume in m³.
- `professionalId` and `contractValue` come from `legal`.
- `notes` suggests the remark, e.g.: "Projeto estrutural em LSF, perfis Ue 90×40×0,95 (NBR 15253), radier e=12 cm, área 54,2 m²" (Structural project in LSF, Ue 90×40×0.95 profiles (NBR 15253), radier e=12 cm, area 54.2 m²).
- `blanks`: labels of the empty required fields.

### 6. Drawing sheets (`sheets`)
The `SheetSpec` list depends on the stage:

- **`preliminary`/`draft`:**
  - ARQ-01: plans and sections;
  - ARQ-02: elevations and roof.
- **`legal`:**
  - ARQ-01: site location, site plan with setbacks, and area/urban schedule;
  - ARQ-02: floor plans of all levels;
  - ARQ-03: sections A and B;
  - ARQ-04: elevations (the 4);
  - ARQ-05: roof.
- **`executive`:** everything from `legal`, plus:
  - EST-01: foundation;
  - EST-02: structure (panel and beam plan);
  - EST-03: panel elevations;
  - EST-04: structural roof and notes;
  - ELE-01: electrical plan per level;
  - ELE-02: single-line diagram and load schedule;
  - HID-01: cold/hot water;
  - HID-02: sewage and venting;
  - HID-03: isometric, water tank, and septic tank.

  Include only those that apply: without installations, no ELE/HID; without a septic tank, no detail for it.

Suggested scales:
- site location 1:500;
- site plan 1:200 (1:100 if the lot is small);
- plans, sections, and elevations 1:50 (1:75 or 1:100 when the largest dimension exceeds 12 m);
- roof 1:100.

Suggested format: the smallest of the A series in which the content fits at the scale, discounting margins and title block. Use the catalog formats, with usable area = format − margins − title block (title block width × usable height).

### 7. Descriptive reports (`memorials`)
Clear pt-BR text, short sentences, project values. Standards cited with their edition.

- **Architecture (descriptive):**
  - identification;
  - site and urban parameters;
  - program (rooms and areas);
  - construction system (foundation, structure, enclosures, insulation, roof with panel and slope, frames from the `openings`, finishes from `finishes`);
  - installations (summary);
  - performance (U of the walls and roof from `performance`, bioclimatic zone).
- **Structure (calculation):**
  - system;
  - standards;
  - materials (steel and fy from the catalog, profiles used with section and thickness, boards);
  - actions (table from `structure.loads`) and combinations;
  - checks (table of type, worst element, demand, capacity, ratio, status);
  - reactions and weight on the foundation;
  - assumptions and limitations, including "pré-dimensionamento automático: o responsável deve verificar ligações, estabilidade global e detalhes" (automatic preliminary sizing: the responsible professional must verify connections, global stability, and details).
- **Foundation (calculation):**
  - radier (thickness, edge, edge beam);
  - allowable stress and origin;
  - average contact pressure;
  - survey recommendation.
- **Electrical:**
  - installed load, demand, and service entrance standard;
  - criteria (minimum cross-section, drop, FCA/FCT);
  - circuit table;
  - RCD/SPD;
  - earthing.
- **Plumbing and sanitary:**
  - consumption and reservoir;
  - weights method, pressures, and point table;
  - sewage (UHC and DN);
  - venting;
  - septic tank, with the NBR 17076 note.
- **Performance (NBR 15575):** partial statement with the available data and a list of what the responsible professional completes.

### 8. `summary`, `ready`, `disclaimer`
- `summary`: one per discipline present. `reviewed` counts the items with review `accepted` or `na`.
- `ready`: no `fail` without an `accepted`/`na` review and no `missing` with `required`.
- `disclaimer`: `APPROVAL_NOTICE`.

## Tests (`packages/engine/src/approval/*.test.ts`)
- The default example computes `approval` and each discipline has at least one item.
- The golden rule: standard `confirmed: false` plus `ok` gives `manual`.
- Urban indices against manual calculation (12 × 30 lot, 6.5 × 8.5 house at [3, 5], eave 0.6):
  - TO;
  - setbacks (front 5, sides 3 and 2.5, rear 16.5, without eave);
  - 90° rotation.
- `missingInputs` changes with the stage.
- The copied review makes `ready` become true.
- The ART form with installations and a septic tank brings the right quantities.
- The sheets per stage and the reduced scale for a large house.
