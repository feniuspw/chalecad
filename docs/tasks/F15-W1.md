# F15-W1: Quality of the generated floor plan and furniture (wizard and ready-made templates)

The user evaluated the houses generated by the wizard and said the automatic assembly is still crooked, with poorly positioned furniture and walls that make no sense.

The visual audit (floor plans drawn from the engine) found:
- **gambrel-2q** (2 bedrooms, mezzanine, bathroom upstairs):
  - stairs in the middle of the living room, against the core wall;
  - TV on one exterior wall and sofa on the opposite exterior wall, with the stairs and the core wall between them. The TV cannot be seen from the sofa.
- **gable-1q:** the TV is on the kitchen side, behind the core wall, and the sofa is on the other side facing that wall.
- **modern-2q:** the TV is on the hallway wall, facing north, and the sofa on the west wall facing east; they are not facing each other.
- **aframe-2q:**
  - the living room (sofa and TV) ended up at the back, under the mezzanine, while the glazed front was left with only the stairs and empty;
  - the bedroom annex leaves two wall stubs extending 2 m into the living room (the annex's top and bottom walls continue into the body of the A-frame). To the user, these are "walls that make no sense".
- **Half walls and knee walls** (1.10 m core, 0.9 m mezzanine knee walls) only make sense when they have a clear function (counter, guardrail, cabinet). Loose walls in the middle of a room, enclosing nothing, must go away.

To reproduce the audit, there is a disposable test at `apps/web/lib/zz-audit.test.ts`. It draws PNGs with `sharp` into `AUDIT_OUT=<folder>` and serves for you to see before and after. **Do not commit this file.** Use it and delete it, or leave it out of the commit.

## 1. Quality metrics (`layoutQuality.ts`, pure, over `Project` + `BuildingModel`)
Function `layoutProblems(project, model): string[]` with clear architect rules:
- **Living room:**
  - sofa and TV (stand) facing each other: opposite normals, with deviation ≤ 20°;
  - distance between 1.8 and 4.0 m;
  - **clear line of sight:** the segment between the centers does not cross a wall (of any height above 0.9 m), stairs, or tall furniture (refrigerator, cabinet);
  - the sofa is not against a knee wall below 1.2 m under a sloped roof plane (low ceiling height).
- **Furniture inside the room:** the whole furniture polygon inside the room polygon (2 cm tolerance). No furniture crosses a wall, door (the leaf's swing area), or stairs (with 0.6 m clearance at the foot and the top).
- **Bed:**
  - access of at least 0.6 m on one long side (for a double bed, on both, if the room has ≥ 2.8 m of usable width);
  - the headboard against a solid wall, not under a low window (sill < 1.0 m) nor on a knee wall.
- **Kitchen:**
  - sink, stove, and refrigerator triangle with a sum between 3.6 and 7.9 m and each side ≥ 0.9 m;
  - stove not against the refrigerator (at least 0.3 m or counter between them) and not under a window;
  - the sink may be under a window.
- **Bathroom:** toilet, sink, and shower inside the room; 0.6 m clearance in front of the toilet and sink; shower stall of at least 0.8 × 0.8 m.
- **Walls that make sense:** every interior wall must have at least 70% of its length separating two different rooms (or a room and the exterior). Exceptions, marked in the generator:
  - functional half wall (core counter next to the stairs);
  - mezzanine knee wall;
  - wall that supports the mezzanine edge.
  - Annex stubs that enter the body count as a problem.
- **Circulation:**
  - from every door to the entrance door there is a path 0.8 m wide free of furniture (0.1 m grid, BFS);
  - the living room has at least 40% of free floor.
- **A-frame with glass facade:** the living room (sofa) sits in the front half, facing the glass or the TV on a side wall, not at the back under the mezzanine.

## 2. Fix the generator and the furnishing
- Reorganize the living room: choose the living zone (the part of the social room free of the counter, the stairs, and circulation) and position the sofa and TV across the short dimension of that zone, with a clear line of sight. Dining table near the kitchen, if it fits.
- **Stairs:** stay against a wall, but must not split the living room in two. Prefer:
  1. against the core wall, with the whole living room on the other side or in front;
  2. next to a side wall, when there is headroom (straight-wall houses with ceiling height ≥ 2.6 m at the side).
  - The foot of the stairs is near the circulation, not facing the sofa.
- **A-frame:** living room at the front, next to the glass (sofa facing the glass or the TV on the core wall); kitchen and bathroom at the back, under the mezzanine.
- **A-frame annexes:** no stubs into the body. The annex walls end at the body's alignment. If the engine requires the walls until they meet the roof plane, use the smallest possible extension and describe it in the report.
- Remove walls with no function. Keep half walls and knee walls only with a function.
- **Ready-made templates:** run the metrics on the 5 and fix whatever fails (furniture and walls), without changing the character of each one. Update the displayed area and cost, if they change (there is a test checking them).
