# F5-W1: App — rooms and finishes in the editor, floor plan, and 3D

**Goal:** the user marks rooms (living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom…) by clicking inside the spaces, chooses floor and wall covering per room, the facade, the lining of the A-frame roof planes, and the roof panel color. Everything appears in 3D with texture, in the floor plan with name and area, and goes into the budget (the engine computes the quantities, in card F5-E1, in parallel).

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `Room`, `RoomUse`, `Finishes` (`project.rooms`, `project.finishes`) in `packages/engine/src/schema/project.ts`.
- `RoomModel` (`results.model.rooms`: `polygon`, `edges` with `hostId`, `area`, `ceiling`, `glazing`, `wet`) in `schema/results.ts`.
- Materials `finish_floor_*`, `finish_wall_*`, `finish_facade_*`, and `finish_ceiling_*` in the catalog, with `name`.
- Store (`lib/store/types.ts`): `Tool` includes `'room'` and `SelectionKind` includes `'room'`. `resolveSelection` returns `{ kind: 'room', room, resolved }`.
- Until F5-E1 lands, `results.model.rooms` is empty. Test with a hand-built `RoomModel`; the orchestrator integrates.

**May change:**
- `apps/web/components/scene/**`, `apps/web/lib/three/**`, `apps/web/lib/plan2d/**`, `apps/web/lib/project/**`.
- `apps/web/components/editor/properties/**`, including the `case 'room'` of `Inspectors.tsx`.
- `apps/web/components/editor/EditorShell.tsx`, only to put the **Cômodo** (Room) tool in the toolbar (icon, shortcut `C`, tooltip).
- `apps/web/lib/pdf/**`, for the room table in the PDF.

## 1. Editing (`lib/project/rooms.ts`, pure and tested)
- `addRoom(draft, level, seed, use)`: ID `r-` + 8 characters. `removeRoom`, `patchRoom` (use, name, floor, walls).
- `ROOM_USES`: pt-BR label and lucide icon per use.
- Automatic finish suggestion on creation:
  - bathroom: `finish_floor_ceramic` + `finish_wall_tile`;
  - kitchen: `finish_floor_porcelain` + `finish_wall_paint`;
  - bedroom: `finish_floor_vinyl` + `finish_wall_paint`;
  - living room: `finish_floor_porcelain` + `finish_wall_paint`;
  - others: nothing.
- `setFinish(draft, key, materialId | undefined)` for `project.finishes`.
- When dimensions or typology change (`reconcileProject` in `edit.ts`), keep the `seed` inside `planBounds`, or remove the room if the level disappears.

## 2. Room tool (`components/scene/tools/RoomTool.tsx`)
- `tool === 'room'`: in the floor plan or in 3D, the raycast on the active level's plane gives the point. The preview highlights the polygon of the room that already exists there (if any) or a marker.
- Click on an empty spot: opens a light menu (drei HTML popover) with the uses. Choosing one creates the room (`update('Marcar cômodo', …)`) and selects it.
- Click on an existing room: selects it.
- Wire it into the `ToolLayer` like the other tools.

## 3. Room inspector (`properties/RoomInspector.tsx`)
- Fields: name, use (Segmented or Select with icons), floor and walls (Select with catalog materials filtered by prefix, showing the price/m² from the project's price base).
- Engine summary: area, min/mean ceiling height, glazing (m² and fraction of the area). Show the `room.*` warnings for that room, coming from `results.warnings` with `refs`.
- Remove button.
- If `polygon` is null: notice "feche o ambiente com paredes" (close the space with walls).

## 4. Finishes section (`properties/FinishesSection.tsx`)
- In the project panel (where Typology and System are), a section with:
  - facade (`finish_facade_*` or "sem revestimento" (no covering));
  - roof plane lining (A-frame only, `finish_ceiling_*`);
  - roof panel color (color swatches);
  - list of the rooms with use and area, clickable to select.

## 5. Floor plan
- `lib/plan2d` and `PlanOverlay`: fill each room with a soft color per use and write the name and area ("Quarto · 9,0 m²") at the visual centroid (simple pole of inaccessibility or a centroid adjusted to fall inside).
- Also in the PDF (`lib/pdf`): "Cômodos" (Rooms) table (name, use, area, average ceiling height, floor, walls).

## 6. 3D with procedural textures (`lib/three/textures.ts` + `components/scene/FinishesView.tsx`)
- Generate a `CanvasTexture` per material with `RepeatWrapping`, real size in meters (the `ExtrudeGeometry` UVs are in meters: repeat 1/module size). Memoize by id.
  - **Wood:** ~12 cm planks with grain and tone variation (flooring, lambri (tongue-and-groove wood paneling), wood siding, lambri lining).
  - **Porcelain tile 60×60 and ceramic 45×45:** grid with thin grout and slight variation.
  - **Vinyl and laminate:** wide planks, light wood tone.
  - **Brick veneer:** courses of 6 × 23 cm with joints.
  - **Vinyl siding:** horizontal bands of 20 cm with shadow.
  - **Texture and paint:** flat color with light noise.
  - **Ceramic wall covering:** light 30×60 wall tile.
- Floor: the room polygon as a thin slab (1 cm) at the level's elevation, with the floor texture.
- Room walls: for each `edge` with a wall `hostId`, a vertical plane set back 2 mm from the interior face (`wallBasis` + thickness/2, on the room's side). Height up to the top of the wall outline along the stretch (use local `topAt` or the outline), capped by the ceiling. Covering texture.
- Facade: exterior walls (`exteriorSide !== null` and not `gable_glazed`) now use the facade texture in `Surface`. For this, allow an optional `map` in `Surface`/`Wall`, without changing the look when no choice is made.
- Roof plane lining: a plane under each `isWall` roof plane, with the `outline` contour in (u, v), offset −2 mm along the normal, and the lining texture.
- Roof panel color: `COLORS.roof` via `finishes.roofColor`.
- Performance: do not recreate textures or geometry per frame. Memoize by id and by `results`.
