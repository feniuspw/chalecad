# F17-W3: App — "Aprovação" (Approval) screen: legal data, lot, code compliance check with review, ART/RRT form, descriptive reports, and export package

The engineer or architect opens the project in ChaléCAD and:
1. fills in the legal data;
2. checks the code compliance verification item by item and gives a review (accepted, rejected, not applicable, with a note);
3. sees the form ready to copy into the CREA system (SITAC) or the CAU system (SICCAU);
4. exports **the complete package** for approval.

(ART = Anotação de Responsabilidade Técnica, the engineer's liability record; RRT = the architect's equivalent; CREA = engineering council; CAU = architecture council.)

## Deliver

### 1. "Aprovação" screen
Button in the top bar, with the shortcut `Shift+A` in the shortcuts table. It is a large dialog or a full-screen side page, with tabs.

#### a) "Dados" (Data)
Form for `project.legal`, with clear labels and a "why we ask" (one line):
- project stage;
- owner (name, CPF/CNPJ (individual/company tax ID) with mask and check-digit validation, address, contact);
- contractor ("o mesmo que o proprietário" (same as the owner) checked by default);
- work address, matrícula (property registry number), registry office (cartório), registration number, lot, block, subdivision;
- urban or rural area;
- electricity and water/sewage utilities (concessionárias);
- supply (network or well);
- earthing scheme;
- start and end dates;
- contract values per discipline.

Dedicated sections:
- **Lot:**
  - frontage, depth, and optional polygon (list of vertices);
  - house position on the lot (x/y offset and rotation), with a live SVG thumbnail showing lot, house, and setbacks;
  - paved area;
  - street name;
  - septic tank and well position.
- **Zoning:** zone, TO (occupancy rate), CA (floor-area ratio), permeability, setbacks, height, stories, minimum ceiling height, "mezanino conta" (mezzanine counts), source. Fixed notice: "Estes valores vêm do plano diretor e da lei de zoneamento do seu município: o ChaléCAD não os conhece" (These values come from your municipality's master plan and zoning law: ChaléCAD does not know them).
- **Soil:** stress origin, survey report reference, borings, water table, percolation test. Link to the allowable stress in `design.soilBearingKPa`.
- **Technical professionals:** list of professionals (name, title, CREA/CAU, registration, state, company, disciplines, ART/RRT number), add, remove, and edit. Validate the CAU registration format (A000000-0) as a warning only.
- **Sheets:** format, drafter, checker, revisions (add R01 with date and description).

Each field saves with `update('Dados legais: <field>', …)` (Legal data: <field>), coalesced per field, so as not to generate one undo per keystroke.

#### b) "Verificação" (Verification)
`approval.compliance` grouped by discipline, with:
- summary per discipline in chips (ok, warning, fails, manual, missing data, n/a, reviewed);
- filters (status, discipline, "só pendentes" (pending only)).

Each item shows:
- standard, edition, and section;
- criterion;
- value and limit;
- detail;
- "Mostrar no 3D/planta" (Show in 3D/floor plan) button when there are `refs` (use the same mechanism as the alerts: `setHighlight` or `select`);
- for `missing`, a button that takes to the field in the Dados tab.

**Review:** "Aceito" (Accepted) / "Rejeitado" (Rejected) / "Não se aplica" (Not applicable), note, and professional (select from those registered). Saves to `legal.reviews[id]` with today's date. Removing the review deletes the entry.

At the top, the badge "Pronto para exportar" (Ready to export) (`approval.ready`) or "N pendências" (N pending items), with the list of required `missingInputs`, clickable.

#### c) "ART/RRT"
The `approval.art` form, separated by professional. Each field has a **"Copiar"** (Copy) button. "Copiar tudo" (Copy all) generates the formatted text.

Activities come in a table with description, quantity, unit, contract value, and suggested remark. Empty fields (`blanks`) are highlighted.

Fixed note: "A ART é emitida pelo profissional no sistema do CREA da sua UF (ou o RRT no SICCAU); esta ficha só reúne os dados." (The ART is issued by the professional in the CREA system of their state (or the RRT in SICCAU); this form only gathers the data.) Include the official links, opened in a new tab:
- Confea/CREA: https://www.confea.org.br/;
- CAU: https://siccau.caubr.gov.br/.

When the professional enters the ART/RRT number, it goes to `professional.docNumber` and appears in the title blocks.

#### d) "Memoriais" (Descriptive reports)
- List of `approval.memorials`, with a preview (sections, paragraphs, tables) and a PDF of each memorial (memorial descritivo, descriptive specification) with `@react-pdf/renderer`, in a new document in `components/approval/pdf/*`.
- The header carries the work, owner, address, and the discipline's responsible professional; the footer carries page x/y and the `APPROVAL_NOTICE` (or the `DISCLAIMER`).
- Also as PDF:
  - the **code compliance check report**, with all items, review, and note, and with the data-entry statement: list of the data provided and its source;
  - the **ART/RRT form**;
  - the **utility form (concessionária)**: installed load, demand, phases, standard, and main breaker;
  - the **form for health surveillance/city hall (septic tank)**: contributors, volume, percolation, distances, when there is a septic tank;
  - the **building permit application form (alvará)**: owner, matrícula, registration number, areas, TO/CA/TP, stories, responsible professionals with ART/RRT.

#### e) Export package
An "Exportar pacote de aprovação (.zip)" (Export approval package (.zip)) button, with progress per step and a cancel option. Assembles folders:
- `01-pranchas/`: PDF of the sheets (`exportSheetsPdf`) and DXF (the current one from `dxf.ts`);
- `02-memoriais/`: one PDF per memorial;
- `03-fichas/`: ART/RRT, utility, septic tank, building permit;
- `04-verificacao/`: compliance report;
- `05-quantitativos/`: the current PDF with plans and list (quantity takeoff), plus the existing CSVs, if there is a pure function;
- `projeto.chalecad.json`;
- `LEIA-ME.txt` (README): what each file is, what is missing (pending items), and the notice.

A step that fails (e.g., sheets not yet implemented) goes into the LEIA-ME as "não gerado: motivo" (not generated: reason) and the zip comes out anyway. The file name is `aprovacao-<slug>-<data>.zip`. If `approval.ready` is false, warn beforehand ("Há N pendências; exportar mesmo assim?" (There are N pending items; export anyway?)) and write "MINUTA — pendências abertas" (DRAFT — open pending items) in the LEIA-ME.

### 2. Lot on the floor plan (floor plan view)
- With `legal.lot`, draw the lot outline (dash-dot line) and the minimum setback strips from the zoning, hatched in light red when the house encroaches.
- Dimension the real setbacks to the boundaries.
- Draw septic tank and well, when located.
- The layer toggles with a "Lote" (Lot) button in the floor plan view (shortcut `L`, if free; check the shortcuts table).
- Dragging the house on the lot is optional: the numeric fields are enough.

### 3. Glossary
New terms: ART, RRT, occupancy rate (taxa de ocupação), floor-area ratio (coeficiente de aproveitamento), permeability rate (taxa de permeabilidade), setback (recuo), matrícula, alvará, SPT, legal project (projeto legal), executive project (projeto executivo), title block (carimbo).
