# F13-W1: App — the wizard generates chalets that are born code-compliant

**Goal:** the chalet that comes out of "Me ajude a montar" (Help me build it) must not arrive with avoidable warning alerts. Today the generated projects come with:
- `room.lighting` and `room.ventilation`: windows too small for the area. E.g., a 38 m² open living room with 5 m² of glass, when the code asks for 1/6 of the area.
- `room.lighting` in the mezzanine bedroom: 0 m² of glass.
- `room.ceiling` in the mezzanine bedroom, with a sloped ceiling.
- Failed checks:
  - mezzanine joists spanning 6.5 m because the free edge has no support;
  - rafter of the modern style (shed) spanning 13 m because the slope runs along the length;
  - headers of large windows in the gable ends.

The generator must avoid this in the design itself and, at the end, run "Ajustar às normas" (Fit to codes) and explain in simple notes what is left.

**May change:**
- `apps/web/lib/project/wizard/**`.
- `apps/web/components/wizard/**`, only to show the notes and the compliance status in the result.
- Tests alongside.

**Do not change:** `packages/**`, `lib/store/**`, `package.json`, `components/scene/**`, and `lib/three/**` (from F12-W1, in parallel). Use `lib/project/normfix.ts`, `edit.ts`, `rooms.ts`, `furniture.ts`, and the rest of `lib/project/**` only as a library. Do not modify them; if you need something new, implement it in `wizard/`.

## 1. Windows by room size
After building the floor plan, for each room with `light` (living room, kitchen, bedroom) compute the area.
- **Lighting:** add or widen windows on the room's exterior edges until glass ≥ area/6 × 1.1, with margin.
- **Ventilation:** openable area ≥ area/12 × 1.1, by `constants.norms.openableFraction` (a sliding window opens 50%).
- In the open living room and kitchen, prefer a sliding glass door on the main facade and additional windows.
- Respect the stud modulation when possible, do not overlap openings, and keep 0.6 m from the corner.
- Limit windows to 1.5 m wide on common walls, because larger headers fail, and use more windows instead of one huge one.
- Bathroom and laundry: tilt ventilation window (area/12).
- Test each `light` room with the engine: without `room.lighting` or `room.ventilation`.

## 2. Mezzanine bedroom
- **Lighting:**
  - A-frame without annexes: skylight(s) on the roof plane over the mezzanine;
  - with annexes: window in the gable end (rear or front facade) within the mezzanine outline;
  - gable and gambrel: window in the gable end above the mezzanine floor.
- **Ceiling height:** the NBR asks for a 2.50 m average ceiling height for a bedroom, and a loft under a sloped roof does not reach it.
  - Reduce the mezzanine room to the strip with usable height, with a knee wall/low wall where the roof drops below ~1.5 m. Or mark the room as "Mezanino (loft)" (Mezzanine (loft)) with use `other`, which asks for 2.30 m. Choose whichever leaves the alert as info or no alert.
  - If it still ends up below, leave a clear note: "O quarto do mezanino tem teto inclinado: fica abaixo dos 2,50 m médios que a norma pede para quarto. É comum em chalés (loft), mas o engenheiro/prefeitura pode exigir que conte como área de uso eventual" (The mezzanine bedroom has a sloped ceiling: it is below the 2.50 m average the code requires for a bedroom. It is common in chalets (loft), but the engineer/city hall may require it to count as an occasional-use area).

## 3. Mezzanine support
- Align a ground-floor interior wall (of a bedroom, bathroom, or powder room under the mezzanine) with the mezzanine's free edge, so the joists span the short side.
  - The engine already chooses the short span when both edges have support: `framing/mezzanine.ts` `supported`. Read it to learn the tolerance.
- If there is no room underneath, use a support wall with a wide door or reduce the mezzanine depth until the beam passes.

## 4. Modern-style roof
- Shed: the slope runs along the short side (`ridgeAxis` or the equivalent of the `shed` typology).
- Flat roof: rafters on the short span.
- The rafter must pass with the catalog profile. If the short span is greater than ~5 m, use an interior support wall under the ridge and half the width.

## 5. Run "Ajustar às normas" at the end
- `planNormFixes(project)` applied to the final project.
- The cost shown in the result is the one after the adjustments.
- The leftover items become simple notes ("Vale conversar com o engenheiro sobre: …" (It is worth talking to the engineer about: …)).
- The result shows a badge:
  - "Dentro das verificações automáticas" (Within the automatic checks), when no warning alert or failed check remains;
  - or "N pontos para revisar" (N points to review), with the list.

## Acceptance tests
- The current matrix stays green.
- New check in the sampled matrix, with no warning alerts (`warn`/`error`), except `norm.safety_glass` (safety glass is a specification notice, and may become a note) and `norm.aframe_thrust` (information for the engineer):
  - no `room.lighting`, `room.ventilation`, or `room.ceiling` (if the mezzanine still needs a note, as info);
  - no structural check with ratio > 1 in the final result (after `planNormFixes`).
- List in the return the combinations that still have open items, and why.
- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/web typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, and `build` green (run separately).
