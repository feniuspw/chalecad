# F6-W1: App — furniture library, drag and drop, procedural 3D models, and floor plan

**Goal:** the user opens a furniture library, drags a sofa into the living room, and it snaps to the wall. They can rotate, move, duplicate, and delete it. The furniture appears in 3D (models drawn in code, good-looking and lightweight) and in the floor plan (architectural symbol). The engine warnings (collision, clearance, door, low ceiling) appear on the selected furniture. The budget can include the furniture. A "Mobiliar" (Furnish) button suggests a layout for a room.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `Furniture` (`project.furniture`) and `Budget.includeFurniture` in `packages/engine/src/schema/project.ts`.
  - Rotation 0: width along x, depth along y, front toward −y.
  - `rotation`: degrees, counterclockwise, around the center.
  - `tone`: optional, visual only.

## 1. Editing (`lib/project/furniture.ts`, pure and tested)
- `addFurniture(draft, level, item, position, rotation)`: ID `f-` + 8 characters.
- `moveFurniture`, `rotateFurniture` (90° step, or free with a value), `duplicateFurniture` (offset 0.3 m), `removeFurniture`.
- `furnitureFootprint(spec, position, rotation)`: 4 corners, for preview and snapping.
- `snapToWall(spec, point, rotationGuess, walls, level)`, for items with `wall`:
  - finds the nearest wall (within 0.6 m of the point) on the level;
  - rotates the furniture so its back rests against it (front facing the side of the point);
  - places the center at `thickness/2 + d/2 + 5 mm` from the axis;
  - returns `{ position, rotation, wallId }`.
  - No wall nearby: 5 cm grid and rotation kept.
- Snapping between pieces: align the edge with another piece's edge when within 8 cm. Optional, but desirable.
- `reconcileProject` (in `edit.ts`): remove furniture from a level that disappeared and keep furniture inside `planBounds`, pushing it inward.
- `furnishRoom(project, catalog, room: RoomModel)` → list of `Furniture`. It is the layout suggestion by use:
  - **Bedroom:** double bed (or single if the usable width is < 2.6 m) with the headboard on the longest wall without a window, nightstands on the sides if they fit, wardrobe on another free wall.
  - **Living room:** sofa on the longest wall without a door, TV unit opposite (at ~2.5 m or on the opposite wall), and coffee table between the two.
  - **Kitchen:** counter with sink, stove, and refrigerator in a line on the longest wall (if it does not fit, in an L).
  - **Bathroom:** toilet, basin, and shower stall placed against the walls.
  - **Laundry:** tub and washing machine.
  - Discard what does not fit: the outline test must not cross a wall, door, or another piece of furniture.
  - It is a heuristic: test that nothing overlaps and that everything stays inside the polygon.

## 2. Library (`components/editor/furniture/FurnitureLibrary.tsx`)
- Side panel or large popover, opened by the Furniture tool.
- Tabs or filters by group: Sala (Living), Jantar (Dining), Quarto (Bedroom), Cozinha (Kitchen), Banheiro (Bathroom), Lavanderia (Laundry), Externo (Outdoor), Decoração (Decor).
- Search by name.
- Each item is a card with a thumbnail (SVG floor plan symbol, coming from the same drawing as the floor plan), name, dimensions ("2,10 × 0,90 m"), and reference price.
- Dragging the card onto the scene (HTML5 drag) drops the furniture at the point on the active level's floor, snapped to the wall. Clicking the card chooses the item and enters placement mode (click on the scene to drop).
- During the preview:
  - a 3D/plan ghost follows the mouse, already snapped;
  - the use zone appears dashed;
  - it turns red if it collides (client-side test with the outline);
  - `R` rotates 90°.
- `Esc` cancels.

## 3. 3D scene (`components/scene/FurnitureView.tsx` + `lib/three/furniture/*.ts`)
- One builder per `FurnitureModelKind` that returns geometries with material (rounded boxes with `RoundedBox`/`ExtrudeGeometry`, cylinders, and tubes). Scales by the catalog `size`, to serve the various sizes.
  - **Upholstered** (sofa, armchair): base, seat, separate cushions, arms, and thin legs. Light gray or beige fabric.
  - **Bed:** platform, white mattress, wooden headboard, pillows, and a folded blanket at the foot.
  - **Tables:** top and legs. Dining tables have chairs around them (4 or 6, by width); the round one has 4.
  - **Cabinets and TV unit:** body, doors with panel moldings, and handles.
  - **Kitchen:** counter with dark stone top, stainless steel sink basin, 4-burner stove, and 2-door refrigerator.
  - **Bathroom:** white porcelain toilet (rounded shapes), basin with cabinet, shower stall with translucent glass and shower head.
  - **Laundry, outdoor, and decor:** washing machine with round door; tub; lounge chair; plant (pot and foliage with a few green spheres or cones); rug as a thin colored slab.
- `tone` changes the main color (light wood, wood, dark, white, color).
- Performance:
  - geometry memoized by (model, size, tone);
  - shared materials;
  - nothing recreated per frame;
  - target of 60 pieces of furniture with no noticeable drop.
  - Shadows: the furniture casts and receives.
- Selection: clicking selects (`select({kind:'furniture', id})`) with a highlighted outline. With the furniture selected:
  - dragging on the floor moves it, with snapping;
  - a gizmo/handle rotates it;
  - `R` rotates 90°; `Delete` removes it; `Ctrl/⌘+D` duplicates it.
- The furniture appears in the Finished and Presentation modes, in Exploded if easy, and in the Plan (top view), but not in Structure or in the heat map.
- Walk-through: the furniture enters the walk collision (`lib/three/walk.ts`), with the outline as an obstacle. A rug does not count.

## 4. Floor plan (`lib/plan2d`, `PlanOverlay`)
- Architectural symbol per model, in thin stroke:
  - bed with pillow; sofa with seat and backrest; table with chairs;
  - toilet with lid; sink with basin; stove with 4 circles; shower stall with a diagonal;
  - wardrobe with a line of doors; plant with a circle.
- Selected furniture: dashed use zone.
- Furniture with a warn warning: red stroke.
- PDF: if the PDF floor plan draws rooms, also draw the furniture, with the same symbol. If it gets complicated, leave it out and say so in the return.

## 5. Inspector (`properties/FurnitureInspector.tsx`)
- Item name and swapping the item within the same group (Select).
- Dimensions, price, and the room it is in.
- Rotation: 90° buttons and a field in degrees.
- Position (x, y) in numeric fields.
- Tone (swatches).
- `furniture.*` warnings for this piece, coming from `results.warnings` with `refs`.
- Duplicate and Remove.

## 6. Room and budget
- In the `RoomInspector`:
  - a **Mobiliar** button (`furnishRoom`), which only adds what fits, in a single undoable change, with a toast saying how many items were added;
  - the list of the room's furniture.
- In the `FinishesSection`, or in the project panel (where Budget is, if it has its own section): a **Incluir móveis no orçamento** (Include furniture in the budget) switch (`budget.includeFurniture`), with the total of furniture next to it.
