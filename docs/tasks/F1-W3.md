# F1-W3: Scene — editing tools, 2D plan, skeleton mode, and exploded view

**Goal:** make the 3D view editable and technical: draw interior walls, insert openings, place stairs, edit the mezzanine, measure; plan/elevation views; skeleton mode with profile instancing; exploded mode; highlighting of pieces coming from the results panel.

**May change:** `apps/web/components/scene/**`, `apps/web/lib/three/**`, `apps/web/lib/tools/**` (+ tests).
**Do not change:** `apps/web/lib/store/**` (use `useEditor` and the actions of the `lib/store/types.ts` contract: `tool`, `toolOptions`, `cameraView`, `viewMode`, `activeLevelId`, `selection`, `hoverId`, `highlight`, `update`, `select`…), `apps/web/components/editor/**` and `components/results/**` (another agent), `packages/**`, `package.json`. The toolbar and the view buttons already exist in the editor and change the store; your scene reacts to the store. If you need a new UI control, put it as an overlay inside `components/scene/` (e.g., `SceneToolbar.tsx`, `ViewCube.tsx`) and export it; the orchestrator positions it.

## Deliverables
1. **Snap** `lib/tools/snap.ts` (pure, tested): 10 cm grid; stud modulation (multiples of `studSpacing` along the wall); notable points (wall endpoints and midpoints, corners); returns the adjusted point + snap type for visual feedback. Respects `toolOptions.snapGrid/snapModulation`; Alt temporarily turns snap off.
2. **Work plane**: raycast onto a horizontal plane at the elevation of the active level (`activeLevelId`) for plan tools.
3. **Wall tool** (`tool === 'wall'`): click sets the start, shows a rubber-band line with live length (drei HTML label), the next click creates the wall (`update('Desenhar parede', d => d.walls.push(...))`) (Draw wall) and continues chaining from the last point; Esc/double-click ends. Orthogonal with Shift. `wet` from `toolOptions.wallWet`. IDs `w-` + 8 chars.
4. **Opening tool** (`tool === 'opening'`): hovering over a wall (exterior or interior) shows a preview of the opening (size from `toolOptions`) following the cursor along the wall with snap to the modulation; red if it does not fit; click creates the opening on the right host (`ext-*` or the user wall's ID) with `offset` in meters from the start of the host (watch out for the from→to direction). Skylight on roof planes.
5. **Stair tool** (`tool === 'stair'`): preview of the stair (type from `toolOptions.stairType`) following the cursor on the ground floor; R rotates 90°; click creates it with `from` = ground level and `to` = first level above (if there is no upper level, show a toast asking to create the mezzanine).
6. **Mezzanine** (`tool === 'mezzanine'`): in plan, shows the upper-level polygon with handles at the vertices and edges; dragging moves a vertex/edge with snap; clicking on an edge adds a vertex; if there is no mezzanine, click-and-drag draws a rectangle and creates the level (`elevation` = ceiling height, `deck: 'osb18'`, `guardrail: true`).
7. **Measure** (`tool === 'measure'`): two clicks measure distance with a label; persistent until Esc.
8. **Selection and drag** (`tool === 'select'`): clicking selects (wall, opening, stair, roof plane, slab); a selected opening can be dragged along the wall (with snap, `updateCoalesced`); a selected interior wall shows handles at its ends to drag; stair draggable in the plane. Discreet gizmo, appropriate cursor.
9. **Views** (`cameraView`): `3d` orbital perspective; `plan` orthographic top camera showing the active level with a cut above it (walls cut at 1.2 m above the level base, like a floor plan: use a clipping plane), automatic dimensions of exterior walls and openings (dimension lines with text), visible grid; `front/back/left/right` orthographic elevations. Smooth camera transition.
10. **Skeleton mode** (`viewMode === 'skeleton'`): renders `results.pieces` with an `InstancedMesh` per `profileId` (simplified C-section geometry extruded, or a thin box with the web/flange width; orientation by `start`, `end`, `webNormal`); walls and closures hidden; radier (raft slab) and slabs translucent. Must keep 60 fps with 2,000+ pieces (one draw call per profile; matrices computed once per `results` change). Color by role (studs, tracks, headers, joists, rafters, purlins) with an overlay legend.
11. **Exploded mode** (`viewMode === 'exploded'`): separated layers (radier, structure, closure, roof), displaced in z with animation.
12. **Highlight** (`highlight`): highlighted pieces/hosts in strong amber; the rest fades (opacity); in finished mode, if the highlight is only pieces, temporarily switch to skeleton (with a "mostrando estrutura" (showing structure) notice) or draw the pieces on top.
13. Performance: do not recreate geometries per frame; memoize by `results` / ids; `frameloop="demand"` with invalidate on interactions.
