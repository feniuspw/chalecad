# F18-L: Lighting — correct height under the roof and mezzanine, automatic switch, single grid, and live lux

The user asked to review "the part about placing lighting." An audit found the following (files relative to the root).

## Today
- **"Luz" (Light) tool:** lives in the electrical dock (`apps/web/lib/elec/kinds.ts`, `apps/web/components/editor/elec/ElecDock.tsx`, `apps/web/components/scene/tools/elec/ElecTools.tsx`).
  - Near a wall it becomes a wall sconce; inside a room it becomes a ceiling light, with `height = room.ceilingAvg` (`apps/web/lib/elec/placement.ts`).
  - Shift+click generates a grid by the lumen method (`apps/web/lib/elec/lightGrid.ts`).
  - Dragging moves; Delete removes.
  - To connect a switch, you must select the switch and click the light.
- **3D:** `apps/web/components/scene/ElecLuminaires.tsx` clamps the light to the roof (`roofZAt` in `apps/web/lib/elec/lights.ts`) only in the drawing.
- **Engine:**
  - automatic layout in `packages/engine/src/installations/electrical/layout.ts`;
  - loads in `loads.ts` (NBR 5410: 100 VA up to 6 m² + 60 VA per whole 4 m²);
  - lux in `lighting.ts`;
  - local `heightAt` in `packages/engine/src/model/rooms.ts`;
  - `targetLux`/`minLux` tables in `packages/catalog/src/data/constants.ts` (`electrical` block).

## Bugs to fix, in priority order
1. **Wrong height.** Every ceiling point stores the room's average ceiling height (`placement.ts`, `ElecTools.tsx`, `layout.ts`). Under a sloped roof, the light sits low under the ridge and above the roof near the eave. The engine uses `p.height` for the box height and the cable length (`loads.ts`).
   - **Fix:** export from the engine `ceilingZAt(model, levelId, xy)`: the lowest of the roof planes and the underside of the mezzanine slab above the point, when the point is under the mezzanine. Use it in:
     - `placement.ts` (placement and `relocatePoint`);
     - the grid;
     - `layout.ts`;
     - in 3D, removing the hack of clamping only in the drawing, or keeping it only as protection.
   - The point stores the real ceiling height at that spot.
2. **Light inside or above the mezzanine slab.** Solved by item 1, because the mezzanine enters the calculation. The double-height part uses the roof.
3. **A light placed after the switch ends up without a switch.**
   - When placing a light (or grid) in a room that already has a switch, connect it to the nearest door's switch.
   - Without a switch in the room, create one next to the room's main door, as the engine layout does (reuse the engine's logic). It is also worth showing a toast with "Adicionar interruptor" (Add switch).
   - The automatic grid does the same.
4. **Duplicated grid, different from the engine's.** `lightGrid.ts` copies `utilization` and `gridPositions` from the engine and does not switch to the stronger luminaire. Make the editor call the engine function (export from `@chalecad/engine` a `suggestRoomLighting(room, model, catalog, opts)` that returns positions, luminaire, and lux) and delete the copy.
5. **One-light grid lands on the cursor.** Use the real centroid of the polygon. In an L-shaped room, when grid positions fall outside, try shifting or densifying. Warn when the final lux is below `minLux`.
6. **No lux feedback.** The ghost and the room/light inspector show live "cômodo: X lux / alvo Y lux" (room: X lux / target Y lux) and the power; reuse the engine and the worker result. There is already lux per room in the engine result (`results.installations.electrical`, see `apps/web/components/results/ElectricalTab.tsx`).
7. **Luminaire type.** On an indoor ceiling, outdoor floodlights (`flood_*`) and strips are not allowed, unless the user chooses them on purpose. For the strip, length is missing: if the schema does not support it, leave the catalog's fixed length and explain.
8. **Switches in the light inspector.** Show the "Controlada por" (Controlled by) list with a selector to add or remove a switch. When moving the light to another room, redo the connection and circuit or warn.
9. **Shortcut for the Light tool.** Choose a free key (check `drawKeys.ts`, the editor shortcuts, and the glossary) and register it in the shortcuts glossary.
10. **Lux table** against NBR ISO/CIE 8995-1:2013, adjusting `targetLux`/`minLux` in `constants.ts` (only the `electrical` block):
    - kitchen (counter) 500 target / 300 min;
    - office 500;
    - bathroom (mirror) 300 / 200;
    - bedroom 150 / 100;
    - living room 150–300;
    - circulation/stairs 100–150.
    - Also adjust the room index (K) to use the real area and perimeter of the polygon instead of the bounding rectangle.
