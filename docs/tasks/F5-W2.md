# F5-W2: Presentation mode — real sun, beautiful lighting, walk-through, and image export

**Goal:** a "Apresentação" (Presentation) mode in the editor. The sun sits at its real position for the site, by city, date, and time, with a control to drag the time of day. Soft shadows, sky, natural ambient light, and ambient occlusion. First-person camera to walk inside. A button to save a high-resolution image.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `project.meta.location?` ({ lat, lon }) and `project.meta.northDeg`: degrees clockwise from +y; 180 = north at the front, which is the default.
- Store (`lib/store/types.ts`):
  - `ViewMode` includes `'presentation'`.
  - `sun: { date: 'YYYY-MM-DD', minutes }`, with `setSun(partial)`.
  - `walk: boolean`, with `setWalk`. `setViewMode` leaves walk mode when leaving the presentation.

**May change:**
- `apps/web/components/scene/**` and `apps/web/lib/three/**`.
- `apps/web/lib/sun/**` (new, pure and tested).
- `apps/web/components/editor/EditorShell.tsx`, only for the "Apresentação" button alongside the modes and the sun panel over the scene.
- `apps/web/components/editor/properties/**`, for the location and north field in an existing "Local" (Site) section, or a new one.
- `apps/web/package.json`, only to add `@react-three/postprocessing` and `postprocessing` with pinned versions at least two weeks old, and `pnpm-lock.yaml`.

**Do not change:** `packages/**`, `lib/store/**`. No HDRI or external file: the lighting is procedural (drei `Sky`, `Environment` with `Lightformer`, without `preset`, which downloads files).

## 1. Sun (`lib/sun/position.ts`, pure)
- `sunPosition(date, minutes, lat, lon, tzOffsetHours)` → `{ azimuthDeg, elevationDeg }`. Use the NOAA algorithm, with equation of time and declination. Azimuth measured from north, clockwise.
- `ufLocation(uf)`: capital of each UF (state) (lat, lon) and time zone. Most are UTC−3; AM, MT, MS, RO, and RR are −4; AC is −5. No daylight saving time.
- `sunTimes(date, lat, lon, tz)`: sunrise and sunset in minutes, to bound the control.
- `sunVector(az, el, northDeg)`: vector in the engine frame (z up), rotating the azimuth by the plan's `northDeg`.
- Tests: São Paulo at solar noon on the December solstice (sun almost overhead, slightly to the south). June, at noon (sun to the north, ~43° elevation). Plausible sunrise and sunset (±15 min from public tables).

## 2. Presentation scene (`components/scene/PresentationLights.tsx` and adjustments to the Viewport/Canvas)
With `viewMode === 'presentation'`:
- Finished look with textures, no grid, no labels, dimensions, or gizmos.
- `DirectionalLight` in the sun's direction, with PCFSoft shadows. The `shadow camera` wraps the model's `bbox` with some margin, with bias tuned to avoid acne. Intensity and color vary with elevation: orange near the horizon, white at noon. At night, only a faint bluish light remains.
- drei `<Sky sunPosition>` and `<Environment>` with simple `Lightformer`s (do not use `preset`). Soft `HemisphereLight`.
- ACES or AgX tone mapping, `SRGBColorSpace`, and tuned exposure.
- Light post-processing with `@react-three/postprocessing`: N8AO (ambient occlusion) and SMAA. Turn it off if FPS drops (measure; drei's `PerformanceMonitor`).
- Ground: a large plane with a soft grass color and procedural texture, receiving shadows. With sloped terrain, use the existing `TerrainView`.
- Optional interior light (toggle "Luzes acesas" (Lights on)): one warm `PointLight` per resolved room (`results.model.rooms`), at the centroid at 2.2 m. Useful at dusk.

## 3. Sun panel (over the scene, bottom corner)
- Date (date input), time (slider from sunrise to sunset, with a label such as "15:30"), a ▶ button to animate the day, and shortcuts "Manhã" (Morning), "Meio-dia" (Noon), "Tarde" (Afternoon).
- Shows azimuth and elevation and a mini compass with the plan's north.
- **Andar por dentro** (Walk inside) (walk) and **Salvar imagem** (Save image) buttons.

## 4. First person
- `walk === true`: drei `PointerLockControls`, WASD and arrow keys, eye at 1.60 m above the active level's floor, speed 1.4 m/s (Shift runs). Esc exits.
- Starts at the entry door (first door in `ext-front`, 1 m inside) or at the center.
- Simple collision: do not pass through walls. Do a segment test against `model.walls` in plan view, with a 25 cm radius, and slide along the wall.

## 5. Save image
- Renders a frame at 2× the current resolution (or 3840 px wide at most) and downloads a PNG `project-name-YYYY-MM-DD-HHhMM.png` through the browser (link with `download`). Needs `preserveDrawingBuffer` only during the capture, or rendering to a `WebGLRenderTarget`.

## 6. Location and north (properties panel)
- City: the coordinates of the UF's capital by default. Option to type latitude and longitude.
- North: dial or slider from 0 to 360° with a compass preview. Text explaining that 180° means the front faces north.
