# F9-W1: App — performance in the editor (thermal, solar, rain) and solar panels in 3D

**Goal:** the user sees whether the chalet meets the NBR 15575 thermal performance in their bioclimatic zone, turns on solar energy and rainwater harvesting, and sees how much they cost and how much they save. With solar energy on, the panels appear on the roof, in 3D and in the presentation.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `project.performance` (`zone?`, `solar`, `rainwater`).
- `results.performance` (`PerformanceReport`: `zone`, `zoneSource`, `thermal[]` with U, CT, α, and limits, `solar`, and `rain`). It stays `null` until F9-E1 lands, in parallel; test with a hand-built report.
- `catalog.constants.performance` (zone by UF, limits, and HSP).

**May change:**
- `apps/web/components/editor/properties/**`.

## 1. Panel (`properties/PerformanceSection.tsx`)
- **Desempenho** (Performance) section, near Installations:
  - **Zona bioclimática** (Bioclimatic zone): Select from 1 to 8 with "Automática (zona X da capital de <UF>)" (Automatic (zone X of the capital of <UF>)). Explain that in the mountains the zone is usually 1 to 3.
  - **Energia solar** (Solar energy): switch, with a one-line summary (kWp, modules, cost, payback).
  - **Água de chuva** (Rainwater): switch, with a summary (liters/year and reservoir).

## 2. Desempenho tab (`components/results/PerformanceTab.tsx`, `Leaf` or `ThermometerSun` icon)
- **Thermal:**
  - one card per element (wall, roof) with U and CT against the limit, in bars or gauges with green/amber/red, and α;
  - short text: what to do when it fails (more insulation, light color, lining);
  - fixed notice: "Procedimento simplificado da NBR 15575. Sistemas leves costumam precisar de simulação computacional."
- **Solar:** estimated consumption, kWp, modules, area and whether it fits on the roof, annual generation, cost (range), savings/year, and payback (years). A simple bar chart of monthly generation is optional; if the engine does not provide month-by-month, do not make it up.
- **Rain:** catchment area, liters/year, non-potable demand, reservoir, and % served.
- No report (old engine): discreet empty state.

## 3. 3D
- **Solar panels:** with `results.performance.solar` and `roofPlaneId`, draw the modules on the indicated roof plane, over the roof panel (5 cm offset):
  - grid of 1.13 × 2.28 m rectangles (the `moduleArea` area), in centered rows with a 30 cm margin from the edges;
  - dark blue material with reflection (metalness 0.3, roughness 0.25) and a thin silver frame;
  - instancing;
  - if they do not all fit, draw the ones that fit and flag the rest in the panel.
  - They appear in Finished and Presentation.
- **Rain reservoir:** with `rain`, a dark green cylindrical tank beside the house (at the back, 1 m from the back wall), with proportional volume.

## 4. PDF
- "Desempenho" section: thermal table (element, U, limit, CT, limit, status) and, if present, the solar and rain summaries.
