# F13-W2: App — ready-made views, batch photos, and 360° video in the presentation

**Goal:** showing the chalet to family, an engineer, or a client becomes easy:
- ready-made, attractive camera views (framed automatically);
- user-saved views;
- a button that generates photos of the main views at once;
- a short video orbiting the house (360°), downloaded by the browser.

**May change:**
- `apps/web/components/scene/**` and `apps/web/lib/three/**`.
- `apps/web/components/scene/SunPanel.tsx`.
- Tests alongside.

**Do not change:** `packages/**`, `lib/store/**`, `package.json`, `lib/project/**`, and `components/wizard/**` (from F13-W1, in parallel). Do not add dependencies: use native `MediaRecorder` + `canvas.captureStream()` (WebM).

## 1. Ready-made views (`lib/three/viewpoints.ts`, pure)
- `autoViewpoints(model)` → camera positions and targets in y-up (use `convert.ts`):
  - **Front:** front of the house, eye height of 1.6 m, distance to fit the facade with a 15% margin by FOV.
  - **Corner:** front-right 3/4, slightly elevated; the "cover".
  - **Rear:** rear-left 3/4.
  - **Top:** aerial view at 45°.
  - **Interior:** from the center of the largest ground-floor room, looking toward the glass facade or the largest window, at 1.6 m.
- The framing depends on the bbox and the FOV. Test the pure function: the projected bbox fits on screen.

## 2. Interface (in the sun panel, "Câmera" (Camera) section)
- Buttons for the ready-made views (small icons + name). Clicking does a smooth 0.8 s transition.
- **Saved views:**
  - "Salvar esta vista" (Save this view) asks for a name and stores it in a per-project `localStorage` (try/catch). It does not change the project JSON or the schema.
  - The list has rename and delete.
- Shortcuts `1` to `5` for the ready-made views, only in Presentation mode and outside text fields.

## 3. Batch photos
- "Salvar 4 fotos" (Save 4 photos): goes through Front, Corner, Rear, and Interior, waits for stabilization (2 frames + textures ready), captures with the "Salvar imagem" (Save image) routine (`lib/three/capture.ts`, 2×) and downloads the images.
  - In a single `.zip` if that is easy without a dependency. Otherwise, 4 PNGs in sequence, which browsers ask about once.
  - Names: `projeto-quina-AAAA-MM-DD.png`.
- Progress indicator and cancel.

## 4. 360° video
- "Gravar vídeo 360°" (Record 360° video): the camera orbits the house in 12 s (one turn), at the Corner height, with smooth acceleration at the start and end. Records the canvas at 30 fps with `MediaRecorder` (`video/webm;codecs=vp9`, with fallback to vp8) and downloads `projeto-360.webm`.
- Option "Do nascer ao pôr do sol" (From sunrise to sunset) (timelapse): fixed camera at the Corner and the sun from sunrise to sunset in 10 s, using the existing `setSun`.
- During recording: hide the panels over the scene, lock interaction, and use High quality (return to the previous one at the end).
- Without `MediaRecorder` support: the button is disabled with a hint.

## Acceptance tests
- `viewpoints.ts`: framing and the interior position inside the room.
- Orbit: the pure camera-by-time function (with easing) starts and ends at the same position.
- File names.
- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/web typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, and `build` green (run separately).
- If you can, verify on the dev server with Playwright, even if recording does not work there: the buttons and the views.
- In the return: how to verify and what was left out.
