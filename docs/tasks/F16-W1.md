# F16-W1: App — electrical and lighting drawn on the floor plan

The engine (F16-E1, on main) computes the electrical design over the **effective layout** and returns `results.installations.electrical` (`ElectricalReport`):
- `layout` with points, conduits, circuits, and board;
- sized circuits (cross-section, breaker, RCD, voltage drop);
- conduits with fill;
- lighting design per room;
- board and demand;
- `elec.*` warnings with `refs`.

The project stores the editable layout in `project.installations.electrical`: with `manual: true` it is the source of truth; otherwise the engine generates it. Read the schema (`ElecPoint`, `Conduit`, `CircuitDef`), the result types, and `docs/research/eletrica.md` (sections 3.5, 5, 6, and 7: heights, lighting, UX, and NBR 5444 symbols).

The user wants the engineer to **draw in the floor plan view** (from above), **easily and intuitively**, as in architecture programs, and everything to follow Brazilian standards.

## Deliver
1. **"Elétrica" (Electrical) layer on the floor plan**, enabled by a button or tab "Instalações → Elétrica" (Installations → Electrical) in the floor plan bar:
   - NBR 5444 symbols for low, mid, and high outlets, TUE, single and three-way switch, ceiling light point and wall sconce, and board;
   - color per circuit;
   - conduits as lines (dashed in the floor, continuous in the ceiling cavity) with DN and number of circuits in the middle;
   - switch → light connections with the control letter (a, b, c);
   - labels with the circuit number next to each point.
   - Draw from `report.electrical.layout`, which applies to both the generated and the manual layout.
2. **Electrical palette** (appears with the layer):
   - Outlet, High outlet, Dedicated-use outlet (chooses the appliance from the catalog), Switch (single/three-way), Light (chooses the luminaire from the catalog), Board, Conduit.
   - **Snap to the wall face** with the type's default height (catalog `heights`).
   - Click **inside a room** with "Luz" (Light) puts it on the ceiling.
   - With Shift, "Luz" makes an **automatic grid** in the room, by the lumen method, until reaching the target lux.
3. **Edit by hand:**
   - on the first edit, if `manual` is false, copy `report.electrical.layout` to `project.installations.electrical` with `manual: true` and a note ("Você passou a editar o projeto elétrico; 'Gerar de novo' refaz tudo automaticamente" (You started editing the electrical design; 'Generate again' redoes everything automatically));
   - **"Gerar automaticamente"** (Generate automatically) button goes back to `manual: false`, with confirmation;
   - drag points (keeps the snap to the wall);
   - Delete removes.
4. **Control:** with a switch selected, clicking the light points turns them on and off (edits `controls`). Show the control lines.
5. **Conduit:**
   - polyline tool (ceiling cavity or floor) with snap to points, board, and other conduits;
   - **"Rotear automaticamente"** (Route automatically) deletes the conduits and uses those of the generated layout.
6. **Properties** of the selected point: type, height, power (with the code default shown), appliance, luminaire and quantity, circuit (list with "novo circuito" (new circuit)). For the circuit: name, type, overrides of cross-section, breaker, curve, and RCD (DR).
7. **Results → "Elétrica" tab:**
   - **load schedule** (circuit, type, VA, A, cross-section, breaker, RCD, drop %, length), with the failing items highlighted in red;
   - board summary: modules, spares, main, RCDs, SPD, demand, service entrance standard, and feeder;
   - **lighting per room** (average lux against the target, with a bar);
   - list of conduits with fill;
   - `elec.*` warnings with "Mostrar na planta" (Show on the floor plan) (uses `refs`).
   - Explain each term with the existing glossary (`Term`) and keep the `DISCLAIMER`.
8. **Light in 3D (Presentation and Finished):**
   - each light point becomes a visible luminaire (ceiling light, spot, pendant, or simple sconce) that turns on with "Luzes acesas" (Lights on) and at night;
   - three.js light source: intensity in candela = lm/(4π) for point and lm/π for spot, with color by CCT;
   - limit real lights (for example, the 12 closest to the camera), and the rest emissive only, so FPS does not drop.
9. **PDF:** the installations page gains the load schedule and the electrical floor plan (reuse `lib/plan2d`).
10. **Shortcuts:** `E` turns on the electrical layer; shortcuts table updated.
