# F12-W1: App — more realistic rendering (procedural PBR, sprite vegetation, landscape, glass, and light)

**Goal:** Presentation mode gets close to a photo, without downloading any file. Everything is generated in code:
- PBR textures (color, normal, and roughness) for all materials;
- vegetation with sprites (trees, shrubs, and grass tufts, in billboards and crossed planes with instancing);
- surrounding landscape (rolling terrain in the distance, a path to the door, a horizon with hills and trees);
- physical glass with reflection;
- interior lights with glow (bloom) and contact shadows.

Performance stays under control through the Auto/High/Low qualities that already exist.

**May change:**
- `apps/web/components/scene/**` and `apps/web/lib/three/**`.
- `apps/web/components/scene/SunPanel.tsx`, for a "Cenário" (Scenery) selector ("Campo" (Countryside), "Serra" (Highlands), "Praia" (Beach), "Sem cenário" (No scenery)) and the "Vegetação" (Vegetation) toggle.
- Tests alongside.

**Do not change:**
- `packages/**`, `lib/store/**`, and `package.json`. Use the dependencies that are already installed: three, drei, `@react-three/postprocessing`, and `postprocessing`. Do not download files: no external HDRI, GLB, or JPG.
- `components/editor/**`, `components/results/**`, `components/wizard/**`, and `lib/project/**`.

## 1. Procedural PBR (`lib/three/pbr.ts` + `textures.ts`)
- For each finish material already described in `describeTexture` (wood, porcelain tile, ceramic, vinyl, laminate, brick veneer, siding, paint/texture, cladding), generate three canvases from the same pattern:
  - **albedo**, the existing one, improved: tone variation per plank/piece, knots and grain in the wood, light dirt in the joints;
  - **normal**, derived from a height map (sunken joints and grout, wood grain, siding groove) by finite differences (Sobel);
  - **roughness**: porcelain tile smoother, wood medium, brick rough.
- Use `MeshStandardMaterial` with `map`, `normalMap` (moderate `normalScale`), and `roughnessMap`, with the renderer's maximum anisotropy.
- Resolution by quality: High = 1024 px per tile, Low = 256 px.
- Memoize by (material, quality) and dispose on unmount.
- Also in procedural PBR:
  - sandwich roof panel (trapezoidal ribs in the normal, slightly shiny metal);
  - concrete of the radier (raft slab) and the deck (deck wood with boards);
  - ground grass, with macro-scale variation so it does not look repetitive (two noise scales).
- Separate the pure function that describes the pattern (heights, colors) from the one that draws. Test the pure one without DOM: the normal of a flat plane is (0.5, 0.5, 1).

## 2. Vegetation (`components/scene/Vegetation.tsx` + `lib/three/foliage.ts`)
- **Sprites drawn on canvas** (no file): 3 tree types (rounded crown, conifer/araucaria, palm), 2 shrubs, and 1 grass/tall-grass tuft.
  - Each sprite is an RGBA texture with alpha (`alphaTest`), with simple shading for volume.
- **Trees:** two crossed planes (X) with instancing, and an impostor crown that always faces the camera (billboard) for distance.
  - They cast shadows (`customDepthMaterial` with alpha, so the shadow has the crown's shape).
- **Distribution** (Poisson disk, fixed seed per project):
  - outside a clearance radius around the house (bbox + 4 m) and outside the path to the door;
  - tall grass near the edges and trees farther out;
  - density by quality.
- **Scenery:**
  - Countryside: crown trees and grass;
  - Highlands: araucarias and conifers, taller hills, and light mist;
  - Beach: palms, sandy ground nearby, and sparse grass;
  - No scenery: only the current ground.
  - Store the choice in `presentationUi`, the presentation's local state.
- Light wind: sway of the top vertices by shader (`onBeforeCompile`), turned off at Low quality.

## 3. Landscape (`components/scene/Landscape.tsx`)
- Large ground with gentle undulation away from the house (without touching the project's pad/terrain near it). The existing `TerrainView` still applies when there is sloped terrain.
- Path to the front door: a strip of gravel or flagstones with procedural texture, from the `ext-front` door to the edge.
- Horizon: ring of silhouetted hills (low geometry) with atmospheric color and light exponential `fog` matching the sky and time of day.

## 4. Materials and light
- **Glass** of the windows and the glazed facade: `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with `transmission` (High quality) or opacity with `envMap` (Low), `ior` 1.5, low roughness, and a slight greenish tint. Reflects the sky (Environment).
- **Frames** in matte black aluminum.
- Selective **bloom** (postprocessing) only on interior light sources at night ("Luzes acesas" (Lights on)) and on the low sun. Luminances above a threshold.
- `ContactShadows` under the house and furniture, at High quality.
- **Simple automatic exposure** by time: less exposure at noon and more at dusk.
- **Roof panel:** specular reflection by color (`finishes.roofColor`).
- Acabado (Finished) mode (outside Presentation) may get the normal and roughness maps, as long as it stays light. Vegetation and landscape appear only in Presentation.

## 5. Performance
- Measure with `PerformanceMonitor`. Auto drops to Low below 40 fps.
- Use instancing for everything that repeats.
- The texture budget by quality, and nothing recreated per frame.
- State in the return how many draw calls the scene has at each quality (use `gl.info.render.calls` in the browser).

- `foliage.ts`: the distribution respects the clearance around the house and the path, and is deterministic by seed.

- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/web typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, and `build` green (run separately).
