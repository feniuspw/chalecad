# F17-W2: App — engineering sheet contents (foundation, LSF structure, electrical, plumbing)

In the executive project, the professional needs the complementary pranchas (drawing sheets). This card draws the engineering contents of the sheets: each one is a `ContentRenderer` that returns `RenderedContent` in paper mm at the requested scale. The sheet, the carimbo (title block), and the PDF belong to F17-W1, which runs in parallel; here only the contents.

Read first:
- `apps/web/lib/sheets/types.ts`, the contract. Register everything in `engRenderers` in `apps/web/lib/sheets/eng/index.ts`.
- `SheetContentKind` in `packages/engine/src/schema/results.ts`.
- `docs/research/aprovacao.md`, sections 5.2, 6.1, 6.2, 7.1, 7.2, and 8.3.
- What to reuse:
  - `apps/web/lib/plan2d/*` (base plan, `svg.ts` primitives);
  - `apps/web/lib/elec/svg.ts` and `symbols.ts` (electrical symbols);
  - `apps/web/lib/plumb/*` (plumbing drawing);
  - `apps/web/lib/pdf/plumbing.ts`;
  - `results.pieces` (LSF pieces with host and position);
  - `results.structure`;
  - `results.installations.electrical` / `.plumbing` (`ElectricalReport`, `PlumbingReport`).

## Deliver (one renderer per `kind`)
Lines:
- cut or main element 0.5 mm;
- secondary 0.25 mm;
- dimension and text 0.18 mm.

Text of 2.5 mm. Each content carries `legend` with the symbols used and `notes` with the discipline's technical notes. Every discipline note cites a standard with its edition, from `catalog.constants.approval.norms`.

**Structure** contents:
- **`foundation`:**
  - radier (raft slab) outline with dimensions;
  - dashed edge beam with the section dimensioned in a detail;
  - thickness;
  - **position of the anchor bolts/anchorages**, from the anchorage pieces or at the panel ends;
  - sewage and water holes and stub-outs, from the plumbing points, when present;
  - level dimensions;
  - note with fck and cover as "a definir pelo responsável" (to be defined by the responsible professional), allowable stress and origin (`legal.soil`), thickness, and mesh.

  Scale 1:50.
- **`framing_plan`** (`target` = level): panel plan with the axis and code of each wall (P01…). Shows the studs as strokes, with spacing dimensioned in one stretch; the mezzanine floor joists with spacing and profile; the bracing (X straps or OSB) per wall. The table of profiles per element enters as a note or legend.
- **`wall_panels`:** elevation of each panel (wall): studs, tracks, headers, jamb studs, openings with dimensions, straps and blocking, from the `results.pieces` of that host. At scale 1:50, arranged in a grid, each with the code and length. Reasonable limit: when it exceeds the space, the sheet goes up a format (W1's decision). Keep each panel compact.
- **`roof_framing`:** rafters/trusses/purlins in plan, with spacing and profile, and ridge.
- **`structure_notes`:** block of general notes:
  - standards;
  - steel (grade/fy) and coating;
  - screws;
  - adopted loads (table from `structure.loads`);
  - wind (V0 and category);
  - **summary table of the checks** with the worst case per type;
  - assumptions and limitations.

**Electrical** contents:
- **`electrical_plan`** (`target` = level): base plan in a thin line, with:
  - points with the symbols from `lib/elec/symbols.ts` and the circuit number next to each point;
  - conduits;
  - switches connected to the lights by letter;
  - power of outlets and luminaires (VA);
  - board.

  Full legend of the symbols with the text "Simbologia conforme legenda (a NBR 5444 foi cancelada)" (Symbols as per legend (NBR 5444 was withdrawn)). Never write "conforme NBR 5444" (per NBR 5444).
- **`single_line`:** single-line diagram of the board: service entrance/meter, main breaker, SPD (DPS), RCD (DR) (grouping the circuits it protects), and one branch per circuit, with breaker (A and curve), cross-section (mm²), description, and power. Include neutral and earth busbars and the earthing scheme (`legal.earthing`).
- **`load_table`:** load schedule with these columns:
  - circuit;
  - description;
  - voltage;
  - power (VA);
  - current;
  - cross-section;
  - breaker;
  - RCD;
  - voltage drop.

  In the total: installed load, demand, and service entrance standard (category and main breaker), with the note "confirmar com a concessionária" (confirm with the utility (concessionária)).

**Plumbing** contents:
- **`plumbing_plan`** (`target` = level): cold and hot water, with pipes, DN, points, valves, and risers (colors allowed in the PDF, but also differentiated by line type for black-and-white printing).
- **`sewer_plan`** (`target` = level): sewage and venting, with DN, slope, arrows, boxes (siphoned, grease, inspection), and outlet to the septic tank or network.
- **`water_isometric`:** isometric of the cold water from the tank, in 30° projection, with DN per run and point elevations.
- **`tank_detail`:** water tank detail: volume, bottom elevation, outlets, overflow, cleanout, and support. The tower is included when present.
- **`septic`:** septic tank and final disposal in plan and schematic section, with volume, dimensions, and minimum distances (those of NBR 7229, with the note "conferir pela NBR 17076:2024" (check against NBR 17076:2024)).

Content without data (no installations, no septic tank) returns `null`.

## Tests
- Each renderer on the default example with installations turned on returns `width`/`height` > 0, non-empty `body`, and the right scale.
- The single-line diagram has one branch per circuit and the RCD groups the right circuits.
- The load schedule sums equal to the report.
- The radier dimensions match the model.
- The text "conforme NBR 5444" does not appear.
