# F18-T: Terrain — flattened areas (pads) drawn on the terrain and suggested retaining walls

The user wants to "select an area to flatten around the terrain and have the program already suggest a retaining wall (muro de arrimo) when needed."

## Today
- **Engine** (`packages/engine/src/terrain/index.ts`):
  - a single rectangular plateau (footprint + `platformMargin`) over a natural plane;
  - cut/fill slopes all around (`finalZ` limited by the natural terrain, Euclidean distance to the rectangle);
  - volumes by 0.25 m grid, `auto` by bisection, 0.5 m mesh;
  - drain and geotextile on the 4 edges; `terrain.*` warnings.
- **Retaining wall:** it is a manual extra (`RetainingExtra`), unrelated to the terrain: it does not change the mesh or the volumes, and has a single height.
- **Duplicated logic:**
  - `apps/web/lib/plan2d/section.ts` (approx. lines 85–110) and `apps/web/lib/three/slopedSite.ts` copy `finalZ` and the rectangle;
  - `apps/web/components/scene/EditorScene.tsx` (182–209) draws the plateau outline (`plateauRect` in `apps/web/lib/three/terrain.ts`);
  - `approval/urban.ts` has its own natural plane.
- **UI:** `apps/web/components/editor/properties/TerrainSection.tsx` has slope, margins, auto/manual elevation, and summary. There is no plateau editing in the viewport.

## Contracts (already on main, do not change schemas)
**`SlopedSite`** (`packages/engine/src/schema/project.ts`) gains:
- `pads: SitePad[]`, each `{ id, name, polygon, level, edges }`. `level` is relative to the house's finished floor: 0 = the house plateau level.
- `edges` (`auto|slope|wall`) for the house plateau.
- `retaining: { type: 'auto'|'gabion'|'concrete'|'block', drainage }`.

**`TerrainReport`** (`results.ts`) gains:
- `pads: TerrainPadReport[]`, with the house plateau as `id: 'house'`;
- `walls: TerrainWall[]`, each with `path`, `heights` per vertex, `side` cut/fill, `type`, `topZ`, `length`, `area`, `maxHeight`, and `reason`.

The current stub returns only the 'house' pad and `walls: []`.

**`catalog.constants.terrainRules`** gains `retainingCutHeight` (1.5), `retainingFillHeight` (1.0), `retainingMinLength`, `retainingBlockMaxHeight` (2), and `retainingConcreteMaxHeight` (4). The `retaining` table (quantities per type) already existed.

**Standard in the catalog:** `nbr11682` (slope stability, `confirmed:false`).

**Surface:** `packages/engine/src/terrain/surface.ts` defines `SiteSurface`, with `groundZ`/`naturalZ` in house coordinates (finished floor = 0, ground 3 cm below). `computeTerrain` already returns `surface` for the rectangle. The deck (card F18-D, in parallel) consumes this interface to size the posts, so keep the interface and make the surface reflect pads and walls.

## Deliver

### Engine (`packages/engine/src/terrain/**`)
1. **Pads.**
   - The house plateau (current rectangle, `id 'house'`, elevation `zp`) plus each `SitePad`, at elevation `zp + pad.level`.
   - The final surface is the natural terrain with each pad imposed.
   - Outside the pads, use slopes from the edge of each pad (distance to the polygon, not to the rectangle), with the same cut/fill rule as today.
   - Where the zones of influence of two pads cross, use a coherent rule: the nearest pad wins, and the slopes between pads of different levels meet.
   - Pad inside pad: the most recent wins.
   - The `auto` of `platformElevation` balances the volume of all pads together.
   - Point-in-polygon and distance to polygon go in engine helpers (there is already geometry in `packages/engine/src/geometry`).
2. **Automatic retaining walls.**
   - Walk the edges of each pad and sample the height `h(s) = natural − z_pad` (cut, if > 0) or `z_pad − natural` (fill).
   - With `edges: 'auto'`, a stretch becomes a wall where:
     - `h > retainingCutHeight` (cut) or `h > retainingFillHeight` (fill); **or**
     - the slope would leave the lot. With `project.legal.lot` present, transform the lot to the project coordinates with `buildingOffset`/`rotationDeg`, as in `approval/urban.ts` and `apps/web/components/scene/tools/lot/*`; extract the helper if needed; **or**
     - the slope would invade the house/radier or another pad.
   - With `edges: 'wall'`, the whole edge is a wall (`reason: 'forced'`); with `'slope'`, none.
   - Merge neighboring stretches and discard those shorter than `retainingMinLength`, or extend to the neighbor.
   - **`auto` type:** block up to `retainingBlockMaxHeight`, concrete up to `retainingConcreteMaxHeight`, and gabion above, decided by the stretch's maximum height.
   - **Surface at the wall:** the surface is vertical (in cut, the natural ground meets the wall; in fill, the pad ends at the wall and the outside follows the natural). The mesh, the volumes, and the `surface` reflect this. A slope next to a wall must close with no hole in the mesh (vertical step by duplicated vertices or a narrow strip, your choice, but visually vertical).
3. **Quantities** (items of the `terrain` stage, `hostId` = wall id, e.g. `terrain-wall-1`):
   - integrate the face area ∫h ds and use the per-type formulas that already exist for `RetainingExtra` (`packages/engine/src/extras/index.ts`, `retaining` block);
   - extract a common pure function (for example `retainingItems(length, area, maxH, type, drainage, catalog)`) in `terrain/retaining.ts` and call it in both places; that part of `extras/index.ts` may be touched only for this;
   - drain and geotextile: behind the cut walls; on tall cut slopes, as today.
4. **Warnings:**
   - `terrain.retaining_suggested` (info), listing the stretches;
   - `terrain.retaining_tall` (warn), wall above `cutWarnHeight`, with "projeto geotécnico, NBR 11682" (geotechnical design, NBR 11682);
   - `terrain.slope_outside_lot`, when `edges: 'slope'` and the slope leaves the lot;
   - `terrain.pad_*` for invalid polygon or self-intersection.
5. **Approval:** item `terrain.retaining` in `approval/compliance.ts`, with the standard `nbr11682` and maximum height and type in the value. It enters as `manual` (geotechnical design) when there are walls.
6. **Performance:** `computeAll` of the example with 2 pads in under 60 ms on the terrain (a test already exists for 60 ms total; keep it). Use the same grid and avoid expensive O(n·m): precompute the edges.

### Web
1. **"Aplainar área" (Flatten area) tool** (`apps/web/components/scene/tools/terrain/**`, new).
   - Available only on sloped terrain. Button in `TerrainSection`, key `G` if free: check `drawKeys.ts`/shortcuts and the shortcuts glossary.
   - Drawing on the plane by rectangle (drag) or polygon (clicks, Enter closes).
   - Snap to the corners and faces of the house, the plateau, and the lot; show the dimensions.
   - On creation, the pad enters with `level` 0 and `edges: 'auto'`.
   - **Editing:** drag vertices and the whole pad, Delete removes, and the level changes by a field in the inspector and by vertical drag with Shift.
   - Show live the outline with the elevation and, on the edges, the color of what will happen (slope or wall, with height).
   - Follow the pattern of the existing tools (`ToolLayer`, `pointer.ts`, `PathDrawLayer`, `components/scene/tools/lot/*`) and the tools store.
2. **`TerrainSection.tsx`:**
   - list of pads (name, level, area, edges Auto/Slope/Wall, delete);
   - edges of the house plateau;
   - type and drainage of the generated walls;
   - summary of the suggested walls (length, maximum height, type, cost, if the budget exposes the item).
   - Clicking a wall focuses the camera on it (if there is a focus helper; otherwise, just selects).
3. **3D:**
   - `TerrainView.tsx` shows the mesh with the steps.
   - New component `TerrainWallsView.tsx` draws the walls from `terrain.walls` (block, concrete, or gabion, with the material of the existing `retaining` in `ExtrasView` as reference), with top and height varying per vertex and a coping.
   - The pads' outline replaces `plateauRect` (use `terrain.pads`).
   - Mount the component in `EditorScene.tsx`: touch only the terrain block.
   - Presentation: `apps/web/lib/three/slopedSite.ts`, `landscapeData.ts`, and `presentationPrep.ts` must use the engine's surface/mesh (no duplicated `finalZ`) and not plant vegetation over pads/walls.
4. **2D:** in `apps/web/lib/plan2d/section.ts`, the terrain part of the section comes from the engine: sample the mesh or export a pure `terrainProfile(project, results, line)` function from the engine. Draw the walls in the section and in the site plan (`apps/web/lib/sheets/arch/lot.ts`, `section.ts`, `elevation.ts`: only the terrain). The extras/deck part of `plan2d/section.ts` belongs to F18-D: do not touch it.
5. **Glossary:** pad/plateau (platô), retaining wall (muro de arrimo), slope (talude), cut/fill (corte/aterro) (`apps/web/lib/glossary/terms.ts`).

## Minimum tests
- **Compatibility:** without pads and with `edges: 'slope'`, volumes and mesh equal to today's (compare with the current numbers in `terrain.test.ts`).
- **Rectangular pad attached to the front of the house, 1 m lower:** the mesh has the right elevation inside the pad, and the volume matches the hand calculation in a simple case (isolated pad with slope 0 and `level` −1, excavation = area × 1).
- **30% terrain** and plateau with a 4 m rear margin:
  - in `auto`, a cut wall appears at the rear with `maxHeight > 1.5`;
  - in `slope`, no wall;
  - in `wall`, wall all around (`reason: 'forced'`).
- **Narrow lot:** the slope would leave the lot, so a wall appears with `reason: 'lot'`.
- **Auto type by height;** wall quantities = same function as `RetainingExtra` with the integrated area.
- **`surface.groundZ`:** inside the pad gives the pad's elevation, and behind the cut wall gives the natural one.
- **Web:** tool helpers (snap, rectangle → polygon, validation), `TerrainWallsView` without NaN, 2D section using the engine's profile.

When done: `pnpm typecheck`, engine and web tests green, one commit `F18-T: …` on branch `card/F18-T`.
