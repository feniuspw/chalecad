# F5-E1: Engine — rooms, per-room checks, and finish quantities

**Goal:** resolve the rooms (`project.rooms`) into polygons from the walls, check code compliance per room (ceiling height, lighting/ventilation, minimum area), mark wet walls, and cost the chosen finishes (`room.floor`, `room.walls`, `finishes.facade`, `finishes.interiorRoof`).

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):** `Room`, `RoomUse`, `Finishes` in `packages/engine/src/schema/project.ts`; `RoomModel`, `RoomEdge`, `BuildingModel.rooms` in `schema/results.ts`; catalog: `finish_*` materials and `constants.norms.rooms` (ceiling height, `light`, `vent`, `wet`, `minArea` per use).

**May change:** `packages/engine/src/model/rooms.ts` (new), `packages/engine/src/model/index.ts`, `packages/engine/src/quantities/**`, `packages/engine/src/warnings/**`, `packages/engine/src/reports/**`, `packages/engine/src/index.ts` (export only), `packages/engine/test/**`.
**Do not change:** `packages/engine/src/schema/**`, `packages/catalog/**`, `apps/**`, `package.json`.

## 1. Resolve rooms (`model/rooms.ts`)
- Boundaries per level:
  - Ground floor: outline on the axis of the exterior walls. Use the axis polygon: rectangle `W × D`, the L/T polygon (`combinedGeometry(t).axisPolygon`), or the A-frame one with annexes (the `annexAxisPolygon` function in `model/index.ts`; export it or move it to a util).
  - Mezzanine (`level.polygon`): the slab polygon itself.
  - Add the user's interior walls for that level (`project.walls` with `level`).
- Planar graph: segments = outline edges + axes of the interior walls.
  - Merge points closer than 5 cm.
  - Split segments at crossings and T-junctions.
  - Interior walls that end within 15 cm of another wall are extended up to it (drawing tolerance).
  - A dangling end (a wall that does not close anything) is ignored when forming faces.
- Enumerate the interior faces. The room's face is the smallest one that contains `seed`; the polygon is output counterclockwise, with no collinear vertices. Outside any closed face: `polygon: null`, area 0, and a `room.open` warning.
- `edges`: each polygon edge with the `hostId` of the wall under it.
  - Exterior walls: the generated IDs (`ext-*`, `wing-*`, `annex-*`, including `-2` segments). Use the distance to the axis in `model.walls`.
  - Interior walls: the user's ID.
  - No wall: `null`. This is the case for the mezzanine edge and for the A-frame stretch without a low wall, where the "wall" is the roof plane.
- `ceiling`: sample the clear height on a ~25 cm grid inside the polygon.
  - Ceiling = the lower of the underside of a slab above, when the point is inside it (`topZ - thickness`), and the roof elevation at the point (lowest roof plane over the point, as in `warnings/norms.ts`).
  - Clear height = ceiling − level elevation.
  - Return min, weighted mean, and max.
- `glazing` and `openingIds`: include the openings in walls whose stretch lies on the outline (opening position along the host within the edge's stretch), the skylights over the polygon, and the glazed facade area (`gable_glazed`) on the outline stretch. Windows, sliding doors, and skylights count; blind doors do not.
- `wet = constants.norms.rooms[use].wet`; `name` = `room.name` or the pt-BR label of the use (Sala, Cozinha, Quarto, Banheiro, Lavanderia, Circulação, Depósito, Outro).
- Build `model.rooms` in `buildModel`, currently an empty stub.
- **Wet walls:** a user interior wall that has an edge of a wet room and no explicit `assembly` now uses `system.assemblies.wet` (`wet: true` in HostWall). To do this, resolve the rooms with the project data before building the interior walls.

## 2. Per-room warnings (`warnings/norms.ts`)
- Ceiling height: `ceiling.avg` < rule for the use → warn (below 2.30 m) or info. Sloped ceiling: cite min and mean.
- Lighting and ventilation: `light` → glazing ≥ area/6 and ventilation 1/12. `vent` only → ventilation 1/12, or "exaustão mecânica" (mechanical exhaust) (info).
- Minimum area: area < `minArea` → info.
- When the ground level has rooms: the volume-based lighting warning (current `norm.lighting`) only covers the part of the volume's area outside the rooms. If the sum of the room areas is ≥ 90% of the volume, do not emit the volume-based warning.
- Fix the text of the warning under the mezzanine: NBR 15575 requires 2.50 m for the kitchen as well. The 2.30 m applies to bathroom, laundry, circulation, and storage.
- Codes: `room.ceiling`, `room.lighting`, `room.ventilation`, `room.area`, `room.open`, with `refs: [room.id]`.

## 3. Finish quantities (`quantities/items.ts`)
Stage `finishing`, `hostId` = room or wall.
- Floor: `room.floor` × room area.
- Walls: `room.walls` × Σ (length of the edge with a wall `hostId` × average clear height of the room, capped at the wall's top height along that stretch) − openings in the stretch.
- Facade: `finishes.facade` × net area of the exterior walls (excluding `gable_glazed`; includes the annex walls and low walls).
- Interior face of the roof planes: `finishes.interiorRoof` × area of the `isWall` roof planes (A-frame) minus openings.
- No choice made: nothing is costed (old projects do not change).

## Acceptance tests
- 6 × 8 rectangle with an interior wall at y = 5: two rooms, one on each side (areas 30 and 18 on the axis).
- T-shaped wall closing a 2 × 2 bathroom in the corner.
- A wall that stops 10 cm from the exterior one still closes the room.
- `seed` outside a closed face gives `polygon` null and the `room.open` warning.
- Mezzanine: a room using the slab edge (edge with `hostId` null).
- A-frame with annex: a room inside the annex.
- Bathroom: the interior wall becomes wet.
- Bedroom without a window: lighting warning. Kitchen with enough window: no warning.
- Finishes: vinyl flooring in a 3 × 3 bedroom gives 9 m² net. Wall paint = perimeter × height − openings. Facade and lambri (tongue-and-groove wood) lining of the A-frame.
- Project with no rooms or finishes: snapshot unchanged (`test/snapshot`). Run `vitest -u` only if the change is intentional and explain why.
- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/engine typecheck test lint` green; `computeAll` of the fixture stays under 300 ms.
