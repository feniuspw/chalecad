**Goal:** the `water_tower` extra becomes a real volume: its own structure, enclosure, roof, the water tank inside, quantities, and verification. Installations now use the tower's tank and check the minimum pressure at the mezzanine water points (NBR 5626).

## 1. Geometry (`extras/water_tower.ts`)
- **Outline:** `width × depth` rectangle centered on `position`.
- **Walls (`segments`):** the four faces, with `baseZ` = elevation of the chalet's roof at that point (the lowest roof plane over the outline; reuse the roof-elevation function used by the rooms/alerts), up to the top = `platformZ` + tank height + 0.3 m. Material `board`.
  - If the tower is outside the roof (beside the house), `baseZ` = 0.
- **Posts (`posts`):** 4 corner studs, from the ground-floor slab (or the mezzanine slab, if the outline falls over it) up to the top.
- **Roof (`roofs`):** gable (`gable`), shed (`shed`), or flat (`flat`, with 3% slope), with a 0.2 m eave. Use `RoofPlane` the way the covered deck does.
- **Tank (`tank`):** centered cylinder. The radius and height come from the volume (usual proportion: height ≈ 0.75 × diameter), with `baseZ` = `platformZ`. `liters` = `tankLiters` ?? `results.installations.tankLiters` (if already computed in the compute order) ?? 1000.

## 2. Installations (`installations/*`)
- With a tower in the project, the water tank goes in it: the `tank` point sits at the tower position, and the cold water pipes start from it.
- **Pressure (NBR 5626, simplified):** for each `water_cold`/`water_hot` point (shower at 2.10 m, others at 1.0 m above the level's floor), compute head difference = (tank bottom) − (point elevation).
  - Shower: head difference < 1.0 m raises the `install.pressure` alert (warn): "Chuveiro do mezanino com só 0,4 m de coluna d'água: eleve a caixa (torre) ou use pressurizador" (Mezzanine shower with only 0.4 m of water column: raise the tank (tower) or use a booster pump).
  - Other points: limit of 0.5 m.
  - Without a tower, the tank sits in the ceiling, 0.3 m below the ridge above the bathroom (where it is already positioned); use that elevation in the calculation.
  - The alert must fire in the typical case of a bathroom on the mezzanine without a tower.
- With a tower, the full-tank load goes out in `install.tank_load`, citing the tower.

## 3. Quantities
Stage `extras`, `hostId` = tower id:
- corner studs and tracks (profile `system.profiles.wallStud`, or wood in `wood`);
- platform (dry slab: 18 mm OSB over `joist` profiles; use the existing materials);
- exterior enclosure (the exterior assembly: boards + membrane + facade finish, if any), over the wall area above the roof;
- roof (the same panel as the chalet), flashings where it meets the roof (perimeter), and ridge;
- the tower's water tank, only if installations are turned off; with them on, the tank is already budgeted. Do not count twice.

## 4. Structure
- Simple verification of the platform beam: two `joist` profiles in a box section, span = shorter side, load = full tank / 2 + self weight.
- Use the existing capacity functions (steel or wood, depending on the system). Check with the existing `kind` (`joist`) and `detail` "Plataforma da caixa d'água" (Water tank platform).

## 5. Engineer package
- Question: "Torre da caixa d'água: confirmar a estrutura de apoio (X kg cheia a Y m) e a vedação no encontro com o telhado" (Water tank tower: confirm the supporting structure (X kg full at Y m) and the sealing where it meets the roof).

## Acceptance tests
- A-frame 6 × 9 with a 1.6 × 1.6 tower over the rear bathroom:
  - the walls start at the roof elevation at that point and not at 0;
  - there are 4 posts;
  - there are two roof planes (gable);
  - the tank sits at `platformZ`.
- Bathroom on the mezzanine (2.6 m) without a tower: `install.pressure`. With a tower at 5.0 m: no alert (5.0 − (2.6 + 2.1) = 0.3? Adjust: the test uses `platformZ` 6.0 → 1.3 m ≥ 1.0, no alert).
- Tower quantities in the `extras` stage; the tank is not doubled when installations are on.
- Projects without a tower stay unchanged (snapshot).
