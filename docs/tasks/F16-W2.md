# F16-W2: App — plumbing drawn on the floor plan (cold and hot water, sewage, venting)

The engine (F16-E2, on main) computes the plumbing over the **effective layout** and returns `results.installations.plumbing` (`PlumbingReport`):
- `layout` with tank, points, and pipes;
- result per pipe (flow, DN, velocity, loss);
- dynamic pressure per point, with `requiredTankRise`;
- sewage (UHC, DN, slope, elevations) and septic tank;
- `water.*` and `sewer.*` warnings with `refs`.

The project stores the editable layout in `project.installations.plumbing`: with `manual: true` it is the source of truth. Read the schema (`WaterPoint`, `Pipe`, `Plumbing`), the result types, and `docs/research/hidraulica.md` (sections 6, 7, and 8: UX, colors, symbols, and heights).

The user wants to **draw in the floor plan view**, easily, and see whether **the pressure arrives**: whether the tank's position and height are adequate, together with the pipe diameters.

## Deliver
1. **"Hidráulica" (Plumbing) layer on the floor plan**, enabled by a button or tab "Instalações → Hidráulica" (Installations → Plumbing):
   - pipes by system with the catalog colors (`plumbing.colors`): cold blue, hot red, sewage brown, venting green;
   - DN next to the pipe;
   - flow arrows on the sewage with the slope in %;
   - symbols for water tank, points (per fixture), riser (up or down: circle), siphoned drain box, grease trap, inspection box, and outlet;
   - **pressure badge** at each water point (m.w.c. and kPa), green when it complies and red when not, with "faltam X m" (X m short).
   - System filter (cold, hot, sewage, venting).
   - Draw from `report.plumbing.layout`.
2. **Palette:** Water point (chooses the fixture from the catalog; the bathroom and kitchen furniture already generate theirs), Water tank (move, and edit the bottom elevation), Cold water pipe, Hot water pipe, Sewage pipe, Venting.
3. **Pipe:**
   - polyline with snap to points, tank, and endpoints or stretches of other pipes (split the stretch at the junction);
   - editable z elevation: the water default is the ceiling cavity (ceiling height − 0.1), going down to the point;
   - sewage sits under the floor, with automatic slope by DN (the engine's default when `slope` is absent);
   - **"Rotear automaticamente"** (Route automatically) uses the generated layout.
4. **Edit by hand:**
   - on the first edit, copy `report.plumbing.layout` to the project with `manual: true` and a note;
   - **"Gerar automaticamente"** (Generate automatically) goes back to `manual: false`, with confirmation;
   - drag the tank and the points; Delete removes.
5. **Properties:**
   - **pipe:** system, DN (automatic or locked), slope (sewage), length, flow, velocity, and loss (from the result);
   - **point:** fixture, pressure (dynamic, static, losses, minimum), and critical path highlighted on the floor plan;
   - **tank:** bottom elevation, volume, full weight, and **"Subir até atender"** (Raise until compliant), which applies the largest `requiredTankRise`.
6. **Results → "Hidráulica" tab:**
   - pressure table per point (fixture, level, m.w.c., kPa, minimum, status, required adjustment);
   - pipes (DN, flow, velocity, loss);
   - sewage (UHC, DN, slope, elevations) and venting;
   - tank (volume and reserve) and septic tank (volume);
   - warnings with "Mostrar na planta" (Show on the floor plan).
   - Use the glossary (`Term`) and keep the `DISCLAIMER`.
7. **3D (Finished, with "Mostrar instalações" (Show installations)):** pipes as cylinders with the colors per system and the real diameter (1:1 scale), replacing or complementing what `InstallationsView` does today with the points.
8. **PDF:** plumbing page with the floor plan and the pressure table.
9. **Shortcuts:** `H` turns on the plumbing layer; shortcuts table updated.
