# F1-W2: Results panel — summary, materials, budget, prices, warnings, and CSV export

**Goal:** the editor's right panel, which shows in real time what the engine calculates (`Results`, types in `packages/engine/src/schema/results.ts`) and lets the user edit prices and budget assumptions.

**May change:** `apps/web/components/results/**`, `apps/web/lib/export/**` (+ tests), `apps/web/lib/format.ts` (+ test).
**Do not change:** `apps/web/lib/store/**` (use `useEditor` and the actions of the `lib/store/types.ts` contract), `apps/web/components/scene/**`, `apps/web/components/editor/**` (except: at the end, replace the import of the old panel (`components/editor/ResultsPanel.tsx`, used in `EditorShell`) with your `components/results/ResultsPanel.tsx` and delete the old one — these are the only changes allowed outside your folder), `packages/**`, `package.json`.

## Deliverables
1. `lib/format.ts`: pt-BR formatting (`formatBRL`, `formatBRLRange(min, max)`, `formatNumber(n, digits)`, `formatArea` "52,3 m²", `formatKg` "1.234 kg" / "3,2 t", `formatLength`, `formatDate`). Tests.
2. `components/results/ResultsPanel.tsx`: panel with tabs (Radix Tabs from `@chalecad/ui`), fixed header with large, legible KPIs: **Custo total** (Total cost; smaller min–max range below), **R$/m²**, **Área construída** (Built area), **Peso** (Weight; superstructure), **Aço kg/m²** (Steel kg/m²) with a mini-bar showing the usual range (`catalog.constants.steelIndexRange`, green inside/yellow outside). Elegant loading state (skeleton) while `results` is null; subtle "recalculando" (recalculating) indicator when `computing`. Fixed ART (Anotação de Responsabilidade Técnica, the engineer's liability record) notice in the panel footer (`DISCLAIMER`).
3. **Resumo** (Summary) tab: donut chart (Recharts) of cost per stage with legend and values; stage table (material, labor, freight, total); areas (`quantities.areas`); weight per stage and top 5 heaviest elements (`weight.byElement`); piece and bar counts; computation time.
4. **Materiais** (Materials) tab: list grouped by stage (`STAGE_LABELS`, `STAGE_ORDER` from `@chalecad/catalog`) with collapsible headers and subtotal; each row: name, purchase quantity + unit, net quantity, waste %, weight, line price. Text search. **Clicking a row calls `setHighlight({ pieceIds, hostIds, source: line.id })`** (clicking again clears it); active row highlighted. For profiles, an expander shows the cutting plan (bars as proportional horizontal strips, colored cuts, and hatched leftover). Simple virtualization if there are more than 200 rows.
5. **Orçamento** (Budget) tab: stacked bars per stage (material/labor/freight) in Recharts; for each stage a "Eu faço" (I do it myself) Switch (`project.budget.diyStages` via `update`); fields for contingency (%), freight (distance in km and R$/t·km), and durations in weeks per stage (`budget.durationsWeeks`); disbursement schedule (bars per week) when durations exist; totals with range.
6. **Preços** (Prices) tab: table of the budget lines (`budget.lines`) with an inline-editable unit price (NumberInput R$); editing writes to `project.priceOverrides[priceItemId]` with today's date and source "cotação manual" (manual quote); badges "desatualizado" (outdated; `stale`), "sem preço" (no price; `missing`), "editado" (edited; `overridden`, with a restore button). **Import CSV** (file or pasted text) with columns `itemId;preco;precoMax?;data?;fonte?` (accept `,` or `;` as separator and decimal comma), preview of recognized/ignored rows, and confirm → a single `update('Importar preços')`. Export the effective price base as CSV.
7. **Alertas** (Warnings) tab (with a counter in the tab title, color by worst severity): list of `results.warnings` with an icon per severity; clicking highlights `refs` (`setHighlight({ pieceIds: [], hostIds: refs, source: code })`) and, if the ref is an opening/wall, `select`s it.
8. Export `lib/export/csv.ts`: `exportPurchaseCsv(results, project)` → CSV **per supplier**: group by supplier category derived from the catalog (LSF steel; boards and closure; concrete and foundation; roofing; window/door frames; plumbing; finishing; services) with columns item, unit, quantity, unit price, total; `exportBudgetCsv`. Separator `;` and UTF-8 BOM (opens directly in pt-BR Excel). Export buttons at the top of the panel ("Exportar" (Export) menu: CSV per supplier, budget CSV, project JSON). CSV tests.

## Visual quality
Follow the existing theme (tokens in `app/globals.css`, components from `@chalecad/ui`). Numbers with `tabular-nums`, right-aligned. Clear hierarchy: the user glances and sees cost, area, and warnings. No cramped tables: 36–40 px rows, soft hover.
