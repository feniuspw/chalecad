# F3-E1: Engine — gambrel and combined (L, T) typologies, construction guide, and engineer package

**Goal:** (a) support `gambrel` and `combined` (L/T) in `buildModel`, `generatePieces`, and quantities; (b) deterministically generate, from the results, the construction guide for self-builders and the assumptions package for the engineer (the AI only enriches the text afterward; the data comes from here).

**May change:** `packages/engine/src/model/**`, `packages/engine/src/framing/**`, `packages/engine/src/quantities/**` (only if needed for the new typologies), `packages/engine/src/reports/**` (new), `packages/engine/src/index.ts`, `packages/engine/test/**` (new files).
**Do not change:** `src/schema/**`, `src/structure/**`, `src/budget/**`, `packages/catalog/**`.

## Typologies
**gambrel**: side walls of height `wallHeight`; each side has two roof planes: lower (`lowerPitchDeg`, horizontal projection = `breakRatio` × half-width) and upper (`upperPitchDeg` up to the ridge). IDs: `roof-a` (lower, side A), `roof-a2` (upper, side A), `roof-b`, `roof-b2`. Gable ends with a 7-vertex outline. Eave on the lower plane, rake on all. Framing: broken rafters (two segments per side, `rafter` pieces), `collar_tie` at the break (or mezzanine), purlins per roof plane. Mezzanine usually at the break level.
**combined** (L/T): main volume (gable of width × depth, ridge along y) + gable wing (`wing.width` × `wing.depth`) extending from the back wall (`ext-back`), with the ridge along x, same pitch. L: wing at `x ∈ [offset, offset + wing.width]`; T: centered. The main volume's `ext-back` wall is split (the part covered by the wing disappears; it becomes a full-width passage opening — do not generate a wall there). Wing walls: `wing-left`, `wing-right`, `wing-back` (gable end). Wing roof planes `wing-roof-a`, `wing-roof-b`, trimmed where they meet the main roof (valley): the wing plane ends at the intersection with the main planes (compute the valley line; the wing plane polygon becomes a trapezoid/triangle). Quantities: valley (`flashing` as valley flashing, length of the valley lines), actual roof area.
Built area, radier (raft slab) (L/T polygon + edge) and footprint must be consistent.

## Reports (`src/reports/`)
```ts
export function buildConstructionGuide(project: Project, results: Results, catalog: Catalog): ConstructionGuide;
export function buildEngineerPackage(project: Project, results: Results, catalog: Catalog): EngineerPackage;
```
Define the types in `src/reports/types.ts` (JSON-serializable):
- `ConstructionGuide`: ordered steps (site layout and batter boards; utilities embedded in the radier; radier; wall panel assembly — one step per panel/wall in order: exterior walls starting with the front, then interior, with piece count and weight of each panel; mezzanine; roofing (rafters, purlins, roof panels); exterior cladding; utilities; interior cladding; flooring; finishes), each step with a pt-BR description, tools, materials to have on hand (IDs of the quantity lines), and estimated duration (man-hours from simple, documented indices). A "buy first" list (foundation and structure materials with typical lead times). A consolidated tools list.
- `EngineerPackage`: project summary (typology, dimensions, areas, profiles and spacings, assemblies), load assumptions (from `results.structure.loads`), table of checks with utilization ratios, points of attention (warn/fail and warnings), open questions generated by rules (e.g., "Validar vão de 6,5 m das vigas do mezanino", "Sondagem do solo: usado padrão de 100 kPa", "Vento: V0 informado 35 m/s"), ART (Anotação de Responsabilidade Técnica, the engineer's liability record) notice.

## Acceptance tests
- gambrel 6 x 8, wallHeight 2.5, 60°/25°, breakRatio 0.4: break and ridge heights checked by hand; 4 roof planes; gable end with 7 vertices.
- combined L 6 x 8 + wing 4 x 4 offset 0: built area = 48 + 16; L-shaped radier with 6 vertices (+ edge); valley line exists; `computeAll` runs.
- combined T: centered wing.
- Construction guide for the fixture: steps in order, one per exterior and interior wall, sum of pieces in the wall steps = pieces of the walls; engineer package with non-empty open questions when there is a warn/fail.
