# F8-E1: Engine — wood frame (treated timber structure)

**Goal:** with `system.structure === 'wood'`, the chalet is framed in autoclave-treated pine lumber. The profiles come from the catalog (`family: 'Wood'`, `material: 'wood'`) and the checks follow NBR 7190. The quantities come out in timber pieces, nails, and volume (m³), and the weight uses the timber's mass. LSF stays exactly the same.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `System.structure: 'lsf' | 'wood'` and `WOOD_PROFILES` (default wood frame profiles) in `schema/project.ts`.
- `QuantityReport.woodM3` and `woodM3PerM2`, currently fixed at 0 in `quantities/index.ts`.
- Catalog:
  - `Profile.material` and `Profile.woodClass`; profiles `W38x89`, `W38x140`, `W38x184`, `W38x235`, and `W25x50` (3, 4.2, and 6 m pieces, with prices);
  - `constants.wood` (density, classes C20/C25/C30 with fc0k, fvk, and E0m, kmod, γ, index range, and nails per m and per m² of board);
  - material `nail_wood` (kg).

## 1. Framing (`framing/*`)
- The piece generator serves both systems. In `wood`:
  - **Double top plate** (two overlapping `track_top` pieces), as wood frame practice requires. The bottom plate is single, over the sealing tape.
  - **Horizontal blocking** (`blocking`, if `PieceRole` already has something similar; otherwise use `cripple` with a note, without changing the schema) at mid-height between studs, in walls taller than 2.7 m.
  - **Headers:** two deep pieces (box = 2 profiles side by side).
  - **Gable end's own track:** `ownTrack` looks for a profile of the same section (`Wood` with the same `web`), not a `U`. Today `walls.ts` only looks for `family === 'U'`.
  - **Purlins/battens:** `system.profiles.purlin` (`W25x50`) in place of the hat channel.
- When mixing (steel profile in `wood`, or timber in `lsf`), emit the `system.profile_mismatch` warning (warn), citing the inconsistent `system.profiles` fields.

## 2. Structure (`structure/capacity.ts` and `index.ts`)
For a profile with `material: 'wood'`, use NBR 7190 with `constants.wood` and the profile's class:
- **Design strengths:**
  - fc0,d = kmod × fc0k / γc;
  - fv,d = kmod × fvk / γv;
  - E0,ef = kmod × E0m.
- **Compression with buckling** (studs):
  - λ = L0 / i;
  - λ ≤ 40: Nrd = A × fc0,d;
  - 40 < λ ≤ 80 (medium slenderness) and 80 < λ ≤ 140 (slender): apply the reduction by the simplified method (accidental eccentricity and a safety factor against Euler buckling, with Fe = π² E0,ef I / L0²). Document the formula in the code.
  - λ > 140: fails, with a slenderness message.
  - Weak-axis buckling braced by the boards (OSB/cement board): use the strong axis, as in LSF.
- **Bending:** σ = M / W ≤ fc0,d (compressed edge).
- **Shear:** τ = 1.5 V / A ≤ fv,d.
- **Deflection:** with E0,ef and the same limits from the catalog.
- The `StructuralCheck`s come out with the same `kind` (`stud`, `joist`, `rafter`…) and `ratio`. The note says "NBR 7190".
- `suggest.ts`: suggestions in `wood` swap for larger sections of the `Wood` family (38 × 89 → 38 × 140 → 38 × 184 → 38 × 235) or for 40 cm spacing. Never suggest a steel profile in `wood`.

## 3. Quantities (`quantities/*`)
- **Cutting and purchase plan:** as it is today, by the profile's `barLengths` (3, 4.2, and 6 m pieces).
- **Fastening:** in `wood`, replace the structural screws (`screw_frame`) with `nail_wood` nails in kg.
  - Nails = total length of pieces × `nailKgPerM` + area of structural boards (OSB) × `nailKgPerM2Board`.
  - The board screws (drywall, cement board) remain.
- `woodM3` = Σ (length × section area) of the timber pieces; `woodM3PerM2` = `woodM3` / built area.
- `steelKg` in `wood` counts only the steel that is left over (straps, connectors); the sum of the timber pieces is not included.
- **Self-weight:** timber mass (`massPerM`), already in the profile.
- **Labor for the structure stage:** the current basis is `steel_kg`. In `wood` it would give almost zero. Use, only in `wood`, wall area × an equivalent rate.
  - No new constant in the catalog: compute the `structure` labor as `steel_kg` × equivalent mass = `woodM3` × density of steel? No: choose a simple rule and explain it in the return.
  - Proposal: in `wood`, the `structure` base quantity becomes (`woodM3` × `constants.wood.density`) kg, with the same R$/kg table. Timber weighs ~1/3 of steel in kg per m², so the value comes out lower. Check whether it is reasonable (R$ 150–250/m² of structure) and adjust in the return if it is not.

## 4. Warnings
- **Index:** in `wood`, the steel-index-per-m² warning (`warnings/index.ts`) becomes a timber one, with `constants.wood.indexRange` in m³/m².
- **Moisture/termites:** in `wood`, an informational warning `wood.treatment`: "Use madeira tratada em autoclave (CCA/CCB) e afaste do solo: barreira de umidade entre o radier e a guia inferior."
