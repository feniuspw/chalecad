# F7-W1: App — installations in the editor (3D and floor plan layer, panel, and tab)

**Goal:** the user turns on "Instalações" (Installations) and sees where outlets, switches, lights, water and sewer points, panel, and water tank are, in 3D and in the floor plan. They choose the water heating and the number of occupants. They see the circuits and the cost in a dedicated tab.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `project.installations` (`enabled`, `waterHeater`, `occupants?`, `panel?`).
- `results.installations` (`InstallationsReport`: `points`, `circuits`, `panel`, `demandKw`, `tankLiters`, `pipes`, `conduit`).
- `installations` stage in the budget.
- The engine (F7-E1) is built in parallel; until it lands, `results.installations` is `null`. Test with a hand-built report.

**May change:**
- `apps/web/components/scene/**` and `apps/web/lib/three/**`.
- `apps/web/lib/plan2d/**`.
- `apps/web/components/editor/properties/**`.
- `apps/web/components/results/**`, for the new tab and its registration in `ResultsPanel`.
- `apps/web/lib/pdf/**` and `apps/web/components/pdf/**`, for the PDF section.
- Tests alongside.

**Do not change:** `packages/**`, `lib/store/**`, `package.json`, `components/editor/TopBar.tsx`, and `lib/export/**` (they belong to F7-W2, in parallel).

## 1. Panel (`properties/InstallationsSection.tsx`)
- **Instalações** section in the project panel, near Finishes, with:
  - "Estimar instalações" (Estimate installations) switch;
  - heating (Segmented: Chuveiro elétrico (Electric shower), Gás (Gas), Solar);
  - occupants (field, with the computed default as placeholder);
  - "Posicionar quadro" (Place panel) button, which enters a simple click-on-the-plan mode. If it gets complex, use two fields x and y.
- Show a summary: points by type, circuits, demand (kW), and water tank (L).
- No rooms: the engine notice, with a link that selects the Room tool.

## 2. 3D layer (`components/scene/InstallationsView.tsx`)
- Visible when `enabled`, in the Finished, Exploded, and Plan modes. In Presentation, only with a "Mostrar instalações" (Show installations) toggle in the sun panel, off by default.
- Small, legible 3D icons, with color per system:
  - electrical in amber: outlet (small 2P+T plate), switch, light (disc on the ceiling), TUE (red plate), panel (box);
  - cold water in blue and hot water in red: small spheres on the wall;
  - sewer in gray: circle on the floor;
  - water tank: cylinder with the volume as a label.
- Instancing per type (there may be 100+ points).
- Click on a point: tooltip with label, circuit, and power. No selection in the store is needed.
- **Schematic routing (optional, desirable):** thin lines from the panel to the points of each circuit, in an L shape (Manhattan) and on the ceiling. For water, from the tank to the points.

## 3. Floor plan (`lib/plan2d`)
- Usual electrical design symbols: outlet (triangle/semicircle), switch (circle with S), light point (circle with X), panel (hatched rectangle).
- Plumbing symbols: cold water (blue circle AF), hot (AQ), sewer (gray circle with a cross).
- A legend.
- Turns on together with the layer.
- `planSvg` accepts an `installations` option to draw the symbols (F7-W2 uses it in the DXF; coordinate through the pure function `installationSymbols(points)` that returns strokes).

## 4. Results tab (`components/results/InstallationsTab.tsx`)
- Only appears with `enabled`.
- Circuit table: no., type, label, points, power, cable (mm²), and breaker (A).
- Totals: demand, cables per gauge (m), conduit (m), pipes (AF, AQ, sewer 40/100 in m), and water tank.
- Cost of the `installations` stage (material + labor) from `results.budget`.
- Fixed text: "Estimativa pelos mínimos da NBR 5410/5626. O projeto elétrico e hidráulico deve ser feito por profissional habilitado."

## 5. PDF
- A "Instalações (estimativa)" (Installations (estimate)) section with the circuit schedule and the totals, when a report exists.

## Acceptance tests
- `installationSymbols`: strokes of each type within 30 cm of the point.
- The circuit table renders with a hand-built report (component test, if the project already has that pattern; otherwise, a pure row function).
- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/web typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, and `build` green. Run the commands separately.
- In the return: how to verify and what was left out.
