# F15-W2: Easy manual drawing on the floor plan (walls and rooms)

The user wants **creating walls and rooms by hand to be easy**, as in architecture programs, drawing in the **floor plan view** (from above). Today there is the wall tool (`components/scene/tools/WallTool.tsx`):
- a click starts, clicks chain;
- Shift makes it orthogonal, Alt turns snap off, Esc or double click ends;
- the snap lives in `lib/tools/snap.ts`.

There are also room, opening, stairs, mezzanine, measure, and furniture tools. Read all of them (`components/scene/tools/*`, `lib/tools/*`, the toolbar in `components/editor/**`) before changing anything.

**May change:** `apps/web/components/scene/tools/**`, `apps/web/lib/tools/**`, `apps/web/components/editor/**` (toolbar, hints, shortcuts), `apps/web/components/onboarding/shortcuts.ts`, `apps/web/lib/store/editor.ts` (only the necessary tool actions and state; do not change the project format), plus the tests alongside and the e2e in `apps/web/e2e/` (only to add).

**Do not change:** `packages/**` (the schema does not change), `lib/project/wizard/**`, `lib/project/presets/**`, `lib/project/furniture*` (F15-W1 in parallel).

## What to deliver (reference: SketchUp, Archicad, Revit, and Floorplanner, in a simple version)
1. **Typed measurement:**
   - while drawing the wall, typing a number (for example `3.5` or `3,5`) and Enter creates the segment with that length in the cursor's direction;
   - `3.5;90` = length and angle;
   - a floating field near the cursor shows what is being typed.
2. **Live dimensions:** the current segment's length and angle appear next to the cursor, with the distance to the nearest parallel wall, when there is one.
3. **Visible and complete snap:**
   - endpoints, wall midpoint, intersection, perpendicular to a wall, and alignment with existing endpoints (dashed guide lines as in SketchUp);
   - 5 cm grid on the floor plan;
   - 15° angles with Shift;
   - an icon and a label for the snap type ("Ponta" (Endpoint), "Meio" (Midpoint), "Perpendicular", "Alinhado" (Aligned)).
4. **Rectangular room:** "Retângulo" (Rectangle) tool (or a mode of the wall tool, key `R`). Two clicks (or one click and typed dimensions, such as `3x4`) create the 4 interior walls. They connect to existing walls when they coincide and do not duplicate a wall that already exists in the same stretch; the interior becomes a room, asking for the use as the room tool does today.
5. **Close the outline:** clicking the starting point closes the polygon and ends, with a highlight when hovering over it.
6. **Direct editing (with the Select tool, on the floor plan):**
   - dragging a wall's endpoint moves the endpoint, and connected walls follow;
   - dragging the middle of a wall moves it in parallel, and the neighbors stretch;
   - show dimensions while dragging;
   - Delete removes;
   - "Dividir parede" (Split wall) and "Juntar paredes colineares" (Join collinear walls) in the wall menu (right-click or properties).
   - All through the existing store (`updateWall`, etc.), with undo and redo working. If there is no action to move connected endpoints, create a store action that applies the change atomically (one undo step).
7. **Automatic splitting:** a new wall whose endpoint lands in the middle of another splits the other one (T) so rooms close correctly, if the engine depends on it. Check how the engine resolves rooms (`resolveRoomLayouts`) first. If splitting is not needed, do not split.
8. **Floor plan toolbar:**
   - drawing tools grouped (Wall, Rectangle, Door/Window, Room, Stairs, Measure), with visible shortcuts (W, R, D, M…);
   - when choosing a drawing tool in the 3D view, offer to switch to the floor plan (or switch automatically, with a hint);
   - the shortcuts table (`?`) updated.
9. **Status bar hints** for each state ("Digite a medida e Enter" (Type the measurement and Enter), "Clique no início para fechar" (Click the start to close)).

## Tests
- Pure unit tests:
  - parsing of the typed measurement;
  - snap (each type, with priority);
  - generation of the rectangle's 4 walls, connecting to existing ones without duplicating;
  - moving the endpoint with connected walls.

  - on the floor plan, draw a wall by typing `3` and Enter, then a `3x3` rectangle;
  - check in the store (`window.__editor`) the walls and the room.
