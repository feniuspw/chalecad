# F14-W1: Wizard — floor plan as an architect would do it, and terrain slope

The user generated through the wizard a 6 × 9.78 A-frame (1 person, mezzanine bedroom, bathroom upstairs, "inside" laundry, gentle terrain). The result came out odd:
- **Laundry:** became a 3 × 2 m side annex, a separate room.
- **Empty storage room:** under the mezzanine a 1.8 × 1.4 m "Depósito" (Storage) was left with nothing in it, with walls thrown in the middle.
- **Stairs:** in the middle of the living room, at (2.6, 0.9) with rotation 0, instead of against a wall.
- **Kitchen:** sits under the low roof plane of the A-frame (x = 0.39), far from the bathroom, so there are two risers (plumbing stacks).
- **Mezzanine:** the bedroom ended up 2.0 × 2.4 m and got a **single** bed. It must **always** have a double bed, even for 1 person (they may host someone).
- **Upstairs bathroom walls:** cross the roof. The engine will cut the walls by the roof (card F14-E1, in parallel), but the generator must not put partitions where the ceiling height under the roof plane is below 2.1 m along the wall line, nor doors where they do not fit.
- **Terrain:** the wizard only offers flat, gentle (10%), or steep (30%). The user wants to be able to type **their own slope**.

## How architects do it (follow this)
- **Single wet core:**
  - bathroom, kitchen, and laundry against the **same plumbing wall** (kitchen on one side, bathroom on the other), at the back and under the mezzanine when there is a mezzanine;
  - the upstairs bathroom is **stacked** over the one below, with the same riser and the water tank tower on top.
- **"Inside" laundry:**
  - it is **not** a room or an annex; it is a **niche**: washer and tub at the end of the kitchen counter (integrated service area) or inside the bathroom, if it fits;
  - record the choice in a note ("A lavanderia ficou como área de serviço no fim da bancada da cozinha, na mesma parede hidráulica" (The laundry became a service area at the end of the kitchen counter, on the same plumbing wall));
  - the "outside" option (covered area at the back) stays as it is.
- **No leftovers:** no empty room. The leftover depth under the mezzanine becomes part of the kitchen, a cabinet, or the service area, never a closed "Depósito" with no use. Do not create walls that do not enclose a useful room.
- **Stairs against a wall:**
  - **Houses with straight walls** (gable, gambrel, shed, flat): straight stairs **next to an exterior side wall**, rising toward the mezzanine edge. Under them, a cabinet or niche.
  - **A-frame:** the sides are the roof, so the stairs go in the strip with ceiling height: **at one side of the central bay** (not in the middle of the living room), against the wet core wall. They run parallel to the ridge and reach the mezzanine edge. Check the clear height over the steps (≥ 2.0 m, use `roofBelowAt`).
  - The living room stays free at the front, near the glass.
- **Kitchen:** counter on the plumbing wall, with full ceiling height (≥ 2.3 m) in front of it. In the A-frame never under the low roof plane: the counter goes in the central bay, under the mezzanine (2.6 m clear) or next to the core wall.
- **Mezzanine:**
  - bedroom with a **double bed** (`furn_bed_double`), always;
  - the usable strip must be wide enough for the bed + 0.6 m of circulation on at least one side (about 2.8 m wide and 2.6 m deep). If it does not fit, increase the mezzanine depth or the A-frame's width and ridge, with a note;
  - the upstairs bathroom occupies only the strip with ceiling height, aligned with the one below.
- **Furniture:**
  - sofa facing the TV or the glass, not against the 0.9 m low roof plane;
  - nothing blocks the foot or top of the stairs, or the doors.

## Terrain: custom slope
- `TerrainChoice` gains `'custom'`, and `WizardAnswers` gains `slopePct?: number` (0–60).
- In the terrain question, the current cards plus a fourth, **"Eu sei a inclinação"** (I know the slope), with a numeric field in **%** and the equivalent in **degrees** (show both, for example "12% ≈ 6,8°"). Also offer typing in degrees (a %/° selector next to the field).
- Include a layperson text: "sobe 12 cm a cada 1 m" (rises 12 cm for every 1 m).
- The generator uses `makeSlopedSite({ slopePct })`:
  - above 20%, retaining wall and a note, like "steep";
  - below 1%, flat terrain.
- The test matrix (`generate.test.ts`) includes `custom` with 5%, 18%, and 35%.
