# F8-W1: App — choose wood frame in the editor and show timber

**Goal:** the user chooses between **Steel frame (LSF)** and **Wood frame** under Construction system (Sistema construtivo). The profiles switch to the timber ones, the 3D structure takes on a wood color, the indicators show m³ of timber instead of kg of steel, and the PDF and AI talk about the right system.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `system.structure: 'lsf' | 'wood'` and `WOOD_PROFILES` in `@chalecad/engine`.
- `quantities.woodM3` and `woodM3PerM2`.
- Profiles `Profile.material` (`steel` | `wood`), `Wood` family in the catalog.
- The engine (F8-E1) is built in parallel. Until it lands, in `wood` the pieces come out of the current generator (W profiles, no double plate) and `woodM3` = 0. The interface must not break with that.

**May change:**
- `apps/web/components/editor/properties/**`, especially `SystemSections.tsx`.
- `apps/web/components/results/**`, for steel/timber indicators and texts.
- `apps/web/components/scene/**` and `apps/web/lib/three/**`, only the color and material of the timber pieces in Structure/Exploded mode and the legend.
- `apps/web/lib/pdf/**` and `apps/web/components/pdf/**`, for assumptions and indicators.
- `apps/web/lib/ai/**`, for the prompt (construction system) and the metrics.
- `apps/web/lib/project/**`, except `presets/` and `templates.ts` (belonging to F8-W2, in parallel). The system switch is a pure function here.
- `apps/web/lib/project/compare.ts`, for the timber metric in the comparison.
- Tests alongside.

**Do not change:** `packages/**`, `lib/store/**`, `package.json`, `components/editor/NewProjectDialog.tsx`, `components/editor/EditorShell.tsx`, `components/onboarding/**`, and `components/projects/**` (belonging to F8-W2).

## 1. System switch (`lib/project/system.ts`, pure)
- `setStructure(draft, 'wood' | 'lsf')`: changes `system.structure` and swaps `system.profiles` for the system defaults (`WOOD_PROFILES`, or the LSF defaults from the schema).
  - Removes `gableStud` if the profile is not of the new material.
  - Keeps spacing, assemblies, and the rest.
- One test for each direction.

## 2. Panel (`SystemSections.tsx`)
- At the top of the Construction system section, a **Steel frame | Wood frame** Segmented control, with an explanatory line below:
  - LSF: "perfis de aço galvanizado, leve e preciso";
  - Wood: "pinus tratado em autoclave, NBR 7190, comum em chalés".
- On switching: `update('Trocar sistema construtivo', …)` and a toast "Perfis trocados para madeira (38 × 89 nos montantes)…".
- The profile Selects (stud, track, gable stud, beam, rafter, purlin) list only profiles of the system's material. The names come from the catalog; for timber, show "38 × 89 mm" etc.
- Items that only make sense in LSF (strap bracing, if applicable) remain. Do not hide anything the engine still uses.

## 3. Results
- **KPIs (`Kpis.tsx`):**
  - in `wood`, replace "Aço · kg/m²" with "Madeira · m³/m²", using `woodM3PerM2` and the `constants.wood.indexRange` range on the small bar;
  - with `woodM3` = 0 (old engine), show "—".
- **Structure tab:** the code notes come from the engine; adjust only the fixed texts that say "aço" (steel) or "LSF" when the system is timber.
- **Materials:** the grouping already follows the catalog; check that the name of the timber pieces displays well ("peça 3 m").

## 4. 3D
- In `wood`, the pieces in Structure/Exploded mode use a timber material (light pine color `#d9b77e`, high roughness; the procedural wood texture, if it already exists in `lib/three/textures.ts`, at small scale). Instancing kept.
- The heat map stays the same.
- The Exploded legend says "Montantes de madeira" (Timber studs).

## 5. PDF and AI
- Assumptions: "Sistema: Wood frame (pinus tratado C25, NBR 7190)" or "Light Steel Frame (ZAR 230)".
- The "Aço por m²" (Steel per m²) indicator becomes "Madeira por m³/m²" (Timber per m³/m²) in `wood`.
- AI prompt (`lib/ai/prompt.ts`): state the system and that profile suggestions must be of the same material. AI and comparison metrics: include `woodM3PerM2`.

## Acceptance tests
- `setStructure`: round trip and removal of an incompatible `gableStud`.
- The profile filter by material (pure function) returns only `Wood` in `wood`.
- The KPI in `wood` uses `woodM3PerM2` (pure formatting function).
- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/web typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, and `build` green (separately).
- In the return: how to verify and what was left out.
