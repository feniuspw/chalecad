# F9-E1: Engine — performance (NBR 15575 thermal, photovoltaic solar, and rainwater)

**Goal:** the engine evaluates the thermal performance of the walls and roof using the NBR 15575 simplified procedure, with transmittance U, thermal capacity CT, and absorptance, by bioclimatic zone. With the options turned on, it also sizes an on-grid photovoltaic system and rainwater harvesting, and costs both.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `project.performance` (`zone?`, `solar`, `rainwater`) and `meta.region` (UF, state) in `schema/project.ts`.
- `ThermalElement`, `SolarReport`, `RainReport`, `PerformanceReport`, and `Results.performance`, currently `null`, in `schema/results.ts`.
- Catalog:
  - `constants.performance`:
    - `thermal`: properties per material, surface resistances, air gap, zone by UF, absorptances, U limits by zone, and minimum CT;
    - `solar`: full-sun hours by UF, consumption per occupant, module, PR, tariff, and minimum bill;
    - `rain`: annual rainfall by UF, runoff coefficient, non-potable fraction, and reserve days.
  - Materials `pv_system` (price per Wp), `rain_filter`, `pump_small`, and `water_tank_*`.

## 1. Thermal (`performance/thermal.ts`)
- **Zone:** `project.performance.zone`, or `thermal.zoneByUF[meta.region]`; `zoneSource` says which one was used.
- **Walls:** for each exterior wall assembly in use (`system.assemblies.exterior`, and `wood` too), compute per NBR 15220-2:
  - R = Σ e/λ of the layers with a thermal property + `airGapR` if the cavity is not entirely filled by the insulation (stud web > insulation thickness; the core is the web of the `wallStud` profile) + `rsiWall` + `rseWall`;
  - U = 1/R;
  - CT = Σ e × ρ × c (kJ/m²K, e in m);
  - the facade finish (`finishes.facade`), if it has properties, adds to the exterior side;
  - `face: 'both'` layers count on both sides;
  - simplification: neglect the thermal bridge of the studs and say so in the label ("seção corrente, sem ponte térmica").
- **Roof:** U of the roof panel (`roof.cladding`), plus the lining (`finishes.interiorRoof`) with an air gap if there is a lining, plus `rsiRoof` + `rseRoof`. The α comes from `roofAlpha[finishes.roofColor ?? 'graphite']`.
- **Wall absorptance:** `facadeAlpha[finishes.facade]`, or `defaultFacadeAlpha`.
- **Limits:**
  - U ≤ `wallU[zone][α ≤ 0.6 ? 0 : 1]`;
  - CT ≥ `wallCT[zone]` (0 = no requirement; `limitCT: null`);
  - roof: U ≤ `roofU[...]`.
- **Warnings:**
  - `thermal.wall_u` / `thermal.roof_u` (warn): "U = 2,9 W/m²K acima de 2,5 (zona 3, α 0,7): aumente o isolante ou use cor clara".
  - `thermal.wall_ct` (info, because LSF almost never reaches CT 130 by the simplified method): "CT baixa (≈ 25 kJ/m²K): sistemas leves pedem avaliação por simulação (NBR 15575-1, procedimento de simulação)".

## 2. Solar (`performance/solar.ts`), with `performance.solar`
- Monthly consumption = occupants × `kWhPerPersonMonth` + electric shower (`installations.waterHeater === 'electric_shower'`) × occupants × `showerKWhPerPersonMonth`.
  - Occupants: by the same rule as the installations (`installations.occupants` or 2 per bedroom, minimum 2), reusing the function from the installations module, if there is one.
- Target generation = consumption − `minBillKWh`.
- kWp = target × 12 / (HSP × 365 × PR).
- Modules = ⌈kWp × 1000 / moduleWp⌉; area = modules × `moduleArea`.
- **Roof plane:** the one with the highest generation = the one whose normal faces most toward the north, using `meta.northDeg` and the tilt.
  - Simple orientation factor: 1 for north, 0.9 east/west, 0.75 south, × a tilt factor close to 1.
  - `fits` = area ≤ usable area of the roof plane (80% of the sloped area).
  - If it does not fit, `fits` is false and the `solar.area` warning (info).
- Cost = Wp × price (range) from the price base. Annual savings = generation × tariff. Payback = cost / savings.
- **Quantities:** `pv_system` in Wp (quantity = modules × Wp), in the `installations` stage.
  - Labor is already in the installed-system price. Do not let the stage's % add on top: use an item with labor 0, or explain the solution.
  - If the stage's % rule adds labor on top, document it and, as an alternative, put the item in the `extras` stage. Choose the one that does not double the cost.

## 3. Rain (`performance/rain.ts`), with `performance.rainwater`
- Catchment area = Σ areas of the roof planes projected in plan.
- Annual volume = area × mm × `runoff`.
- Non-potable demand = occupants × `waterPerPerson` (installations) × `nonPotableShare` × 365.
- Reservoir = daily demand × `reserveDays`, capped by the average monthly harvested volume, rounded to the installations' `tankSizes` → `water_tank_<L>`.
- Coverage = min(1, annual volume / annual demand).
- **Items:** `water_tank_<L>`, `rain_filter`, `pump_small`, and gutters, which already exist if `roof.gutters`; do not duplicate. `installations` stage.

## 4. compute.ts and engineer package
- `results.performance` always: thermal always comes out; solar and rain only with the flags (otherwise `null`).
- Snapshot: the new report changes the hash. Run `vitest -u` and explain.
- Engineer package: zone, U and CT of the walls and roof, and the question: "Desempenho térmico: confirmar por simulação (CT baixa em sistema leve)?".

## Acceptance tests
- Standard LSF wall (cement board 10 + OSB 11.1 + wool 50 in a 90 web + drywall 12.5): U against manual calculation (±2%), with the air gap for the remaining 40 mm.
- Zone 1 (PR) vs. zone 8 (RJ): the limits change; CT has no requirement in zone 8.
- Black vs. white roof panel: α changes the roof limit in zone 3.
- Solar in SP with 4 occupants and an electric shower: consumption (4 × 55 + 4 × 30 = 340), kWp, and modules against manual calculation.
- North-facing roof plane chosen on the gable roof with an east-west ridge.
- Rain: a house with 60 m² of roof in SP → annual volume ≈ 60 × 1450 × 0.8 L.
- Without the flags: `solar` and `rain` are `null`, with no new items in the budget.
- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/engine typecheck test lint` green (separately).
- In the return: formulas, simplifications, and what was left out.
