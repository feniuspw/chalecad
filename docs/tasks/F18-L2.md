# F18-L2: Integration fixes for electrical after F18 (conduit fill and manual-move test)

After merging F18-D, F18-T and F18-L, two problems remain. Both are in the electrical engine.

## 1. Auto conduit over the fill limit (wizard matrix)
`apps/web/lib/project/wizard/generate.test.ts` ("gerador: matriz de combinações") fails for `modern 1q 2b` (all mezzanine options, `coz=integrated`, `laundry: 'inside'`). The error is `elec.conduit.fill`: auto conduit `auto-cd43` has 58% fill against a 40% limit.

**Diagnosis.**
- Conduit `auto-cd43` (DN 40) carries 6 circuits: lighting `auto-c1`, sockets `auto-c4` and `auto-c5`, and TUE circuits `auto-c6`, `auto-c7` and `auto-c8`. Two of the TUEs are 5,500 VA showers.
- With 6 grouped circuits FCA = 0.57, which pushes each shower to 25 mm².
- `packages/engine/src/installations/electrical/route.ts` limits circuits per conduit to 4 (`MAX_CIRCUITS_PER_CONDUIT`) and keeps heavy circuits alone (`MAX_CIRCUITS_HEAVY = 1`), and it estimates cable area with the FCA of 4 circuits.
- Even so, the final conduit groups 6 circuits. The `feasible` check only walks tree ancestors, while conduits are built from merged geometric edges (`calc.ts`, the worst edge per conduit), so branches that share a path end up in the same conduit.
- This passed before F18-L. F18-L changed the lighting layout (more points, switch placement, local ceiling heights → longer cable runs).

**Goal.** The automatic routing never produces a conduit over its fill limit, and never puts showers or other heavy TUE circuits together with others beyond the rule. Fix the root cause in routing or in conduit building. Do not just raise limits.

## 2. `packages/engine/test/installations/electrical/manual.test.ts`
The test "mover uma tomada para longe (com o eletroduto) aumenta o comprimento e a queda do circuito" fails: expected 21.0 > 24.0.

It started failing after the kitchen lux change (general lighting is now 300 lux target / 200 minimum, in `catalog.constants.electrical.lighting`), which changes how many luminaires there are and how circuits are assigned.

Find out whether this is a real bug (moving a point does not lengthen its circuit) or a test fixture that depended on the old layout. Fix accordingly. Keep the test's intent.
