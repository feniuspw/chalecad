## What to do
1. **Effective layout** (`layout`):
   - if `plumbing.manual`, use the saved points, pipes, and tank;
   - otherwise, **generate**:
     - water points from the furniture (`fixtureByFurniture`; the shower becomes `shower_electric`, or `shower_mixer` with `waterHeater` gas or solar);
     - water tank: over the tower (`water_tower`, use `platformZ`) or in the ceiling cavity over the bathroom, with the bottom at `atticTankRise` above the ceiling;
     - **cold water**: header (barrilete) through the ceiling cavity, riser going down in the bathroom, and branches in orthogonal runs to each point (z at the point's height);
     - **hot water**: only with gas or solar;
     - **sewage**: from the fixtures to the outlet under the floor, with minimum slope per DN, siphoned drain box in the bathroom and the toilet branch DN 100;
     - **venting**: vent pipe from the bathroom up above the roof.
   - Deterministic `auto-*` ids. The `layout` must pass `Plumbing.parse`, because the interface copies this layout when "editing by hand".
2. **Cold and hot water** (section 10.3):
   - graph by coinciding endpoints (2 cm), a tree rooted at the tank;
   - 3D lengths;
   - inferred fittings (a 90° vertex becomes an elbow; a 3-way node becomes a tee, run or branch; tank outlet; gate valve on the header; pressure valve on the shower's sub-branch);
   - accumulated weights, Q = max(0.3·√ΣP, max q);
   - DN by target velocity and by minimum DN, without decreasing toward the tank, respecting the user's `dn`;
   - FWH (Fair-Whipple-Hsiao; cold and hot);
   - dynamic pressure at each point;
   - `requiredTankRise`;
   - maximum velocity and static pressure.
   - `water.*` warnings: `pressure_low` is `warn` at a level above the ground floor and `info` on the ground floor, with the message "suba a caixa X m ou aumente o tubo" (raise the tank X m or increase the pipe); also `velocity_high`, `static_high`, `disconnected`, `tank_small`, and `dn_min`.
3. **Sewage** (section 10.4):
   - accumulated UHC (hydraulic contribution units);
   - DN per branch and stack, without reducing;
   - toilet always DN 100;
   - slope (default by DN, or the user's `slope`);
   - elevations;
   - distance to the vent;
   - kitchen sink requires a grease trap.
   - `sewer.*` warnings: `toilet_dn`, `uhc_exceeded`, `slope`, `dn_reduction`, `vent_distance`, `no_vent`, and `grease_trap`.
4. **Septic tank** (`sewerDisposal: 'septic'`): V = 1000 + N(C·T + K·Lf), with N = `installations.occupants`.
5. **Tank:** volume by the reserve; `fullKg`. Keep the existing `install.tank_load` warning.
6. **Budget items:** pipes by DN and material (m), fittings counted from the graph, valves, boxes, septic tank, and filter. The old estimated plumbing items go away when the new one runs.
