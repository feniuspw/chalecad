## 1. Facade on the outside only
- Today the facade texture (`finishes.facade`) also appears on the inner face of the exterior walls when the room is not marked (`BuildingView.tsx`, `Wall`, `facadeMap` on the whole `Surface`).
- Apply the facade only on the exterior face (`exteriorSide`). Possible approaches:
  - a thin plane set back 2 mm on the exterior face, as `FinishesView` does on the inside;
  - or groups/material per face in the `ExtrudeGeometry`.
- The inner face without a room keeps the interior wall color (`COLORS.wallInterior`).

## 2. Presentation without technical edges
- In `viewMode === 'presentation'`, hide the `Edges` of the extras (`ExtrasView.tsx`) and any other technical edge/line that remains: outlines, labels, dimensions.
- Selection still highlights.

## 3. Underside of the roof when walking inside
- Without `finishes.interiorRoof`, the inner face of the roof planes is dark in walk mode.
- Give the interior face of the roof planes a default light material (light board/OSB, correct `side`) that receives the light from the rooms' `PointLight`s and from the ambient light.
- Also check that the roof planes cast shadows and do not let light leak through gaps at the eave (`shadow-bias`/`normalBias`).

## 4. Night sky
- With the sun below the horizon (elevation < −4°), replace the `Sky` with a night-blue background with stars: drei `<Stars>`, procedural, no file.
- Make a smooth transition at twilight (opacity or intensity by elevation).
- The moon is optional.

## 5. Walk-through: stairs
- Stair collision: nobody passes through the side or the opening.
- Climbing the stairs:
  - when walking over the projection of a stair (`results.model.stairs`), the eye height follows the ramp of the steps, interpolating by progress along the flight;
  - on reaching the top, switch to the upper level: floor = mezzanine elevation; collision = walls of that level + slab edge with guardrail;
  - going down is the reverse;
  - the mezzanine guardrail blocks falling: the slab edge without a wall is an obstacle, except at the stair arrival.
- Pure function `stairHeightAt(stair, point)` in `lib/three/walk.ts`, tested (straight and L-shaped).
