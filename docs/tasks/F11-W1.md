# F11-W1: App — "Me ajude a montar" wizard for non-experts and water tank tower in the editor

**Goal:** someone who knows nothing about construction creates a chalet by answering simple questions, with a short explanation on each and illustrations. At the end, they see the finished chalet (3D, rooms, furniture, and cost) and create the project. They can also add and view the **water tank tower** in the editor.

**Contracts (already on main, do not change):**
- `WaterTowerExtra` (`kind: 'water_tower'`, `position`, `width`, `depth`, `platformZ`, `tankLiters?`, `roof`).
- `ExtraModel` with `segments`, `posts`, `roofs?`, and `tank?`. The geometry comes from F11-E1, in parallel; until then the tower model comes back empty. Draw from the `ExtraModel` and, in tests, use a hand-built model.
- Everything that already exists for building projects:
  - `createProject` and the templates;
  - `lib/project/presets/builder.ts`, which places furniture against the wall by geometry;
  - `addRoom`/`patchRoom`, `setFinish`, `addAnnex`, `addFurniture`, `furnishRoom`, and `addExtra`;
  - `setStructure`;
  - `installations` and `performance`.

**May change:**
- `apps/web/lib/project/wizard/**` (new, pure and tested).
- `apps/web/components/wizard/**` (new).
- `apps/web/components/editor/NewProjectDialog.tsx`: third tab, first in the list, **"Me ajude a montar"** (Help me build it).
- `apps/web/components/projects/**`: call to the wizard on the empty list.
- `apps/web/lib/project/extras.ts` and `apps/web/components/editor/properties/**`, to add and edit the tower ("+ Torre da caixa d'água" (+ Water tank tower) button in the extras and fields for position, size, tank height, and roof).
- `apps/web/components/scene/ExtrasView.tsx` and `apps/web/lib/three/**`, to draw the tower (walls like the chalet's, roof in the roof panel color, translucent blue water tank visible through an optional cutaway).

- Tests alongside.

## 1. Questions (`lib/project/wizard/questions.ts`)
- Each question has a simple title, a one-line "why we ask," and options with an icon or illustration (reuse `TypologyIllustrations`).
- Skip questions that do not apply.
- Sensible defaults: you can click "Próximo" (Next) without thinking.

1. **What is the chalet for?** (Para que é o chalé?) Weekend / vacation · Living · Rental (inn, Airbnb). Affects the suggested finishes, durability, and the laundry.
2. **How many people will sleep in it?** Counter from 1 to 10.
3. **How many bedrooms?** Suggested from the number of people (2 per bedroom), editable, with "a living room with a sofa bed counts as an extra bed".
4. **What style do you imagine?**
   - A-frame (roof down to the ground);
   - Traditional chalet (gable roof, with a mezzanine);
   - Barn (gambrel);
   - Modern (shed or flat roof);
   - "Me ajude a escolher" (Help me choose): recommends based on the number of bedrooms and the budget, with an explanation.
5. **Mezzanine (a partial upper floor)?** No · Yes, for sleeping · Yes, for living room/office. Only for styles that support it; explain that the low A-frame has little standing room up there.
6. **How many bathrooms?** 1 · 2.
   - If there is a mezzanine: **"Quer um banheiro lá em cima?"** (Do you want a bathroom upstairs?), with the explanation: "Banheiro no mezanino precisa da caixa d'água mais alta que o chuveiro. Muitos chalés fazem uma torrezinha que atravessa o telhado só para isso" (A mezzanine bathroom needs the water tank higher than the shower. Many chalets build a little tower that goes through the roof just for this). Choosing yes adds the tower over that bathroom.
7. **Bedrooms or bathroom on the sides (A-frame)?** No · One side · Both sides. Explain that it gains space without enlarging the A-frame.
8. **Kitchen:** open to the living room (more space and light) · separate.
9. **Laundry:** indoors · covered outdoor area · not needed.
10. **Porch/deck:** none · at the front · at the front and covered.
11. **Site:** flat · slight slope · steeply sloped. And the state (UF). Ask "is it in the highlands/mountains?" to adjust the bioclimatic zone (1–3) and the wind.
12. **Wood or steel for the structure?** Steel frame (galvanized steel, precise and light) · Wood frame (treated wood, cozy) · "Tanto faz" (Either; steel). One line of pros and cons for a layperson.
13. **Extras:** solar power · rainwater harvesting · place furniture already · estimate installations. Pre-check furniture and installations.
14. **How much do you plan to spend?** Range (up to 150k, 150–250k, 250–400k, above, don't know). Used only for comparison at the end.

## 2. Generator (`lib/project/wizard/generate.ts`)
`generateProject(answers, catalog) → { project, notes: string[] }`, pure.

**Dimensions:** from the target area (≈ 22 m² of living room, kitchen, and circulation + 9 m² per bedroom + 4 m² per bathroom + 4 m² of indoor laundry), adjusted to the style:
- **A-frame:** base width 6–8 m, depth from the ground-floor area with a 0.6–0.9 m knee wall; extra bedrooms in the annexes.
- **Gable:** width 6–7 m.
- **Gambrel:** 6–7 m.
- **Modern:** box with a flat or shed roof.
- Limit to reasonable dimensions. If it does not fit, use the mezzanine or the annexes and record a note.

**Floor plan:** interior walls forming closed rooms, from per-style rules (bands: front living room/kitchen, rear bedrooms and bathroom; bathroom next to the kitchen to group the plumbing).
- Interior doors in each room.
- Window in each bedroom; tilt window in the bathroom.
- The stairs against a wall, with headroom: use the existing functions (`fitAFrameStairs`, `straightStairFor`…).

**Mezzanine:** as requested. The mezzanine bathroom has walls on the mezzanine and the tower over it: `platformZ` = mezzanine floor + 2.1 + 1.2 m.

**Rooms:** marked with use and name ("Quarto 1" (Bedroom 1), "Quarto 2" (Bedroom 2)).
- Finishes by purpose: rental → porcelain tile and paint, more durable; weekend → vinyl and wood.
- Facade by style.

**Furniture:** with `furnishRoom` in each room.

**Other parameters:** `setStructure`, `installations.enabled`, and `performance.solar/rainwater`; `meta.region` and `performance.zone` (highlands = zone 2); terrain (slight or steep slope → `site` with the already supported slope); deck through the extra.

**Notes in simple pt-BR:** what was decided and why ("Coloquei os 2 quartos nas laterais para caber sem aumentar o A-frame" (I put the 2 bedrooms on the sides so it fits without enlarging the A-frame)).

**Matrix test:** generate a broad sample of the combinations (styles × bedrooms 1–4 × bathrooms 1–2 × mezzanine × upstairs bathroom × annexes). For each one:
- `Project.parse` ok;
- `computeAll` without `error`;
- all rooms with `polygon` ≠ null;
- requested bedrooms and bathrooms present (or a note explaining why not);
- furniture without `furniture.collision`, `furniture.wall`, `furniture.outside`, or `furniture.door`;
- stairs without a severe `norm.stair_headroom`.
List in the return the combinations that failed and why.

## 3. Interface (`components/wizard/WizardDialog.tsx`)
- One step per screen, with a progress bar, "Voltar" (Back), "Próximo" (Next), and "Pular para o resultado" (Skip to the result).
- Large clickable cards.
- Layperson language: no "LSF" without explanation; technical terms with a tooltip.
- **Result:**
  - 3D thumbnail: a simple `Viewport` or the existing facade and floor plan SVGs;
  - floor plan with the rooms;
  - list of what it will have (rooms and areas);
  - estimated cost (range) against the stated budget:
    - above the range: cut suggestions in plain language ("tirar o banheiro de cima economiza ~R$ X" (removing the upstairs bathroom saves ~R$ X), computed for real by running the generator without that option);
  - the generator's notes;
  - "Criar meu chalé" (Create my chalet) and "Voltar e ajustar" (Go back and adjust).
- On create: the project opens in the editor, with the toast "Seu chalé está pronto. Mude o que quiser: tudo recalcula na hora" (Your chalet is ready. Change whatever you like: everything recalculates instantly).

## 4. Tower in the editor
- Extras: "+ Torre da caixa d'água" button, which places the tower over the mezzanine bathroom, or the ground-floor one, or at the center.
- Fields: position (x, y), width, depth, tank floor height, and roof (Segmented).
- Explanation: "Precisa ficar ~1 m acima do chuveiro mais alto" (It needs to be ~1 m above the highest shower).
- **3D:** walls (`segments`) with the facade material; roof (`roofs`) in the roof panel color; tank (`tank`) as a blue cylinder visible in Explodido (Exploded) and Estrutura (Structure); posts (`posts`) in Estrutura.
- **Floor plan:** dashed outline with a label.

## Acceptance tests
- The generator matrix, above.
- Cut suggestions: removing an option lowers the cost (real number, from the engine).
- Questions: conditional skips (without a mezzanine it does not ask about the upstairs bathroom; non-A-frame does not ask about the sides).
- The tower in the editor: adding places it over the mezzanine bathroom; the floor plan draws the outline.
- `pnpm --filter @chalecad/web typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, and `build` green (run separately).
- In the return: how to verify, the combinations that failed, and what was left out.
