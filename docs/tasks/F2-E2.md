# F2-E2: Engine — shed and flat-roof typologies, and extras (deck, wall, fence)

**Goal:** extend `buildModel`, `generatePieces`, and the quantity takeoff to the `shed` and `flat_roof` typologies, and calculate the `deck`, `wall` (masonry wall), and `fence` extras, with geometry for the 3D view.

**May change:** `packages/engine/src/model/**`, `packages/engine/src/framing/**`, `packages/engine/src/quantities/**`, `packages/engine/src/extras/**` (new), `packages/engine/src/index.ts` (exports), `packages/engine/test/**` (new files `test/model/shed.test.ts`, `test/model/flat.test.ts`, `test/extras/**`; targeted adjustments to existing ones if the change is intentional).
**Do not change:** `src/schema/**`, `src/structure/**`, `src/budget/**`, `packages/catalog/**`.

The contract `BuildingModel.extras: ExtraModel[]` already exists in `results.ts` (the orchestrator added it). Fill it in.

## Typologies
**shed** (`highSide` front/back/left/right): exterior walls with a trapezoidal outline on the sides and rectangular (high/low) on the others; a single roof plane `roof-a` with slope atan((high − low)/span), eave `eave` on all sides of the roof plane (horizontal projection on the low/high side, extension on the sides). Framing: rafters from the low side to the high side, purlins, sloped top tracks on the trapezoidal walls. Mezzanine: same as the gable.
**flat_roof**: exterior walls of height `wallHeight + parapetHeight` (the parapet is the top part: create the walls with full height and mark the parapet strip by creating separate hosts `kind: 'parapet'` above `wallHeight`, IDs `ext-front-parapet` etc.); a single roof plane `roof-a` inside the parapet, slope = atan(slopePct/100) falling to the back, no eave; internal gutter on the low side (length = width). Framing: roof joists (profile `system.profiles.joist`) on the shorter span every `rafterSpacing`, purlins/hat sections (cartolas). Quantities: roof panel over the roof plane area; flashing at the top of the parapet (perimeter); internal gutter.

## Extras (`src/extras/`)
Generate `ExtraModel` for the 3D view and quantity lines (stage `extras`) via a `computeExtras(project, model, catalog)` function called by `computeQuantities` (integrate it there).
- **Wall** (`wall`): segments of the `path` (closed if `closed`). Concrete/structural block: area = Σ length × height; blocks = area × `masonry.blocksPerM2` × (1 + waste); grout = area × `groutM3PerM2` (structural: double); CA-50 rebar = area × `rebarKgPerM2`; mortar (`mortar`) 0.02 m³/m² → bags; strip footing: concrete = length × `stripFootingWidth` × `stripFootingDepth`, rebar 3 kg/m; grade beam (`grade_beam`): 0.15 × 0.3 m section. `lsf_board`: simplified LSF structure (studs every 0.6 m, tracks) + cement board on both faces.
- **Fence** (`fence`): posts every `postSpacing` (+1 per corner, rounded), type per `postType` (`post_concrete`/`post_eucalyptus`/`post_steel`); mesh (`fence_welded`/`fence_chain`) by length in rolls; 3-strand barbed wire on top if `mesh = 'barbed'` (then no mesh, 5 strands); block base wall if `baseWallHeight > 0` (blocks as in the wall) with footing.
- **Deck** (`deck`): polygon abutting the facade `side` (length = entire facade if omitted, starting at `offset`), depth `depth`, elevation `elevation`. Wood (`deck_wood`, m²) and frame (`deck_frame`, m²); guardrail (`guardrail`, m) on the free sides if `guardrail`; optional roof (`covered`): a lean-to sandwich roof panel plane (area = length × (depth + 0.3)/cos 10°), with purlins.
- **Retaining wall** (`retaining`): geometry and warning only for now (Phase 4 calculates it).
- `ExtraModel`: segments with height/thickness/baseZ, posts, deck polygon and elevation, `covered` flag.
