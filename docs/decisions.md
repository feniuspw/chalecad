# Architecture decisions (log)

Decisions made by the orchestrator with the autonomy granted by the user on 2026-11-07.

0. **Repository location:** the project started at `~/Documents/Projects/chalecad`, but macOS blocked the session's access to `~/Documents` in the middle of Phase 0. Work continued at `~/Developer/chalecad` and, once access was back, the repository returned to `~/Documents/Projects/chalecad`. The original full spec is in `docs/spec.md`; the operational summary is in `docs/spec-resumo.md`.
1. **pnpm 11.5** (installed) instead of pnpm 9; **TypeScript 5.9** (native 7.0 is still too new for Next/ESLint); **ESLint 9**; **Vitest 4**; **Zod 4**; **Next 16**, **React 19.3**, **three 0.187**, **R3F 9**, **drei 10**, **Tailwind 4**.
2. **Exterior walls, gable ends, and roof planes are derived from the typology**, not saved in the JSON, with stable IDs (`ext-front`, `ext-back`, `ext-left`, `ext-right`, `roof-a`, `roof-b`, `wing-*`). Openings reference these IDs in `host`. `walls` only holds user walls.
3. **Schema v1 covers all phases** (typologies from Phases 2/3, terrain from Phase 4, extras, structural and cost estimate assumptions). The engine ignores what it does not implement yet and raises an alert.
4. **Edited prices stay in the project** (`priceOverrides`), on top of the `priceBookId` base: a reopened project reproduces the same results.
5. **Parametric labor per stage** (R$/m² or R$/kg, in the price base), zeroed in the "eu faço" ("I do it") stages.
6. **Walls rendered with `ExtrudeGeometry` + holes** for openings, instead of CSG.
7. **Database:** `DATA_MODE=sqlite` uses Drizzle + better-sqlite3 at `./data/chalecad.sqlite`; local autosave in IndexedDB. No login in local mode.
8. **Parallelism:** the rule "never 2 subagents in the same package" was relaxed for `apps/web` when the cards have disjoint folders and the dependencies are already on main.
9. **Deflection limits** in the catalog as a denominator (L/350 → 350). Profile **sections** with rounded corners (centerline radius = 2t).
10. **Agent worktrees** at `../chalecad-wt/<card>` (outside the repository), created by the orchestrator.
11. **Steel index:** the engine counts piece by piece what it models. The spec's 6.5 x 8.5 chalet, with studs at 60 cm and a rafter roof (no trusses or ceiling), gives about 16 kg/m²; at 40 cm, about 19 kg/m². The 21.9–24.7 kg/m² range from the literature applies to homes with trusses and a structured ceiling. We kept the range as an alert (we did not tune the engine to "fit"). Rafters follow the stud modulation (aligned structure).
12. **A-frame side annexes** (`typology.annexes`, at most one per side): a box with a shed roof that rises toward the A-frame and continues inside the base line until it crosses the main roof plane. The A-frame roof plane is cut in that stretch (shortened rafters, interrupted purlins, header at the top of the cut) and the opening stays open to the interior; end walls also close the triangle between the two roof planes (outline with sloped base, framing uses `bottomAt`). New field with default `[]`, so it remains in schema v1. IDs: `annex-<side>-front|side|back|roof`; with a low wall, the stretch after the annex becomes `ext-left-2`/`ext-right-2`. The editor adjusts the measurements so the geometry stays valid (`lib/project/annex.ts`: low side ≥ 2 m, annex roof plane less steep than the A-frame's, junction away from the ridge).
13. **Code alerts** (`warnings/norms.ts`, constants in `catalog.constants.norms`): ceiling height (NBR 15575: 2.50 m living room/bedrooms, 2.30 m service areas), 2.10 m clear height over the stairs, 1.10 m guardrail with gaps ≤ 11 cm (NBR 14718), handrail, lighting 1/6 and ventilation 1/12 per volume (typical building code; does not detect enclosed rooms), safety glass (NBR 7199), and A-frame thrust (three-hinged arch, H = w·S²/8f). The stair tread is now calculated so that 2r + g falls in the middle of the Blondel range (63–64 cm). Failed headers receive a profile suggestion without `profileId` (there is no header profile field in the project). These are usual values: the municipality's building code may require more.
14. **Localized reinforcement:** `system.profiles.gableStud` (optional) gives tall gable ends (`kind: 'gable'`) their own stud, with a matching track; the check becomes `gable_stud` and the suggestion changes only that profile, without touching the spacing. `system.rafterSpacing` (optional, A-frame only) separates the rafter spacing, because the A-frame roof planes bear on the radier (raft slab) and do not need to follow the studs; the annexes remain aligned. The glazed facade has no stud or header check (it is left to the window frame supplier). In the user's chalet, "Ajustar às normas" (Adjust to codes) went from +R$ 47k to +R$ 38k; the rest is the 8 m rafter, which needs intermediate support (engineer's decision).
15. **Rooms and finishes (F5):** the room only stores use, `seed`, and finishes; the engine finds the polygon through the planar graph of the walls (joins points < 5 cm apart, extends walls ≤ 15 cm from another, prunes loose ends) and checks ceiling height, lighting/ventilation, and minimum area per use (`constants.norms.rooms`). Interior walls of a wet room use the wet assembly. Finishes only enter the cost estimate when chosen. In 3D the textures are procedural (canvas), with no file downloads. The store now normalizes the project with the schema defaults on load (old projects without `rooms`/`finishes`). Pending: room table in `ChaleDocument` (the data already comes out in `PdfData.rooms`); ventilation uses the total glass (there is no openable-area constant).
16. **Presentation mode (F5-W2):** sun from the real position (NOAA; state capital or typed coordinates; `meta.northDeg`, default 180 = front facing north), procedural sky and environment (no HDRI), post-processing with `@react-three/postprocessing` 3.2.2 / `postprocessing` 6.39.5 (N8AO without `halfRes`, which breaks with three 0.187), `percentage` shadows (three 0.187 removed PCFSoft), Auto/High/Light quality with `PerformanceMonitor`, first person with collision against the level's walls, and 2× PNG capture. Performance on a real GPU has not been measured yet (the panel shows the FPS).
17. **Furniture (F6):** `project.furniture` only stores the catalog item, position (center), and rotation (front facing −y at 0°); size, clearance, whether it sits against the wall, and whether it needs water come from `catalog.furniture` (34 items, 3D models and floor plan symbols drawn in code, no file downloads). The engine resolves outlines and emits `furniture.*` alerts (outside, wall, collision, clearance, door, low ceiling, stairs, water; circulation in the simple version: free area < 40%). Furniture enters the cost estimate only with `budget.includeFurniture` and does not count toward self-weight. Room ventilation now uses the openable area (`norms.openableFraction`; a fixed glass facade does not ventilate). Walking inside climbs stairs and collides with furniture, stairs, and the mezzanine edge; at night the sky has stars.
18. **Estimated utilities (F7):** with `installations.enabled`, the engine places points per the minimums of NBR 5410 (outlets by perimeter, lighting 100 VA + 60 VA/4 m², shower TUE (dedicated-use outlet)) and NBR 5626 (reserve 150 L/resident × 2 days), builds circuits from the catalog's cable table, and prices them in the new `installations` stage (labor 90–120% of material). It is a planning estimate, not a design. The water tank only generates a load alert for the engineer. DXF R12 in Windows-1252 (as the header declares), with floor plans, elevations, title block, furniture, and utilities in layers.
19. **Wood frame (F8):** `system.structure: 'wood'` uses `W*` profiles (C25 pressure-treated pine) with NBR 7190 (kmod 0.56; compression with the simplified buckling method, strong axis only, since boards brace the weak one). Defaults: 38×140 in walls, 38×235 in beams, 38×184 in rafters (38×89 failed at chalet ceiling height). Double top track, blocker at mid-height above 2.7 m, nails in kg. Structure labor uses the wood mass in the same R$/kg table (~R$ 250/m²). The 6.4 m mezzanine span fails in sawn lumber (needs an intermediate beam or glulam): correct result, not a bug.
20. **First steps and construction (F8):** 5 complete ready-made templates (rooms, finishes, furniture) in New project; 6-step tour (does not open with `navigator.webdriver`); single shortcut table (`?`). `project.progress` stores purchases and completed stages (does not change calculations); Obra (Construction) tab with a Gantt estimated from the roadmap's man-hours and a purchase deadline from the catalog lead times.
21. **Performance (F9):** thermal via the simplified procedure of NBR 15575 (U and CT of the typical section, no thermal bridge; capital's zone or chosen). LSF passes on U and fails on CT (≥ 130): becomes an informational notice asking for simulation. On-grid solar by the state's HSP (peak sun hours) and consumption per resident; the `pv_system` item has `laborIncluded` and stays out of the percentage labor. Rain via simplified NBR 15527 (plan area × rainfall × 0.8). Solar orientation/tilt factors are local heuristics.
22. **View project and quality (F10):** `/ver?id=` is read-only (does not mount autosave), with 3D in presentation mode, floor plan, numbers, and PDF, responsive for phones (orbit, no walk-through). E2E covers the flows of phases 5 to 9 (21 tests, ~3 min); the dev error "synchronously unmount a root" comes from drei's `<Html>` with React 19 and is ignored. Running e2e requires another directory if the `next dev` on port 3000 is up (Next 16 refuses two dev servers in the same folder).
23. **Assistant for beginners and tower (F11):** "Me ajude a montar" (Help me build) is the default tab in New project: 15 questions with "por que perguntamos" (why we ask) and a pure generator that builds the typology, floor plan (interior walls, doors, windows), rooms, finishes by use, furniture, system, and options; the matrix of 768 combinations passes without error. Suggested cost estimate cuts run the real generator and engine without the option. A bathroom on the mezzanine creates the water tank tower (`water_tower`), with geometry, verified platform, and quantities in the extras stage; `install.pressure` (NBR 5626, head ≥ 1 m at the shower) is a caution on an upper level and informational on the ground floor.
24. **Usability and regional cost (F12):** single glossary (54 terms) with automatic tooltip, alerts with "o que quer dizer / o que fazer" (what it means / what to do) and "Mostrar no 3D" (Show in 3D), search in properties (`/`), 4-sentence summary, and collapsible side panels (`[`, `]`, `\`). Labor is multiplied by the state's regional factor (CUB R1-N ÷ SP, `laborRegionalFactor`); materials keep the national price.
25. **Procedural realism (F12-W1):** PBR generated in code (color, Sobel normal, and roughness, seamless periodic noise) for finishes, roof panel, deck, concrete, and grass; vegetation in canvas sprites with instancing, cutout shadow, and impostors; Countryside/Mountains/Beach scenes with hills, fog, and a path to the door; physical glass with transmission on High; custom sky (`SkyDome`, drei's `Sky` came out washed out with ACES); threshold bloom and exposure by time of day. drei's `ContactShadows` left a rectangle with the EffectComposer: replaced by a decal. Nothing is downloaded; photographic textures (Poly Haven, ambientCG) depend on authorization to download.
26. **Assistant within the checks (F13-W1):** the generator sizes windows by area (1/6 and 1/12 with margin), checks the header and bracing of each new window, supports the mezzanine edge on a wall, puts the shed roof on the short span, reduces the mezzanine bedroom to the strip with usable ceiling height, and runs "Ajustar às normas" before the furniture. The result shows a badge ("Dentro das verificações automáticas" (Within the automatic checks) or "N pontos para revisar" (N points to review)). The ready-made templates go through the same conformance. Known pending items: sawn-lumber mezzanine beam (W38x235 does not clear ~4.6 m), bracing of the compact A-frame, and the gambrel loft beam (edge with no wall below). The steel/wood index became informational.
27. **Presentation for showing (F13-W2):** ready-made views with automatic framing (keys 1–5), views saved per project in `localStorage`, "Salvar 4 fotos" (Save 4 photos) in .zip (own zip writer, no dependency), and 360° video/sun timelapse in WebM via `MediaRecorder`.
28. **Photographic textures (F13-W3):** 16 CC0 materials from Poly Haven (1K: color, OpenGL normal, roughness; 26 MB in `public/textures`) replace the procedural image in the High and Editor qualities after loading (same `Texture` objects, without recreating the material). The average color and roughness of each photo is normalized to that of the finish: the photo brings the detail, the color remains the chosen one. Roof panel and grass remain procedural (they are tinted); the Light quality does not download photos.
29. **Walls under the roof and architect's floor plan (F14):** the engine cuts the outline of interior walls at the underside of the roof (clearance 0.05 m, minimum 0.3 m; warning `wall.roof_clip` for a door that does not fit). The assistant builds a single wet core: kitchen, laundry (niche at the end of the countertop, never a room or annex), and bathroom on the same plumbing wall, upper bathroom stacked over the lower one; stairs against a wall (in the A-frame, the core's 1.10 m half-wall in front of the mezzanine; in gable houses, the wet wall, because next to the eave there is no clear height); mezzanine always with a double bed (the A-frame widens to 7 m when the usable strip does not fit); no empty room. Terrain slope can be typed (% or °).
30. **Asynchronous presentation (F14-W2):** clicking "Apresentação" (Presentation) prepares textures, photos, landscape, and terrain in cancelable steps (with progress), without freezing the editor; "Abrir" (Open) enters when ready, and the same project signature reopens instantly. Sloped terrain shows the real hillside with trees and a path over the surface. drei's `<Html>` was replaced by `SafeHtml` (root unmounted after commit), which eliminated the unhandled `removeChild` when switching modes. The "Interior" view picks the photographer's point (back of the room, clear view to the glass, away from walls, stairs, and furniture).
31. **Architect-quality floor plan (F15-W1):** `layoutQuality.layoutProblems` is the oracle for generated floor plans and ready-made templates: sofa and TV facing each other (1.8–4.0 m, clear view), furniture inside the room, access to the bed, kitchen triangle, bathroom clearances, walls with a function (≥ 70% separating rooms; half-wall, low wall, and mezzanine edge recognized by geometry), 0.8 m circulation by BFS and, in the glazed A-frame, living room at the front. Furnishing is a search over candidates that accepts the best position that passes the metrics. The assistant matrix requires zero problems. Pending: the A-frame annex's wall triangle (an engine requirement) still intrudes ~1.1 m into the body.
32. **Manual drawing (F15-W2):** typed measurement (`3.5`, `3.5;90`, `3x4`), live dimensions, snap with priority and guides (endpoint, intersection, midpoint, perpendicular, aligned, 5 cm grid, 15° with Shift), Rectangle tool (R), close outline, drag endpoint/midpoint with connected walls following (one undo step), split and join walls. Measure moved to T. `SafeHtml` does not capture the mouse (interactive content turns on `pointer-events`).
33. **Engineering utilities (F16):** research in `docs/research/eletrica.md` and `hidraulica.md`. `installations.electrical` and `installations.plumbing` store the editable layout (`manual`); without editing, the engine generates it. Electrical (NBR 5410): loads from the minimums, circuits by the rules, conduits through the ceiling with a graph, FCA/FCT (grouping/temperature correction factors), Ib→In→cross-section→voltage drop (4% terminal, 5% total), PE (protective earth), RCD (DR) per wet room, SPD (DPS), distribution board, demand, and service entrance standard (generic: confirm with the utility (concessionária)), conduit fill, lux by the lumen method. Plumbing (NBR 5626 weights method, 8160, 7229): tank tree, inferred fittings, FWH (fixture weights), dynamic pressure per point with how much to raise the tank, sewage by UHC (hydraulic load units) with slope, siphon/grease/inspection boxes, venting (including dedicated venting for a box far from the vent stack), and septic tank. Floor plan: Electrical (Shift+E, NBR 5444 symbols, color per circuit) and Plumbing (H, colors per system, pressure badge) layers, palettes, editing with atomic undo, Elétrica and Hidráulica tabs (Electrical and Plumbing) in the results, lit light fixtures in 3D, PDF. Missing constants in the catalog are marked `LOCAL` and commented in the modules (list in the cards' reports).
34. **Legal approval and documentation for ART/RRT (F17):** research in `docs/research/aprovacao.md`. ART (Anotação de Responsabilidade Técnica) is the engineer's liability record; RRT is the architect's equivalent.
    - **Data:** `project.legal` stores the stage (NBR 16636), owner/client, address/matrícula (property registry number), lot and house position, zoning (typed by the user; nothing municipal in the catalog), soil/SPT, responsible professionals with ART/RRT numbers, revisions, and reviews per item.
    - **Engine:** `results.approval` brings the code compliance check by discipline, zoning indices, area schedule, ART/RRT form, list of drawing sheets (pranchas) by stage, and descriptive specifications (memoriais).
      - An item whose standard has `confirmed: false` in the catalog never comes out "ok" on its own: it becomes `manual` ("pendente de conferência profissional" (pending professional review)).
      - Setbacks are measured from the exterior face of the walls, without the eave (conservative).
      - The septic tank remains calculated by NBR 7229 (legacy) with a notice about NBR 17076:2024, whose formulas were not checked.
      - NBR 5444 is cancelled: own legend, never "conforme NBR 5444" (per NBR 5444).
    - **Drawing sheets:** SVG in paper mm, A-series sheet (NBR 10068) with a 16-field title block (carimbo), space for the city hall's approval, notes, and legend.
      - Architecture: site location, site plan, floor plans, sections, elevations, roof, and schedules.
      - Engineering: foundation, LSF panels, roof, notes, electrical plan, single-line diagram, load schedule, water, sewage, isometric, water tank, and septic tank.
      - Vector PDF with `jspdf` and an own SVG reader (no DOM); texts go through `clean` (WinAnsi).
    - **"Aprovação" (Approval) screen** (Shift+A): data, check with professional's review, form for SITAC/SICCAU, descriptive specifications and PDF forms (utility (concessionária), septic tank, alvará (building permit)), and .zip package.
    - **Lot on the floor plan:** key L.
